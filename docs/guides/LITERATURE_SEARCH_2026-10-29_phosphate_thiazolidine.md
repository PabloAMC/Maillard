# Literature search 2026-10-29: phosphate catalysis of the Amadori steps; thiazolidine K and ring kinetics vs pH

Agent search (web, abstracts and full texts where open), notes as saved during the search. Conclusion: no
direct measurement of phosphate catalysis of TTCA -> cysteine Amadori compound, and no K(pH) or ring-opening
k(pH) for a cysteine-sugar thiazolidine at 60-140 C. Quantitative anchors: Davidek 2002 (degradation, 90 C);
Munanairi 2007 (~10-12x per equivalent of phosphate, 37 C, A280, not Amadori-specific); model-thiazolidine ring
opening pH-independent above pH 3.5 with general-acid buffer catalysis (Canle & More O Ferrall 1996, 25 C); Zhai
2020 (TTCA -> ARP favoured at pH 7.5-9.5, spray drying). Consequence: phosphate catalysis of the formation step
enters stage 1b as a declared assumption with a band; the experiment brief asks the measurement.
Papers marked "not read" are leads only.

# Phosphate catalysis / thiazolidine pH literature search (2026-10-29)

## Bell 1997 Food Chem 59:143-147 — DOI 10.1016/S0308-8146(96)00257-9
- Glucose + glycine, phosphate vs citrate buffers, pH 7, 25 C (per search snippet; abstract seen only via secondary snippets, ScienceDirect 403).
- Glycine loss and browning rates increase with phosphate concentration; citrate: no glycine loss/browning. Bifunctional phosphate catalysis proposed. No numbers obtained. Bears on (a) qualitatively; T far below 60-140 C.
- Status: abstract-level (secondary).

## Bell, White, Chen 1998 J Food Sci 63:785-788 — DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2621.1998.tb17900.x
- Glassy low-moisture solids. Glucose loss rate fell with buffer conc.; browning up with phosphate; "phosphate appeared to catalyze later pathways". Not aqueous -> low relevance. Abstract via Crossref.

## Bell & Wetzel 1995 J Agric Food Chem 43:2608-2612 — aspartame degradation in phosphate/citrate 0.01-1.0 M, pH 3 & 7, 25 C. NOT Maillard. Irrelevant except as general phosphate buffer catalysis.

## Protein glycation (Watkins/Baynes 1987 JBC; Smith & Thornalley-type gamma-globulin KIE study) — phosphate catalyses Amadori rearrangement on proteins (concentration-dependent; arsenate mimics). To verify details below.

## Watkins, Neglia-Fisher, Dyer, Thorpe, Baynes 1987 J Biol Chem 262:7207-7212 (protein glycation, phosphate) — DOI likely 10.1016/S0021-9258(18)48225-0 (JBC Elsevier re-DOI; verify)
- RNase + glucose, phosphate vs other buffers: Schiff base extent same in all buffers, Amadori formation faster in phosphate -> phosphate catalyses Amadori rearrangement (concentration-dependent; arsenate mimics). Also lysozyme, cyt c, albumin, Hb. Abstract-level via secondary snippet. Bears on (a) mechanistically (protein, 37 C).

## Kallen 1971b JACS 93:6236-6248 — DOI 10.1021/ja00752a040 (verified via Crossref)
- "Mechanism of reactions involving Schiff base intermediates. Thiazolidine formation from L-cysteine and formaldehyde". Companion to 93:6227 (DOI 10.1021/ja00752a039). Abstract not retrievable (ACS 403). Content not read.

## Zhai et al. 2020 JAFC 68:10884-10892 — DOI 10.1021/acs.jafc.0c04287 (already-known Zhai group; noting new numbers)
- Xyl-Cys spray-drying; TTCA->ARP favoured pH 7.5-9.5; at pH 9.5 equilibrium ARP conversion 47.23% (59.48% of TTCA+ARP); 190 C inlet: ARP 20.83% vs 6.03% w/o spray drying. No rate constants. Abstract only.
## Zhai et al. 2021 JAFC 69:10648-10656 — DOI 10.1021/acs.jafc.1c03727 — TTCA degradation faster at higher T and pH; extra xylose traps released Cys. Qualitative abstract only.

## Esterbauer, Ertl, Scholz 1976 Tetrahedron 32:285-289 — DOI 10.1016/0040-4020(76)87015-9
- Cysteine + acrolein/crotonaldehyde/4-hydroxypentenal; thiazolidines from the Michael adducts; all adducts in equilibrium with Cys + aldehyde; forward/reverse k estimated; forward rate ~10x per pH unit over pH 2-10 (abstract via EPA HERO; unclear whether this is the Michael step or thiazolidine step — likely thiolate Michael addition). T not stated in abstract. Bears on (d) weakly.

## Hellstrom, Almqvist, Aamisepp 1969 J Chem Soc B 1103-1108 — DOI 10.1039/j29690001103
- Cysteine + 4-oxopentanoic acid (ketone) thiazolidine; Cys dissociation constants measured; equilibrium pH-dependent with maximum at pH 9 favouring product; forward rate first order in ketone and in a cysteine species. Snippet-level only (RSC 403). Bears on (d): shape of K(pH).

## Munanairi, O'Banion, Gamble, Breuer, Harris, Sandwick 2007 Carbohydr Res 342:2575-2592 — DOI 10.1016/j.carres.2007.08.003 (PMC2141680, FULL TEXT read via WebFetch summariser)
- 37 C, pH 7.4-8.0; sugar(-P) 0.050 M, amine 0.10 M; ribose/deoxyribose runs contain 0.050 M NaH2PO4 (so no Pi-free control in the tabulated kinetics).
- Table 1 (37 C): ribose+Pi+NAcLys pH 7.5: k = 0.021 M-1 h-1, k* = 11.8 M-1 h-1; R5P+NAcLys: k* 36 M-1 h-1 (pH 8.0), 180 (pH 7.5) [summariser numbers; verify in PDF].
- Intramolecular phosphate: R5P ~15x faster sugar loss than ribose+Pi; G3P ~6x faster than glyceraldehyde+Pi.
- Intermolecular Pi (data NOT shown): glycine + glyceraldehyde or ribose, +/- Pi, "catalytic effect of phosphate approximately 10-12 fold per 1 molar equivalent of soluble phosphate" measured as A280 increase (browning-precursor/UV, not Amadori per se). Conditions not stated in that passage (likely 37 C).
- Does NOT isolate Amadori-rearrangement catalysis. Cites Potman & van Wijk 1989 ACS Symp Ser 409 pp 182-195 [ref 4] and Rizzi 2004 JAFC 52:953-957 [ref 5] for phosphate catalysis.
- Follow-up: Gersten et al. 2010 Carbohydr Res 345:2499-2506 (ribose 18x slower than R5P; Ru5P much faster) — ACS PRF reports conclude acyclic fraction dominates, intramolecular phosphate catalysis "relatively small".

## Potman & van Wijk 1989 — VERIFIED citation: "Mechanistic Studies of the Maillard Reaction with Emphasis on Phosphate-Mediated Catalysis", ACS Symp Ser 409 (Thermal Generation of Aromas), ch. 17, pp 182-195. DOI 10.1021/bk-1989-0409.ch017. Abstract elided (S2 null; ACS 403). Content NOT read.

## Rizzi 2004 JAFC 52:953-957 — "Role of Phosphate and Carboxylate Ions in Maillard Browning" — DOI 10.1021/jf030691t (abstract via PubMed)
- Phosphate/carboxylate accelerate browning of reducing sugars ALONE (no amine) and with beta-alanine -> anions act on sugar decomposition directly (OPD trapping of reactive intermediates); contradicts pure "proton abstraction from Amadori" explanation; warns phosphate buffers complicate Maillard kinetics. No numbers in abstract. Bears on (a)/(c) as a confounder.

## Guan, Wang, Yu, Yu, Zhao 2012 J Dairy Sci 95:590-601 — DOI 10.3168/jds.2011-4860 (PMID 22281323; abstract only, full text 403)
- Glucose + proline in water vs several buffers (phosphate, citrate ...); reported elsewhere as 110 and 130 C (search snippet; unverified). Phosphate accelerated reversible breakdown of early products (proposed H2PO4-/HPO4 2- proton shuttle to N-glycosylamine) and accelerated degradation of fructosyl-proline (ARP) -> HMF + formic acid. Citrate lowered HMF. No numbers in abstract. Bears on (a)/(c): same-lab water vs phosphate at high T — candidate for numbers if full text obtained.

## Bermejo-Velasco, Nawale, Oommen, Hilborn, Varghese 2018 Chem Commun 54:12507-12510 — DOI 10.1039/C8CC05405C (FULL TEXT read, open access)
- L-Cys + 16-fold propionaldehyde in deuterated phosphate buffer, ~RT, 1H NMR: complete conversion to 2-ethyl-TCA within 5 min at pD 5, 7.4, 9; at pD 3 considerably slower (complete within ~30 min). Product stable (no degradation) for 7 days at pD 3, 5, 7.4, 9. Benzaldehyde pseudo-1st-order k1 = 0.0146 min-1, 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde 0.0034 min-1 (pD 7.4?). pD acidification during reaction at neutral pD (needed NaOD). Rapid syn/anti interconversion on pD change (NH protonation).
- Bears on (d): formation rate slows sharply below pH ~4 (thiol/amine protonation); equilibrium strongly to thiazolidine at pH 5-9 for aliphatic aldehydes at mM. Room T only; no K values; phosphate not varied.

## Kim, Yun, Park 1986 Bull Korean Chem Soc 7(1):25-27 (FULL TEXT read, koreascience PDF)
- Cys + cinnamaldehyde, 25 C, I=0.1: Michael addition then thiazolidine (second Cys). Table 1: k1 (M-1 s-1) pH 1-11.5; flat ~5.6e-3 at pH<=4, rises ~10x/pH unit from pH 5.5 to 8 (pH 7.0: 0.49; pH 7.5: 1.00; pH 9.0: 4.98), bell max ~pH 9-9.5, falls above. Buffers: HCl/KCl, acetate, KH2PO4+borax (pH 6-9), NaOH/NaCl. Rate follows thiolate fraction (Cys pK ~8.4, 10.2). This is the Michael step, not the carbonyl-thiazolidine step -> weak relevance for (d); shows the generic thiolate-controlled pH profile. Phosphate not varied.

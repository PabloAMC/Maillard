# Strategy, 2026-10-29: from many threads to one sequence

Owner, 2026-10-29: *"Step back and strategize … taking a step back and doing this well will pay off."* This page
orders the work opened on 2026-10-28/29. It does not replace `tasks/data_restructure_plan.md` §7 (the backlog
and its records); it says what comes first and why. Confidence in brackets.

## 1. What we are building, and for whom

**Purpose (owner, A8):** help design better alternative-protein flavours. Concretely, given a formulation (amino
acids incl. cysteine, sugars incl. ribose / xylose, thiamine, nucleotides, buffer salts such as phosphate, fat)
and a process (time-temperature, vessel, pH), predict the key odorants (2-methyl-3-furanthiol, 2-furfurylthiol,
H2S, Strecker aldehydes, pyrazines, lipid aldehydes) with honest, lab-independent uncertainty. The sulfur lane
comes first: cysteine + pentose + heat is the chemistry of meat-type reaction flavours, and it has the most data.

**What "good" means (the north star, made measurable):** (1) leave-one-laboratory-out error per lane, median fold
and its spread; (2) coverage of the 90 % interval on the same; (3) evidence grounding (share of each pot's flux
on measured, in-regime constants); (4) blind prospective tests (WUR, recorded before the data exist). Scores
diagnose; evidence decides.

## 2. What the last two days taught us (the diagnosis)

1. **The shipped sulfur lane is structurally wrong for anything slower than 20 min at 145 °C** [high]. Its main
   step (cysteine as an instant catalyst on pentose) has no measured basis. De Roos's measured route is the right
   structure, and with it the slow-cook shapes move the right way.
2. **The binding constraint is data per constant, not statistics** [high]. Intermediate species are measured in
   five laboratories; 61 dossiers hold undigitised figures; the evidence audits keep missing direct measurements.
3. **Big joint fits hide errors** [high]. Every refit on the 52 flavour rows moved constants the rows cannot see
   (B59a-r, B59b). The constants that behaved were the ones fitted on their own species (stage 1).
4. **Apparent contradictions are mostly missing conditions** [moderate-high]: pH, phosphate, heat-up, analyte
   definitions. Modelled as chemistry, they generalise; as lab terms, they would not.
5. **Our process leaks errors** [high]: hand-written fitting code (two bugs, plus a latent one in every fit),
   claims typed by hand, reviews after the compute, 109 uncommitted files.

## 3. The strategy: foundation, data, sub-models bottom-up, then one joint fit

**Phase 0 — consolidate (about a day).** Commit the finished work in reviewable pieces: (a) kind-1 engineering
(inline pH coupling + numba, heating fields on the bundles, the choices register and its gate, the fitting
module); (b) the corrections (de Roos's decay profile); (c) each wave's record as a closed record (B64: no
reading chosen; B59a-r: deferred; B59b: did not ship, redesign; stage 1: procedure passed, constants not
forwarded). Ship the inline coupling as the default (verified, 0.005 dex). Nothing scientific moves in Phase 0.
*Why first:* every later step needs a clean base, and 109 uncommitted files are a risk of loss and confusion.

**Phase 1 — data (days, mostly parallel agents).** (a) The measurement index (species and step → every dossier
passage that measures it), so audits are lookups. (b) Digitise the top-ranked figure-only data for the sulfur
lane (inventory running), each with its read error. (c) Harvest pH trajectories and organic-acid yields. (d)
Author requests for raw data where figures are too coarse; one designed experiment proposed (phosphate ± in one
lab, pH logged) through the experiment ranking. *Why before modelling:* each stage so far failed on a measurement
it had not seen.

*Phase 1 inputs found 2026-10-29* ([tasks/sulfur_data_inventory_2026-10-29.md](../../tasks/sulfur_data_inventory_2026-10-29.md),
an agent's scan of ~75 sulfur-lane dossiers): the top figure-only items are Zhai 2023 Food Chem Figs. 2-3
(deoxyosones, regenerated cysteine, dicarbonyls vs time from fed TTCA at three temperatures), Wang 2022 (MFT, FFT
and Cys-Amadori on a 5 × 6 temperature-time grid), Wang 2026b Fig. 4A, Desclaux 2006, Li 2011, Hofmann 2001 (SIDA
FFT loss), Hou 2017 and Zhou 2025b (pH vs time), Wang 2026 Fig. 2C (MFT and FFT vs pH 5-9), plus missing
supplements (Zhang 2024 S1-S3, Zhang 2026 S6-S8, Wang 2026b S1, Zhao 2019 S1). Three integrity items come first:
(1) the only sulfur temperature ladder (Kang 2026 Table S4) is claimed by two Zhai 2023 papers, one on a different
quantification basis, so it may be one data set counted two or three times; (2) the shipped Zhang 2025b benchmark
sums volatiles over two GC columns (possible double count); (3) no sulfur time series is ingested in
`data/lit/timeseries/` yet. The inventory is an agent's reading of the dossiers: each item is verified against its
dossier before use.

**Phase 2 — sub-models calibrated on their own species, bottom-up (each small, cheap, reviewed twice).** In
order of the chemistry's flow:
1. **pH and acid production** (Davidek 2002, Brands 2001/2002c, the pH trajectories; the ionisable centres).
   Everything pH-dependent downstream inherits it, so it comes first.
2. **The cysteine-pentose route** (stage 1b: K and ring kinetics with pH laws, phosphate catalysis of the
   Amadori steps, de Roos corrected to the pot's buffer; Cao, Zhai 2019/2021, Kang, Zhang).
3. **The deoxyosones** (Zhai 2019 Fig. 3B, Zhai 2021 Fig. 5; the cysteine Strecker step).
4. **Thiol formation and loss** (Schieberle's 100 °C series, Kumazawa's survival, the fed-precursor pots).
Each sub-model: a no-refit probe first; a design review before any fit; fitting through
`src/kinetic_core/fitting.py` (two starts, identification, leave-one-laboratory-out with the predictive check);
a results review; numbers printed from the run, not typed. A sub-model whose own data it cannot reproduce stops
the sequence and sends us to the literature, not to the next stage.

**Phase 3 — one joint fit, then ship.** All simulable aqueous data from every laboratory, through the measurement
model (a scale per series shrunk to its method class, integrated out; a discrepancy error per observable class),
latent conditions where unmeasured (heat-up per vessel type, replacing B64's five global readings), starting from
the Phase-2 constants. Accuracy by leave-one-laboratory-out; the posterior by importance sampling from the
Laplace fit; then switches on, artifacts regenerated once, the certifying suite, and the WUR blind score when
its data arrive. B59a-r (the Strecker sulfur) is tested here as an arm, not before.

**Phase 4 — the other lanes the purpose needs.** Precursor supply (ribose-5-phosphate and IMP, the meat
precursors; thiamine), Strecker aldehydes and pyrazines, lipid oxidation and its interaction with the thiols, then
matrix effects (protein binding, headspace) for real products. Same recipe: data, sub-models, joint fit.

## 4. What we stop or defer

The 15-run refit grids on the flavour rows; B64's discrete heating readings (they become a latent condition in
Phase 3); B59a-r alone; B61-B63 and the lipid dual-law question until Phase 4 (recorded, not dropped);
regenerating the envelope and scorecards before Phase 3 (each regeneration of a structurally wrong model is
spent effort).

## 5. Standing practices (from the two days' lessons)

Cheap probe before any expensive run; two reviews per wave (design, results); the shared fitting module for every
fit; the measurement index for every audit; contradictions handled as missing conditions first; rules questioned
on their merits; agents save as they go; commit at each phase gate; compute niced within 8 of 10 cores.

## 6. Odds and the main risks

- Phase 2 produces sub-models that each reproduce their own species across laboratories within ×2: ~65 %.
- Phase 3's joint fit keeps the 145 °C pots within ×3 while getting the 100-110 °C shapes right: ~45 % (it was
  ~35 % before the sub-model route; the bottom-up order removes most of the compensation channels).
- Main risks: the corpus lacks the measurement that separates two explanations (then a designed experiment is the
  only way, and we say so); the network misses a high-temperature mechanism (Phase 3 residuals will show it);
  process drift back into large grids (the practices above are the guard).
- Time: Phase 0 about a day; Phase 1 days, mostly agents and the owner's downloads; Phase 2 roughly one sub-model
  per one or two sessions; Phase 3 a few sessions including compute.

## 7. Decisions taken (owner, 2026-10-29: "I trust you to take the best decisions … plan and prioritize")

1. **Sulfur lane first, through Phase 3; lipid off-flavour second.** It has the most data and the clearest
   mechanism, and the shared tooling it forces (fitting module, measurement index, measurement model) carries over.
   Agents may gather lipid-lane data in parallel, but only one lane is modelled at a time.
2. **Relative accuracy is the primary target, absolute levels secondary.** Most design questions are comparisons
   (which recipe, does phosphate help, how long to hold); within-study contrasts and dose-responses cancel
   calibration and are the cleanest data. Headline scores: leave-one-laboratory-out error on within-study
   contrasts (direction and fold), then on levels.
3. **Bottom-up sub-models, final constants only from the joint fit.** Sub-models establish structure, starting
   values and mechanism errors; they are not the shipped constants.
4. **Data just in time, except the foundations.** Each sub-model gathers and digitises its own data before its
   design review. First, for everyone: the three integrity checks, the measurement index, and the pH and
   acid-production data.
5. **New measurements are proposed, not depended on.** A one-page experiment brief (one lab, cysteine + xylose ±
   phosphate, pH logged, intermediates and thiols over time at two temperatures) is written for the owner to send
   if and when a collaborator is available. Until then the strategy names the questions that stay open.
6. **Phase 0 moves nothing scientific**: the inline pH coupling becomes the default at Phase 3's single
   regeneration, not now. Sub-model pre-registrations are kept short (about a page). The owner's decisions and
   downloads are batched, at most once per phase.

**Execution order from here:** Phase 0 (tests and gates green, local commits in groups, no push) → the three
integrity checks → the measurement index → sub-model 1, pH and acid production (data: Davidek 2002 NaOH uptake
and acid yields, Brands 2001/2002c, Cao's and Kang's and Zhou's pH data; digitise Hou 2017 and Zhou 2025b pH) →
sub-model 2, the route (stage 1b, data ready) → sub-model 3, the deoxyosones → sub-model 4, thiol formation and
loss → Phase 3.

# Cross-domain literature search, 2026-10-31: kinetics from adjacent fields

The search was read-only: no repo edits, no downloads. I checked every candidate against all earlier records: the
`docs/guides/LITERATURE_SEARCH_2026-10*.md` files, MECHANISM_REVIEWS, EVIDENCE_MAP and the private download queue (538 DOIs plus
author and keyword greps), and against `data/articles/` and `data/lit/extraction_dossiers/`. None of the papers listed
below appears in those records. While checking, I dropped candidates that turned out to be covered already: Hofmann 1996
(thiol oxidative stability), Guth 1995, Kreitman 2016/2017, Kachur 1999, Lienhard & Jencks 1966, Esterbauer 1976,
Bermejo-Velasco 2018, Canle 1996, Kjellstrand 2001, Mittelmaier 2011, Huang/Tippmann/Becker 2017, Chan & Reineccius 1994,
Monforte 2021, Nikolantonaki 2012 (jf204295s) and Thornalley 1999.

**DOI rule.** "verified" means I resolved the DOI in Crossref myself and the title matched (DataCite where stated).
"lead" means I did not verify it. **Basis** is what my confidence rests on: the abstract (PubMed, Semantic Scholar or
publisher), the full text, or the title or a search snippet only.

**Ranking.** Each candidate's score is gap weight × confidence. Gaps 1, 2 and 4 weigh 3; gaps 3, 5 and 6 weigh 2; gap 7
weighs 1. Confidence counts high 3, medium 2, low 1.

**Caveat that applies to all of these.** These are class or analogue constants, not measurements on the Maillard
species themselves. 5-ALA stands in for aminoacetone (both are alpha-aminoketones). GSH and Cys stand in for MFT/FFT.
Glyoxal and methylglyoxal with ammonium stand in for the pot. Most values were measured at 25-50 C; only Elfsson
(37-85 C), Luo (a temperature series) and Sedehi (a temperature series) give barriers. Any use needs a stated transfer
assumption with a band.

## Gaps that came back EMPTY or nearly so

- **Gap 4 (thiazolidine kinetics vs T, pH, buffer): EMPTY.** No multi-temperature or Ea data for Cys + aldehyde or
  sugar thiazolidine formation or ring opening turned up in the prodrug, N-terminal-Cys ligation, PLP-Cys or
  parenteral-nutrition literature. Everything that did surface (Bermejo-Velasco, Canle / More O'Ferrall, Esterbauer
  1976, Roberts / Nagasawa, Pesek & Frost, Kallen) is already in the records. Only room-temperature PLP-Cys and
  cinnamaldehyde studies exist, at the lead level.
- **Gap 6, Strecker side (lipid carbonyl as Strecker donor, non-Seville lab): EMPTY.** Every lipid-Strecker hit was
  Zamora / Hidalgo. Only the **thiol-trap side** is filled (items 5, 10 and 19, from toxicology).
- **Gap 3 (Strecker step measured in water with a dicarbonyl donor): essentially EMPTY.** The adjacent fields gave
  only analogues: the ninhydrin kinetics (item 25), the glyoxal + amino acid imidazole channel (item 12) and the Reading
  malt fit (item 24). No measured dicarbonyl + amino acid → aldehyde rate constant in water was found.
- **Gap 1, MFT specifically: EMPTY.** There is no heated-water MFT lifetime anywhere. The fill is furanthiol (FFT) +
  quinone (items 4 and 18) plus thiolate class constants (items 6, 7, 9).
- **Gap 2, aminoacetone itself:** there is no measured aminoacetone dimerisation constant. The closest alpha-aminoketone
  analogue is 5-ALA (items 1, 8, 26-28), which does include dihydropyrazine → pyrazine oxidation and its O2 dependence
  (Bunke), but probably not as a clean constant from the abstracts.
- **Gap 5, 3-DG lifetime with phosphate or amine catalysis at 37 C with Ea:** no clean constant. What exists is amine-free
  3-DG ⇌ 3,4-DGE ⇌ 3-DGal time courses (items 20-23).

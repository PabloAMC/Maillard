## 4. Tests outside the conflict

- **The third print decides which paper is the outlier.** Zhou 2023c (pH 7.5) gives MFT 1150 and FFT 511 µg/L, within ×1.4-1.5 of Zhou 2023 (pH 7; Zhou 2023's pH 8 column is lower, 526 µg/L MFT, so a pH 7.5 value of ~1000-1200 is expected). Against 2023c, Zhou 2022 is ×5.7 low on MFT and ×2.7 low on FFT. **Two prints agree; Zhou 2022 is the outlier.**
- MFT/FFT: 2022 1.08; 2023 2.10; 2023c 2.25. Zhou 2022 is the outlier on the ratio as well, so it is not a common scale factor on both thiols (E1 would have to hit the two thiol curves differently; E2, a chemical loss faster for MFT, fits).
- Furfural (FFT's precursor) agrees between 2022 and 2023 (×1.09), so FFT's precursor supply was the same: the FFT difference arises after furfural, in H2S supply, the thiol step, or the measurement of the thiol (E1, E2), not in the pot's sugar chemistry (against E3).
- Zhou 2022's own time course rises monotonically (63 → 203 → 263 → 345 µg/L MFT, 30-120 min), so its 60-min value is not a single bad vial: the whole 2022 series sits low.
- Tables S1 (both papers) would settle E1 directly; neither is on disk.

**No-refit probe.** The shipped engine gives MFT 170, FFT 131 µg/L for the pH 7 pot (closer to 2022). This is not
independent: zhou_pH7_MFT / _FFT are fit rows and B60 compromises among conflicting rows. The model cannot decide a
calibration question.

**Experiment.** The designed protocol (SIDA for MFT and FFT, standards added after cooling) would give an absolute
level for a free-sugar pot, not for the AX-ARP pot; it does not cover this conflict. The cheap settling step is
documentary: request both Tables S1 and the sample-storage times from the Jiangnan group.

## 5. Verdict

**Likely explanation (a measurement or handling difference in Zhou 2022), supported by a third print; the exact cause
is unresolved.** Zhou 2023 and 2023c agree within ×1.5 with the same method; Zhou 2022's thiols are low by ×3-6
while its non-thiol compounds agree.

| explanation | P |
|---|---|
| Zhou 2022 low by a thiol-specific measurement or handling loss (E1 or E2) | **0.65** |
| an unstated pot variable in 2022 (E4) | 0.15 |
| Zhou 2023 / 2023c high together (shared calibration error) | 0.15 |
| other | 0.05 |

**Single most informative test:** the two papers' Table S1 calibration curves (documentary). For the fit: use Zhou
2023 and 2023c as the laboratory's level (they are two prints, counted as two only if their pots are distinct runs),
and Zhou 2022 as a normalised time-course shape; or, if all three are kept as levels, give the laboratory a
between-paper scale term with a prior width of at least 0.8 dex (the observed ×6).

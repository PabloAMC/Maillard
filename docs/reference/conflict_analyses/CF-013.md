## 4. Tests outside the conflict

**Within-laboratory temperature contrasts (the decisive outside evidence for E1):**
- Wang 2022 itself: **the same pot rises at 100-110 °C and peaks at 120-140 °C** (text). One lab, one vessel, one lipid charge: only temperature varies.
- Zhai 2023 JAFC: at 120 °C both thiols still rise at 180 min (MFT 0.43 → 2.48; FFT 1.13 → 4.28); at 140 °C FFT peaks at 80 min. Zhai 2023 *Food Chem* Fig. 1a (100 °C, 20-min grid): MFT rises smoothly to 120 min and is flat to 140 min (1.237 → 1.179), FFT 3.73 → 3.03. So **within each "falling" laboratory, the lower temperature rises**. No source on disk shows a fall at ≤ 110 °C in a free-sugar pot.
- Every "rising" pot is at ≤ 110 °C; every clear fall is at ≥ 120 °C (Wang) or 140 °C (Zhai). The sources span the temperature axis without overlapping on it.

**The patent's standard timing (E4) bounds the thiol sink**: its [2H3]-MFT survived 4 h at 110 °C well enough to be quantified, and the reported 4-h level (1855 ng/g) is of the same order as the post-heating SIDA levels of Schieberle's 100 °C pot (1560 ng/g at 6 h) and US 8,920,862 (1560 ng/g at 95 °C, 6 h). The correction factor e^(λ·240 min) is therefore not huge: λ_MFT(110 °C) of order 0.005-0.02 /min, consistent with van Seeventer 2001 (MFT −59 % per day at 50 °C, extrapolated with Ea 40-60 kJ/mol).

**No-refit probe (shipped engine):**
- Schieberle 2000 pot at 100 °C: MFT 1.3 / 3.1 / 3.5 / 1.9 µg at 30 / 60 / 360 / 720 min (measured 4.5 / 13.8 / 156 / 179): the engine plateaus by 1 h and then falls. FFT 1.8 / 11 / 228 / 224 µg (measured 2.0 / 3.1 / 110 / 132): FFT's shape is roughly right.
- US 10,076,126 pot (0.2 M phosphate assumed): native MFT plateaus by 2 h (0.0027 / 0.0050 / 0.0065 / 0.0062 mM); the engine's per-molecule MFT loss averages **0.095 /min (half-life 7 min)** at 110 °C. With that loss, a [2H3]-MFT standard charged at t = 0 would be at 1e-10 of its charge by 4 h and the patent could have reported nothing. **The engine's MFT sink at 110 °C is ≥ 5× too fast**, independently of the shape.
- So the conflict is not between the sources; it is between the sources and the engine's sinks (B16's diagnosis), now with an outside bound.

**Model test still to run:** the Zhai 2023 JAFC four-time grid and Wang's text claim with the engine at 100 / 120 / 140 °C: E1 + E2 predict the engine (with a corrected sink) peaks earlier at 140 °C than at 120 °C, and Zhai's TTCA-fed pots peak before a free-sugar pot at equal temperature.

**Experiment.** The designed protocol core (100 / 120 / 140 °C, 0-160 min, SIDA for MFT and FFT, free cysteine, pentose, norfuraneol, furfural, H2S) **covers E1 and E2 directly** (one lab, one free-sugar pot, three temperatures). It should add the patent's control: one vial set per temperature with [2H3]-MFT / [2H2]-FFT charged before heating (and the usual after-cooling standards in sister vials), which measures the thiol sink in situ. Wang's lipid (E3) is outside the protocol and need not be added: enter Wang only with its lipid charge.

## 5. Verdict

**Explained (as a source conflict).** The rising and falling sources occupy different temperatures and
precursor regimes, and within both "falling" laboratories the lower temperature rises (the outside test). The
falls are amplified by Wang's lipid charge and may be partly a run offset in Zhai's 180-min column.

| explanation | P (main cause of the rising-vs-falling difference) |
|---|---|
| E1 temperature, with E2 precursor supply | **0.75** |
| E3 lipid (Wang only) | 0.10 |
| E4 measurement artefacts (patent IS timing, Zhai run offset) | 0.10 |
| other | 0.05 |

What remains is the engine: its MFT sink is too fast at 100-110 °C by ≥ 5× (patent IS survival) and its MFT
plateaus by 1 h at 100 °C against a measured rise to 6-12 h. **Single most informative test:** the protocol core
at 100 °C to 160 min with a before-heating isotope standard in sister vials (measures formation and loss
separately).

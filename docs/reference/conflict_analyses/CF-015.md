## 3. Candidate explanations (for the ×7 in MFT/FFT)

| # | explanation | direction | size | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Sugar excess and concentration favour the furfural → FFT branch** in US 8,920,862 (xylose 1.45 mol/kg, 3:1, ~30 % solids) | lower MFT/FFT in 8,920,862 ✓ | ×2-4 | FFT comes from furfural (pentose dehydration, first order in sugar: 6× more xylose here); MFT is cysteine/H2S-limited (Cys only 2× higher). Lower water activity favours FFT strongly: Hofmann's dry-heated ribose pot raises FFT ×8 and MFT ×1.3 (T2). The papers at 3:1 dilute (1.4-1.6) sit between the patents, each patent displaced toward its own ratio. |
| E2 | **Standard charged before heating in US 10,076,126** inflates MFT more than FFT | higher MFT/FFT in 10,076,126 ✓ | e^((λ_MFT − λ_FFT)·240 min): ×2 at λ_MFT 0.005 /min, ×5 at 0.01 (λ_FFT ≈ λ_MFT/2.7, van Seeventer 2001's 50 °C loss ratio) | sec. 1. Bounded above: the patent's 4-h MFT (1855 ng/g) is the same order as post-heating SIDA levels elsewhere (US 8,920,862 1560 ng/g; Schieberle 6 h 1560 ng/g), so the inflation is not orders of magnitude. |
| E3 | Temperature and time (110 °C 4 h vs 95 °C 6 h) | longer hold lowers MFT/FFT (MFT less stable) ✓; lower T lowers furfural formation (high barrier) ✗ | ×1-1.5 net | the two effects partly cancel |
| E4 | Patent analytics (single runs, no error, coarse FFT axis) | either | ×1.5 | FFT 520 ± 90 at 4 h, 150 ± 90 at 2 h |

**The time shape** (US 10,076,126 lag then acceleration vs the engine's front-loaded curve): Schieberle 2000's
own early points are also a slow onset (MFT at 0.5 / 1 h = 2.9 % / 8.8 % of its 6-h value; the patent's 0.5 / 1 h =
0.8 % / 6.7 % of its 4-h value). The two sources are compatible; the register's "Schieberle rises without the
patent's lag" is not supported by Schieberle's 30- and 60-min numbers. FFT in the patent also lags (< 90 ng/g to 1 h),
and FFT is little inflated by E2, so the lag is mostly native chemistry (precursor build-up). E2 steepens the MFT
curve further (inflation grows with t). **The conflict on shape is with the engine (0.42 / 0.80 / 1.05 / 1), not
between sources.**

## 4. Tests outside the conflict

- **Absolute levels, not ratios:** MFT agrees across three sources within ×1.2 (1855, 1560, 1560-1790 ng/g); **FFT
  does not**: 520 (US 10,076,126) / 1100-1320 (Schieberle 6-12 h) / 3140 ng/g (US 8,920,862). The ×7 in the ratio is
  mostly an FFT difference, and FFT is highest where xylose is highest. That is E1's prediction; E2 predicts the
  difference in MFT instead. Per mol of cysteine, though, the MFT yields differ (69 / 28 / 415 µmol/mol), so the level
  agreement is partly coincidence: moderate weight.
- **Hofmann 1998 T2, dry vs aqueous** (same lab, same precursors): MFT/FFT 0.26 dry vs 1.64 aqueous: lowering water
  activity moves the ratio by ×6 in E1's direction (confounded with 180 °C / 6 min vs 145 °C / 20 min, so an upper
  bound on the water-activity share).
- **US 8,920,862's own structured-lipid arm**: MFT/FFT 0.68 at the same xylose excess, with standards after heating:
  the low ratio travels with the recipe, not with the matrix.
- **No-refit probe.** Shipped engine, verified conditions: US 10,076,126 (0.2 M phosphate assumed) native MFT/FFT at
  4 h 0.016; US 8,920,862 0.008. The engine moves the ratio ×2 in the measured direction from the conditions alone
  (E1 + E3), against ×7 measured; both absolute ratios are 200× too low because the engine's FFT is 30-90× too high in
  these concentrated pots (MFT 4.3 vs 9.4 µmol/mol xylose; FFT 526 vs 18.9) and its MFT sink is too fast (CF-013).
  The engine cannot yet weigh E1 against E2.
- **E2 directly:** the patent's own Example 1 could be repeated with standards charged after heating in sister vials;
  no source on disk did both.

**Experiment.** The designed protocol core is 1:1 at 30 mM (no ratio or concentration arm) and uses standards after
cooling. To cover this conflict add (a) one 3:1 xylose : cysteine cell at the centre point, and (b) the
before-heating standard in sister vials (also proposed under CF-013). Neither is in the draft.

## 5. Verdict

**Likely explanation, pending a check of the patents' FFT basis; partly unresolved.** The ×7 is two displacements in
opposite directions from the papers' 1.4-1.6: US 8,920,862 low because its concentrated 3:1 recipe favours FFT (E1),
US 10,076,126 high partly because its isotope standards went through the heating (E2).

| explanation (main share of the ×7) | P |
|---|---|
| E1 sugar excess / concentration / water activity | **0.40** |
| E2 standard timing in US 10,076,126 | 0.25 |
| E3 temperature and time | 0.10 |
| E4 patent analytics | 0.25 |

The time-shape part of the entry is **explained**: the patent's lag is compatible with Schieberle 2000's onset and is
partly an E2 artefact; the misfit is the engine's front-loading. **Single most informative test:** a sister-vial
contrast of standards before vs after heating at 110 °C (it measures E2 and the in-situ thiol sink at once).
Keep the plan's rule: report the patents' MFT/FFT, do not fit it; if the US 10,076,126 shape is scored, score it with
the standard-survival correction declared.

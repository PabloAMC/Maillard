*Root-cause analysis, 2026-10-29, misfit protocol (`docs/guides/OPERATING_PROCEDURE.md` sec. 4). Register entry

## 1. Integrity

| check | finding |
|---|---|

| **Hofmann's furfural method — not SIDA** | The register says "SIDA for furfural in Hofmann 1998". The paper: "Quantitation of Furan-2-aldehyde. After cooling, certain amounts of 5-methylfurfural were added to the reaction mixtures as the internal standard. After extraction of the analyte and the standard with diethyl ether (four times, total volume 50 mL) ... An MS response factor of 0.95 was calculated ... in the chemical ionization mode." **A homologue internal standard, solvent extraction**, not an isotopologue. So 52 → 5.2 µg per pot. |
| Which aqueous numbers are new in Schieberle 1998 | Its aqueous thiols and thiazine (FFT 121, MFT 198, 5-acetyl-2,3-dihydro-1,4-thiazine 424, 3-mercapto-2-pentanone 599, 2-acetyl-2-thiazoline 7) are exactly Schieberle 1996 Table 3 (µg/L of the same pot). ## 3. Candidate explanations

| # | explanation | direction | size | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Purge-and-trap under-recovery of furfural** in the chapter, quantified against a standard that does not share furfural's volatility | Schieberle low ✓ | ×10-100 | Furfural is very water-soluble (Henry constant ~4 × 10⁻⁶ atm m³/mol, Kaw ~1.6 × 10⁻⁴ at 25 °C); a 20-min helium purge at room temperature (flow not stated; ~1 L of gas at a typical 50 mL/min) strips of order 1 % of it from 15 mL, against most of the far more volatile thiols. Only a labelled furfural would cancel that. |
| E2 | Typo / unit slip in one print | either | ×10 (×1.3 residual) | Hofmann's other T5 rows (glucose 6.9, rhamnose 6.2) and Schieberle's dry 79000 give no internal check. |
| E3 | Homologue-standard bias in Hofmann (5-methylfurfural formed in the pot, or extracted differently) | Hofmann high if 5-MF were lost, low if formed ✗ | ×1-2 | 5-methylfurfural is not a ribose product (Schieberle 1998 T VI: < 1 µg per pot even from rhamnose, aqueous); its extraction behaviour is close to furfural's. Small. |
| E4 | Different runs (run-to-run furfural variation) | either | ×1.5-2 | furfural is a minor, fast-turning-over intermediate; a ×13 between runs of one recipe is implausible for a lab that reproduces its thiols to 10 %. |

## 4. Tests outside the conflict

- **Other laboratories, buffered pentose + cysteine pots at 140-145 °C, pH 5 (ratios to MFT, method-matched within each
  paper):** Meynier 1995 T3 (140 °C, 1 h, pyrophosphate, SDE): furfural / MFT = 1.5 / 6.3 = **0.24**; Cerny 2007 T2
  (145 °C, 20 min, phosphate, SPME TIC areas, with thiamin): 158 / 336 = **0.47**. Hofmann's furfural / MFT is
  67.5 / 19.8 = **3.4**; Schieberle's 5.2 / 19.8 = **0.26**. Both outside sources sit near Schieberle's value, ×7-14
  below Hofmann's. Caveats: both are semi-quantitative (SDE and SPME both under-recover MFT, which would raise their
  ratios, so the true ratios are if anything lower), and the pots differ in ratio and time.
- **The FFT mass balance does not discriminate:** with Hofmann's own furfural + H2S rate (T3: 1 mmol each in 50 mL,
  145 °C, 20 min → 551 µg FFT), a standing furfural of 67.5 µg per pot needs ~0.6-1.3 M H2S over 20 min to make the pot's
  12.1 µg FFT, and 5.2 µg needs ~8-16 M (cysteine is 33 mM; H2S lost to the T3 autoclave headspace makes the true rate
  higher, by up to ~10×, which still leaves both prints short). Neither print can feed FFT through free furfural + H2S at the measured rate (an observation
  for the FFT branch, B62, not for this conflict).
- **No-refit probe:** the shipped engine puts 138 µg furfural in the Hofmann pH 5 pot (Hofmann ×2.0, Schieberle ×27);
  hofmann_ribose_FUR_insitu is a fit row, so this is not independent. What the probe shows is the stake: the engine
  makes 100 % of FFT in this pot through FUR + H2S, so the choice between the prints moves the unpinned `k_fur_fft_hs`
  by ×13.

**Experiment.** The designed protocol core measures furfural by HPLC-DAD at 140 °C, pH 5, 0.2 M phosphate, ribose 30 +
Cys 30 mM, 20 min — close to but not the Hofmann recipe. The settling measurement is cheap and should be added as one
cell: **Hofmann's exact pot (ribose 100 + Cys 33 mM, 0.5 M phosphate pH 5, 145 °C, 20 min) with furfural by HPLC-DAD
or [²H4]-furfural SIDA after cooling** (three vials). Fed arm A (furfural + Na2S) separately fixes the FFT step.

## 5. Verdict

**Unresolved, with a likely explanation.** The two prints are separate measurements (Schieberle's aqueous furfural is
not a reprint), by different isolation methods. Two outside laboratories' furfural/MFT ratios sit with Schieberle's
value.

| explanation | P |
|---|---|
| E1 Schieberle 1998 under-recovered (purge-and-trap without a furfural isotopologue) | 0.35 |
| E2 a ×10 slip in one print | 0.25 |
| Hofmann 1998 high (E3, or an unrecognised extraction artefact) | 0.25 |
| E4 run-to-run | 0.15 |

Note the tension: method plausibility favours Hofmann (a homologue standard with extraction is sound for furfural),
the outside ratios favour Schieberle. Until the three-vial check is done, carry the row as the two-valued choice the
register proposes, not as Hofmann's value at sigma 0.5; the geometric mean (18.7 µg) with sigma ≥ 0.6 dex covers
both. **Single most informative test:** the three-vial Hofmann-pot furfural measurement.

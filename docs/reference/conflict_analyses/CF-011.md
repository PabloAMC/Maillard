## 4. Tests outside the conflict

**Outside data.**
- **Zhang 2025b (Xie, phosphate, pH 6.5, 120 °C)** is the sharpest: E1 predicts its Cys-Amadori under-predicted like Cao's; the stage-1 check found −0.9 to −1.1 dex, larger than Cao's. But part of that is temperature: the joint fit's formation barrier (42.5 kJ/mol, 0.3 sigma above the 40 floor) is pinned by Zhai 2019's water ladder; with the measured glucose-system analogue (100.9 kJ/mol, Zhang 2026) re-anchored at Cao's 90 °C, the 90 → 120 °C extrapolation is ×4.3 larger (0.64 dex). E1 (×2-3, 0.3-0.5 dex) plus a measured-analogue barrier closes Zhang's gap; E3 does not reach Zhang (40 mM, lower amine than Zhai). So Zhang separates E1+barrier from E3.
- **Within-lab medium contrast**: no laboratory on disk ran the route in both water and phosphate. Davidek 2002 (DFG, water vs 0.1 M phosphate, pH-stat) is the only same-lab buffer contrast; it is on a different Amadori compound and on degradation, not formation.
- **Kang 2026 SI S3/S4 and Zhai 2021 Fig. 4b** (Jiangnan, water, TTCA-fed): constrain the ring opening and TTCA loss vs pH inside one lab; they do not see phosphate.
- **Zhai 2019's own pool**: TTCA + Cys-Amadori ≤ 82.6 mM with free Cys unmeasured; a low-reading ELSD ARP (E4) would not show in the TTCA plateau. No internal check.

**Model probes (no refit).**
1. Run Cao, Zhai 2019 and Zhang 2025b at their verified conditions with the leave-Zhai-out constants (k_form 10^-2.648) and the formation barrier fixed at 100.9 kJ/mol re-anchored at 90 °C. E1 predicts: Cao and Zhang fit, Zhai 2019's Cys-Amadori over-predicted by ~×2-3 at every temperature (a constant offset across 70-130 °C). A temperature-dependent residual on Zhai instead points to the barrier, not the buffer.
2. Same constants, Zhai 2019 with a prescribed pH falling from 7.4 to 5 within 20 min (the TTCA-formation acidification, from the inline pH model at Zhai's charge). If K(pH) carries Kallen's shape, the K tension should vanish without moving k_form; if it does not vanish, the K push is model structure (`k_ttca_deg`, ring opening), not data.

**Experiment.** Designed protocol **buffer arm C** (`DESIGNED_TIMECOURSE_PROTOCOL_DRAFT.md`): core recipe at 120 °C, pH 6 and 7.4, in unbuffered water (pH logged), 0.05 M and 0.2 M phosphate, and a Good's buffer at equal pH, TTCA and Cys-Amadori first. **It covers this conflict exactly** (one lab, one method, medium varied at fixed pH; the phosphate dose gives the catalytic order). It also measures the unbuffered pH trajectory no paper on disk reports.

## 5. Verdict

**Likely explanation, pending the probe.** Most likely root cause: **dihydrogen-phosphate catalysis of the Amadori step (E1), with Zhai 2019's water ladder pinning the formation barrier low** so that the joint fit transfers the medium difference into a temperature law. Probabilities (the residual gap, not only the ×1.87):

| explanation | P(main cause) |
|---|---|
| E1 phosphate catalysis (with the barrier confound) | **0.45** |
| E4 Zhai 2019 ELSD quantification of the low ARP | 0.20 |
| E3 amine / concentration | 0.10 |
| E2 pH on k_form | 0.10 |
| something else (laboratory, vessel, heat-up) | 0.15 |

The K tension is a separate question; pH (E2) is its most likely cause (0.5), with model structure (0.35) second.
**Single most informative test:** buffer arm C. **Cheapest:** probe 1 on Zhang 2025b with the measured-analogue barrier.
**Integrity fix needed in the register:** Zhai 2019 has no measured pH trajectory (sec. 1).

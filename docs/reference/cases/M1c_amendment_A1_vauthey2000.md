# M1c case, amendment A1: Vauthey 2000 declared before its data are read

*Written 2026-11-05, after `vauthey2000.pdf` was downloaded and BEFORE its data were extracted or read. The only
content seen is the first page (title, authors, affiliation, keywords) and the download-queue description. No
engine run has been made on any Vauthey pot. The frozen case (`M1c_thiol_supply_structure.md`, bd641ae8) is
unchanged; this amendment adds one candidate source and fixes, in advance, what it must contain to count.*

**Source.** Vauthey, Milo, Frossard, Garti, Leser & Watzke 2000, "Structured fluids as microreactors for flavor
formation by the Maillard reaction", J. Agric. Food Chem. 48:4808-4816, 10.1021/jf991254a (Nestle Research Centre).
Queue description: cysteine + ribose in structured fluids (microemulsion, cubic phase) WITH an aqueous control;
MFT and / or FFT quantified; a laboratory outside the DFA network.

**Only the aqueous control can bear on M1c.** The structured-fluid arms change the medium (interfaces, local
concentration, water activity) and are not a model of the engine's well-mixed aqueous pot; they are evidence on
matrix effects only and are not scored here.

**Role 1: decisive unit for signature (d), the FFT shape, if and only if the aqueous control reports**
- FFT (and / or MFT) at >= 3 heating times in one pot design, and
- stated cysteine and ribose charges, temperature and time, and the pH or buffer (on a stated scale), and
- replicate noise or enough replicates to estimate it.

If met, the unit is the time-course SHAPE (levels normalised to one time point, so a calibration offset cancels;
value basis "ratio", no between-laboratory tau), scored under M1.1's rules (omega 0.419 dex per point, exact or
PSIS densities, the adequacy rule, floors from the paper's own LOD). It is out of every objective and is the only
unseen decisive FFT-shape unit for M1c. If not met (e.g. one time point), the source is evidence only and the case's
verdict stays development-grade.

**Role 2: candidate second-laboratory absolute thiol data for lifting H0's refusal (EX-059, D2 branch (i)).**
Absolute MFT / FFT from the aqueous control, if quantified with a stated method (ideally stable-isotope dilution)
and a recovery, are what H0's ruling says would lift the refusal of absolute thiol levels for a new laboratory. Using
them for that requires its OWN pre-registration, written and frozen before any engine prediction for these pots is
computed or seen: which rows, how the between-laboratory tau is re-estimated with the added laboratory, and what lifts
or keeps the refusal. Until then, no engine output may be compared with these values.

**Handling.** The extraction dossier marks every Vauthey row "unseen by the engine, reserved (M1c A1 roles 1-2)".
No generator, screen or probe may run the engine on these pots until the relevant design is frozen.

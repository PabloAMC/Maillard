## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

Heating each lipid with cysteine + ribose **cuts saturated n-aldehydes about two-fold and 2-alkenals /
2,4-alkadienals by more than ten-fold, often to zero**, while 2-alkanones and miscellaneous ketones are
"little affected" and 1-alcohols and 2-alkylfurans **rise** with PC (up to ~20×). Hexanal +/− ratio:
BTG 0.47, BPL 0.50, PC 0.83, PE 0.32. The authors reject a general antioxidant effect (ketones unchanged)
and attribute the aldehyde loss to reaction with cysteine's amino/thiol groups, NH3 and H2S. The PE (and
plasmalogen-rich BPL) lipids already give >10× less unsaturated aldehyde than PC/BTG when heated alone,
attributed to the ethanolamine amine.

| item | as printed |
|---|---|
| Lipids | BTG from subcutaneous rump fat, BPL from *M. semimembranosus* of a Hereford × Friesian steer (~15 months) (extraction as Farmer & Mottram 1990); egg PC (~99 %) and egg PE (~98 %), Sigma |
| Pot | **lipid 30 mg** in a flamed 2.5 mL freeze-drying ampoule; **2 mL** of either phosphate buffer (0.5 M, pH 5.6) or the same buffer containing **cysteine 5 mg/mL and ribose 4.5 mg/mL**; also Cys+ribose without lipid and buffer alone; quadruplicate |
| [Z] concentrations | Cys **41.3 mM**, ribose **30.0 mM** (Cys:R 1.38); lipid 15 g/L |
| Atmosphere | N2 blown over 2 min, flame-sealed |
| Emulsification | ultrasonic bath, 40 °C, 1 h |
| Heating | **1 h at 140 °C** in an autoclave (Certoclav); come-up not stated |
| Isolation | each mixture (2 mL) diluted to 20 mL in phosphate buffer, stirred at **60 °C**, headspace swept onto Tenax with N2 **50 mL/min** (duration not restated here; previous papers); internal standard C10–C24 alkanes (10 ng each) + **1,2-dichlorobenzene 65 ng** on the trap |
| GC-MS | Carlo Erba 4130 + Finnigan 4000, CPWAX52CB 50 m × 0.32 mm, CO2-cooled front |

## 5. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

- **Sulfur lane: nothing.** No sulfur volatile is reported (they are in `farmer1990_extraction.md`, the
  companion paper on the same experimental design).
- **Lipid lane / matrix (the lipid-aldehyde / hexanal over-prediction work):** this is a
  **replicated within-study ratio** for how much a cysteine + ribose reaction (41 mM / 30 mM, 140 °C, 1 h,
  pH 5.6) lowers headspace aldehydes from four real lipids. A model that lets hexanal accumulate from
  lipid oxidation with no amine/thiol/H2S sink will over-predict hexanal by ~1.2–3× in a Maillard-active
  pot, and 2-alkenals/2,4-alkadienals by 10× to > 50×. Usable as a **HOLD-OUT ratio test** (the +/− ratio
  per lipid), not as an absolute level (no calibration, no response factors, headspace trapping).
- **Mechanistic constraint:** ketones and 1-octen-3-ol are not suppressed → the sink is carbonyl-specific
  (aldehyde + amine/thiol), not radical scavenging. That matches an aldehyde–amine/–H2S consumption step
  rather than an antioxidant term.
- **PE vs PC:** endogenous ethanolamine already removes unsaturated aldehydes (PE − alkenals 0.7 % vs PC
  23.1 % of total RPA) — supports a lipid-headgroup amine sink in `matrix_sites.py`-type bookkeeping.
- **Not usable:** absolute amounts; any temperature or time dependence (one condition).

## M1c case criterion (frozen bd641ae8)

The frozen case (`docs/reference/cases/M1c_thiol_supply_structure.md` on `origin/m1c-case`, sec. 4.3) names davidek2003
**decisive for R-cal only on a Zhang-like pot (cysteine + pentose, ~40 mM, pH ~6.5, ~120 °C)**, on: *free Cys, free
pentose and TTCA / CAMA by an assay independent of ELSD, >= 2 times*; otherwise *floors for its own assay*.

**Verdict: NOT MET.**
- **No Zhang-like pot.** The two pots are glucose + glycine 1.0 + 1.0 mol/L, label pH 9, 90 °C, and DFG 0.1 mol/L,
  0.1 M phosphate label pH 8, 120 °C. Neither contains cysteine or a pentose.
- **No free cysteine, free pentose, TTCA or CAMA** is measured, at any time.
- **No time series**: one chromatogram per pot (3 h; 1 h), and no concentration printed from either.
- **The assay is ELSD-independent** (integrated amperometry + DAD), but the successor paper reports that this
  anion-exchange column decomposes a pentose Amadori compound on-column, so the assay as published could not quantify
  a pentose Amadori species.
- **The fallback role, floors for its own assay, is filled**: LOD 1.0 (glucose), 2.0 (glycine), 0.5 (DFG) umol/L in the
  injected solution; 1.0 / 2.0 / 0.5 mmol/L in the pot at the paper's 1000x dilution [Z]; linear to 100 umol/L
  (50 for glycine).

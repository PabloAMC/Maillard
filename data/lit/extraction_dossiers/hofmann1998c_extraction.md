### *"Identification of key aroma compounds generated from cysteine and carbohydrates under roasting conditions"*
### Cysteine 3.3 mmol + ribose, glucose or rhamnose 10.0 mmol on 3.0 g silica gel carrying 300 µL of 0.5 M phosphate pH 5.0, closed vessel, 180 °C / 6 min ("dry-heating"). AEDA of acidic and neutral/basic fractions for all three sugars; stable-isotope dilution for FOUR PYRAZINES ONLY. **No MFT or FFT amount is printed anywhere in this paper** — every thiol number is a flavour dilution (FD) factor. ## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

The dry-heated twin of Hofmann & Schieberle 1995 (ribose) and 1997 (glucose, rhamnose). AEDA ranks
2-furfurylthiol (FFT, FD **16 384** in the acidic fraction) and 2-acetyl-2-thiazoline (FD 1024) highest
from ribose; 2-acetyl- and 2-propionyl-2-thiazoline and FFT from glucose; 5-methyl-2-furfurylthiol (FD
4096), HDMF (furaneol), the two thiazolines and (Z)-2-propenyl-3,5-dimethylpyrazine (PDMP, new
odorant) from rhamnose. Compared with the aqueous 145 °C / 20 min systems (Table 4), dry-heating raises
the thiazolines and four alkylpyrazines and lowers 3-mercapto-2-pentanone, 3-mercapto-2-butanone and
5-acetyl-2,3-dihydro-1,4-thiazine; FFT goes **up** for ribose (FD 4096 → 16 384) but **down** for glucose
(1024 → 256) and rhamnose (512 → 32); MFT goes down in FD for ribose (1024 → 256). **The only
quantities are four pyrazines by SIDA (Table 5, µg).** The FFT/MFT amounts that the text alludes to
("recently been confirmed by quantitative measurements [5]") live in Hofmann & Schieberle 1998 JAFC
(`hofmann1998_reconciliation.md`, Table 2 "5.0ᶜ" dry column) and Schieberle & Hofmann 1998 ACS
(`schieberle1998_extraction.md`), not here.

## 4. DERIVED [Z]

- **Per-sugar FD shifts, aqueous → dry (Table 4), log2 steps:** FFT ribose +2, glucose −2, rhamnose −4;
  MFT ribose −2; thiazine −8 (ribose), −7 (glucose), −8 (rhamnose); 3-mercapto-2-pentanone −5, −5, −4;
  2-acetyl-2-thiazoline +2, +3, +2. Compare the SIDA ratios in `hofmann1998_reconciliation.md` (FFT
  dry/aqueous ribose 8.03×, glucose 0.50×, rhamnose 0.50×; MFT 1.27×, 2.21×, 3.88×). **FD direction
  agrees for FFT in all three sugars, disagrees for MFT** (FD down ×4 for ribose while SIDA is up ×1.27) —
  the expected AEDA artefact for an MFT that oxidises in the ether extract (the paper's own no. 21 remark).
- **EDMP molar yield** (if Table 5 is per 3.3/10 mmol pot): 11 µg / 136.19 = 0.081 µmol = **2.4 × 10⁻⁵ mol/mol
  cysteine**; ten-fold lower if per 100-mmol batch. EDMP is sugar-independent (11/15/12 µg).

## 5. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

- **Cannot:** any MFT/FFT level, rate or yield — none printed. Not a benchmark-row source.
- **Can (qualitative, already held quantitatively elsewhere):** the sign of the dry/aqueous FFT shift by
  sugar class, consistent with the JAFC SIDA rows the repo already holds as HOLD-OUT
  (`hofmann1998_reconciliation.md` §2.2).
- **New relative to the corpus:** the dry-heated **glucose** AEDA (Table 2) — 2-(1-mercaptoethyl)furan
  is the glucose-specific thiol under dry conditions too; MFT FD only 4. FD factors are not levels.
- **Pyrazines:** EDMP/DEMP/EnDMP/PDMP µg are a duplicate of `schieberle1998` Table VII; do not count
  them twice. Relevant only to `parameters_pyrazine.py` if a dry-arm pyrazine target is ever wanted.
- **Moisture axis:** one dry point (~6 % w/w water, 180 °C, 6 min) — no a_w series; temperature and
  water change together, so the arm cannot separate them.

## 6. FLAGS

1. **No MFT/FFT quantity** despite the paper being listed as a "dry-heat twin" candidate; the abstract's
   "Based on quantitative data" refers to the pyrazines only.
2. **Table 5 basis ambiguous by 10×**: this paper implies the 3.3/10 mmol pot; `schieberle1998` states a
   33/100 mmol batch for the identical numbers. Unresolved; if used, carry both.
3. **Table 4 column Ia (ribose) is 4× the source it cites**: Hofmann & Schieberle 1995 (ref. [3];
   `hofmann1995_extraction.md`) prints FFT 1024, MP 512, MFT 256, thiazine 256, 3-mercapto-2-butanone 128;
   this table prints 4096/2048/1024/1024/512 — every value exactly two dilution steps higher. The glucose and
   rhamnose Ia columns match `hofmann1997_extraction.md`. Use the 1995 originals.
4. **"16284"** (rhamnose aqueous pyranone) is not a power of two; Hofmann 1997 prints **16384**. Typo.
5. **Glucose MFT Ia printed "<4"**; Hofmann 1997 has MFT below AEDA detection (<1) in glucose.
6. **Buffer molarity** 0.5 M here vs 2 M in Hofmann 1998 JAFC for the same dry arm (see §2).
7. **SIDA replicates not stated**; no SD.
8. **Text-layer glyph damage** (µ→m, <→~, –→P, °→7): anyone re-extracting from `pdftotext` will read
   Table 5 as milligrams.

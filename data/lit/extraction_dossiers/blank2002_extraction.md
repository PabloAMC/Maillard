## 1. ONE-PARAGRAPH VERDICT

This paper gives a **complete 8-composition Fenton-reagent factorial for FFT loss measured at BOTH
37 °C and 22 °C** (Table 3) — the requested two aqueous temperature arms — plus a metal/reductant
substitution set at 37 °C only (Table 4), a 22 °C 8-day stability series in three simple systems
(Table 2), a product-distribution table across six Fenton compositions (Table 6, µg/mL, 1 h, 37 °C),
full EI/CI mass spectra and retention indices for nine volatile products (Table 5), and two
figure-only kinetic series at 37 °C over 2 h (Fig. 3 = FFT decay; Fig. 4 = mono-/di-/trisulfide
formation). **There are NO rate constants, NO half-lives, NO Arrhenius parameters and NO activation
energies anywhere in the paper.** The authors print no regression, no R², no fitted model — every
curve in Figures 3 and 4 is an unlabelled hand-drawn "general tendency" line, explicitly so described
in the Figure 4 caption. All error bars are standard deviations from at least duplicate reactions,
each injected twice into the GC (Table 3 note b states variation coefficients 1–6 %, mean 3.5 %;
Table 6 note a states variation coefficients < 10 %). Two structural caveats limit the model value:
(i) the thiol is delivered **in 3.3 % aqueous SDS**, so the "aqueous" systems contain surfactant
above its CMC (see §2 and [Z] derivation), and (ii) the 37 °C and 22 °C arms of Table 3 were run at
**different initial FFT concentrations** (3.8 mM vs 3.2 mM), which confounds the temperature contrast.
There is only ONE pH (5.5 for all Fenton systems; 5.1 for the Table 2 systems) — this paper provides
no pH axis at all.

### 2.3 System (ii) — the eight Fenton model systems (basis of Tables 1, 3, 6)

> "The aqueous solutions described in Table 1 were freshly prepared before use, that is, ascorbic acid

> vigorously for 5 s and incubated for 1 h at 37 °C, after which time EtOH (0.1 mL) was added to
> terminate the Fenton reaction. Chemical analyses were performed after the pH had been adjusted to
> pH 3.5 and neutral compounds extracted with Et2O (1 mL). The organic phase was centrifuged (5–15

- **Temperatures:** "All reactions, however, were performed at physiological (37 °C) and/or room

- **Quench:** EtOH 0.1 mL.
- **Analytical method:** GC-FID (quantification) / GC-MS / GC-FPD; on-column injection.
- **Vessel, headspace, atmosphere (air vs inert), agitation during incubation: not stated** beyond

## 1. Methods

All from p. 2349 (Materials) and p. 2350 (Preparation, SDE, GC-MS).

- **Substrates.** Spray-dried chicken egg albumin (Nive, NL); purified casein (Sigma); maltodextrin DE 12
  (Roquette). Compounds: **1** bis(2-methyl-3-furanyl) disulfide (MFT disulfide), **2** 2-methyl-3-furanthiol
  (MFT), **3** 2-furanmethanethiol (FFT), **4** bis(2-furanylmethyl) disulfide (FFT disulfide). 1-3 from
  Aldrich, 4 a gift from a flavour company.
- **Charge.** 50 g substrate in 750 mL distilled water (**66.7 g/L, derived here**: 50/0.75), plus either
  2.5 g of a commercial savoury flavouring (composition unknown to the authors) or **500 µg of one
  disulfide** in 0.5 mL ethanol, or 40 µg thiol 3 + 500 µg disulfide 4. Stirred gently 2 h (temperature
  not printed; ambient implied).
- **Heating = the extraction.** Simultaneous steam distillation-solvent extraction, modified
  Likens-Nickerson, **2 h**, pentane 27 mL + diethyl ether 3 mL. The abstract calls this "heated at 100 °C
  in aqueous solution"; no other temperature is printed. Internal standard 130 µg 1,2-dichlorobenzene.
- **pH:** not printed, not controlled. **Protein SH or S-S content:** not printed for either protein (only
  the qualitative statement that casein has "a very low proportion of sulfhydryl groups").
- Quantitation of 1-4 against authentic standards (TIC areas); other compounds approximate. Triplicates.

Concentration of the disulfide charge (derived here): 500 µg / 226.31 g/mol = 2.21 µmol in 0.750 L =
**2.9 µmol/L**; maximum thiol obtainable = 4.42 µmol.

**Text findings (p. 2350-2351):**
- Disulfide falls "by almost 100-fold" with albumin while the thiol rises; no such change with
  maltodextrin. Disulfide 4 recovery from water "almost 100 %".
- Thiol 3 added to water or maltodextrin was mostly lost "either on mixing or during the extraction" and
  the disulfide INCREASED, i.e. the free thiol oxidised in the protein-free systems.
- "In the systems containing protein there was no evidence of thiol oxidation but the disulfides were
  reduced to the corresponding thiol." Not all lost disulfide comes back as thiol, attributed to
  "formation of disulfide links between protein and thiol".
- Mechanism proposed, not measured: sulfhydryl-disulfide interchange with protein cysteine/cystine.
  Albumin "contains a much greater proportion of these -SH groups than casein" (no number printed).
- Odour thresholds quoted (cited, not measured): disulfide 1 2 × 10^-5 µg/kg, thiol 2 5 × 10^-3 µg/kg.

## 3. What it means for the model

**The direction the model lacks.** The sulfur lane's only protein channel is `ch_protein_ss_fft` /
`ch_protein_ss_mft` (`src/kinetic_core/sulfur.py` lines 629-652): thiol + protein disulfide site
(`PROT_SS`) -> protein-bound thiol `PRB`, **terminal**, at the code constant
`k_protein_ss` = sqrt(3.0e-6 × 1.22e-5) = **6.05e-6 L/(mmol·min)** (derived here from
`PROTEIN_DISULFIDE_EXCHANGE_BRACKET_L_PER_MMOL_MIN`, `src/kinetic_core/parameters_sulfur.py` ~line 866;
source anchor Anantharamkrishnan & Reineccius 2020b Fig. 7c, ambient, no Ea; evidence class
`bounded_from_a_timescale_bracket`). It is not a fitted coordinate and is absent from
`core_prediction_uncertainty.json`; `PROT_SS` is zero in every scored system so it carries no flux.
Mottram measures the **opposite arrow** as the dominant one at ~100 °C: protein (albumin) converts flavour
**disulfide into free thiol** (25 % FFT, 45 % MFT molar yield), with the rest lost to protein binding.
The model has no protein-driven disulfide reduction at all; `species_sulfur.py` (~line 389) already
records PRB terminality and the missing release as a known gap.

**The disulfide release coordinate.** `k_dimer_release` (thiol disulfide -> 2 thiol) exists but is held at
its inert default **zero** (`dimer_release_parameters`, B17 not shipped; barrier would be Zhang 2026's
122.2 kJ/mol). The dimerisation coordinates the engine does run, from
`results/validation/core_prediction_uncertainty.json`:

| key | centre | distribution / band | reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| `b8.k_dimer_mft.log10_k_ref_145C` | 0.500 (L²/(mmol²·min), 3rd order) | normal_log10, σ 0.354, band [-10, 0.5] | laplace_covariance_at_b8_optimum; **bound_limited** (data_wishlist §1), sitting on the 0.5 ceiling |
| `b8.k_dimer_fft.log10_k_ref_145C` | 0.500 | normal_log10, σ 0.549, band [-10, 0.5] | same; **bound_limited** |
| `b8.k_dimer_decay.log10_k_ref_145C` | -9.71 | normal_log10, σ 1.2e-5 | laplace; **flat** in data_wishlist §1 (effectively zero) |

**Can it pin anything?** No rate. Single endpoint after 2 h ambient stirring plus 2 h SDE, with the
volatiles removed continuously during heating, no time course, no temperature series, no pH, and no
protein SH or S-S titre, so neither a pseudo-first-order constant nor a second-order constant per site can
be formed. What it does give is (i) a **qualitative gate**: in a protein matrix at ~100 °C the
disulfide/thiol ratio must move toward the thiol, which a model with terminal binding and zero release
cannot produce; (ii) a **matrix contrast** (albumin >> casein, maltodextrin and water nil) consistent with
SH-content dependence; (iii) a size: ≥ 98.8 % of 2.9 µM disulfide consumed with 67 g/L albumin within the
4 h protocol.

**Relevance to plant-protein products.** The plant isolates in the product matrix are not measured here.
The paper's own explanation ties the effect to free -SH content, so a plant isolate's free-SH and S-S
titre (not on file here) would be the variable to measure before transferring this.

## What it does not give

- Any temperature other than the nominal ~100 °C of SDE; any time course; any rate constant or Ea.
- pH of any system; protein SH or disulfide content; protein identity beyond "egg albumin" and "casein".
- Mass balance closure: 54-74 % of the sulfur is unaccounted for in the albumin systems; the bound form is
  inferred, not measured.
- Whether the loss happened during the 2 h ambient stirring or during the 2 h SDE: the design does not
  separate them.
- Any plant protein.

# Qi, Wang, Zhan & Tian 2022 — EXTRACTION
### *"Characterization of key aroma compounds in stewed mutton (goat meat) added with thyme (Thymus vulgaris L.) based on the combination of instrumental analysis and sensory verification"*
### Boer-goat hind-leg meat (fat : lean 2 : 8) stewed 1 h in water with salt and thyme in four utensils (casserole, wok, pressure cooker, 120 °C sealed bottle). HS-SPME (70 °C) GC-MS/O; 26 odorants quantified by 5-point external calibration in a deodorised SMT matrix (SIM), µg/kg minced meat. **The only sulfur compounds detected, even by GC-O, are methanethiol (2.8-10.4 µg/kg) and dimethyl trisulfide (16.5-27.9 µg/kg). MFT, FFT and methional were neither detected nor quantified.** Hexanal 8,600-23,300 µg/kg. Read 2026-10-28.

**Source on disk:** `data/articles/qi2022.pdf` (8 pp., typeset version of record). Tables 1, 3, 4 read
from `pdftotext -layout`; **Table 2 re-typed from a 170-dpi render** (scratchpad `img/qiT2-5.png`;
the text layer splits every concentration and its SD over two lines). Fig. 1 (heatmap) and Fig. 2
(sensory radar) not digitised (no new concentrations).

## 1. Methods [M]

| item | as printed |
|---|---|
| Meat | hind legs of **Boer goat**, market in Jinan (Shandong); fascia removed; fat and lean separated, sliced 2.0 cm; slaughterhouse standard, 4 °C overnight |
| Thyme | *Thymus vulgaris* leaves, Yulin (Shaanxi), flowering period, early summer 2019; sun-dried to < 4.5 % moisture |
| Stew | **200 g raw mutton (fat : lean = 2 : 8, m/m)** blanched 1 min in boiling water → **300 mL deionised water + 3 g salt + 0.6 g thyme**; **stewed 1 h after boiling**; cooled in ice water, **minced** (the meat); vacuum-packed, −20 °C, ≤ 2 weeks |
| Utensils | **CS** casserole and **WS** wok (normal pressure; boiling water added every 20 min to the initial level); **PCS** pressure cooker (**pressure/temperature not stated** [!]); **SS** simulated stew in a **pressure-resistant bottle, oil bath 120 °C**, 300 rpm. Three replicates each |
| SPME | **5 g SMT + 5 µL 1,2-dichlorobenzene (100 ng/µL in MeOH)**, 20 mL vial, PTFE/silicone septum; PAL RSI 85; **70 °C equilibration 20 min + extraction 25 min**; 50/30 µm DVB/CAR/PDMS (1 cm); desorb 250 °C, 5 min splitless |
| GC-MS | Agilent 7890B / 5977A; **DB-WAX** 60 m × 0.25 mm × 0.25 µm and **HP-5** 30 m × 0.25 mm × 0.25 µm; He 1.0 mL/min; EI 70 eV, m/z 30-400 |
| GC-O | ODP 3 (Gerstel), DB-WAX, 1:1 split; three trained assessors; 0-5 intensity scale; duplicate runs |
| Quantification | **5-point external standard curves** in a **deodorised SMT matrix** (homogenised SMT washed with boiling water, MeOH, diethyl ether, CH₂Cl₂ until no volatiles by SPME-GC-MS); standards 10-1000 mg/L in MeOH diluted in water, 10 µL + 5 µL IS into the matrix; **SIM**; triplicate |
| OAV | concentration / threshold **in water** (literature) |
| Sensory | 10 panelists; recombination of 20 odorants (OAV ≥ 1) into odourless matrix; omission by triangle tests (48 complete + 24 omission models) |

## 3. Derived / comparative [Z]

**Absolute meat thiol levels — the brief's question.** This paper quantifies **only two sulfur
odorants, methanethiol and DMTS**, by matrix-matched external calibration (the strongest
quantitation design among the stewed-meat papers on disk apart from SIDA). **MFT, FFT, methional,
bis(2-methyl-3-furyl) disulfide and H₂S do not appear in the GC-O list (31 odour-active regions) or in
the MS identifications.** With a DVB/CAR/PDMS fibre at 70 °C and GC-O, MFT and FFT (thresholds
0.007-0.01 µg/L) would normally be sniffed if present at ≥ ~0.1-1 µg/kg, so their absence is weak
evidence that they are below that level in stewed goat — but SPME of trace thiols from a 20 %-fat
matrix is lossy, and the "unknown 1" (leather, rubber; RI 1306 DB-WAX) and "unknown 2" (roasted nuts,
burnt; 1320) regions sit close to where MFT elutes on wax (lit. RI ~1300-1320) [!] — an
unassigned MFT cannot be excluded.

| quantity | this paper (stewed goat, µg/kg meat) | `guth1994` (stewed beef juice, µg/kg juice, SIDA) | `fan2018` (chicken broth, ng/g = µg/kg broth) |
|---|---|---|---|
| methanethiol | **2.8-10.4** | **300** | — |
| DMTS | **16.5-27.9** | — | — |
| MFT | not detected | **not detected** | 1.07-1.48 |
| FFT | not detected | 0.5 | 2.71 (BYJ) |
| methional | not detected | 13 | 118-158 |
| hexanal | **8,624-23,338** | 72 | 107-141 |

**[!] Hexanal is 100-300× the beef-juice and chicken-broth values.** Possible contributors: 20 % fat
in the meat (vs broth/juice), mutton's lipid profile, the quantity being per kg of minced fatty meat,
and the SPME at 70 °C (5 g, 20 mL vial) that may generate hexanal during extraction from an
unprotected fatty matrix. The calibration slope for hexanal (0.0059) is the second lowest in Table 2
and its R² (0.9888) the lowest — treat the level as order-of-magnitude.

**Methanethiol falls with pressure/temperature** (CS 10.4, WS 7.2 → PCS 2.8, SS 3.9 µg/kg), while DMTS
does not change (16.5-27.9, all group ᵃ): consistent with MeSH oxidation/coupling to polysulfides at
the higher temperature, or MeSH venting from the pressure vessels on opening (it boils at 6 °C).

**Utensil effect on lipid markers:** 1-octen-3-ol ×5 and 2-pentylfuran ×5 higher in SS (120 °C,
sealed) than CS; hexanal ÷2.2 lower in SS — sealed 120 °C does not simply scale oxidation.

## 4. What this can and cannot anchor in the model

**Can:**
1. **A matrix-calibrated methanethiol level in a 1 h stewed meat: 2.8-10.4 µg/kg** (open vs
   pressure), and **DMTS 16.5-27.9 µg/kg** — usable as order-of-magnitude hold-outs for the
   methionine lane's MeSH and polysulfide pools in a stewed-meat pot, with the caveat that the
   paper does not report the meat's methionine, cysteine or water content.
2. **A null on MFT/FFT/methional by GC-O + MS** in stewed goat (1 h, 100-120 °C) — weak, as
   discussed; it agrees in sign with Guth & Grosch 1994 (no MFT in stewed beef juice by SIDA).
3. Lipid-aldehyde levels in a stewed red meat, if the lipid lane ever scores meat (with the
   hexanal caveat).

**Cannot:**
- **No absolute MFT or FFT** — the brief's main target is not in this paper.
- No precursor data (free amino acids, reducing sugars, thiamine, cysteine), no pH, no kinetics.
- Pressure-cooker temperature not stated; SS at 120 °C oil bath but internal temperature not
  measured.
- Thyme adds terpenes/phenols (thymol, carvacrol) that are radical scavengers and could themselves
  alter thiol and lipid chemistry; no thyme-free control.
- OAVs use water thresholds for a fatty meat matrix.

---

## 5. Flags

1. **No MFT/FFT** despite "key aroma compounds" framing; two unknown GC-O regions near MFT's wax RI.
2. Hexanal 8.6-23.3 mg/kg — anomalously high vs every stewed-meat dossier on disk.
3. Methanethiol's calibration intercept (0.001) and slope (1.0382) on m/z 47 SIM: methanethiol
   standard (95 %, a gas at room temperature) handling in MeOH/water is not described; its
   recovery is uncertain.
4. Table 2's (E)-2-heptenal in Table 1 is detected only in PCS by GC-O yet quantified in all four
   samples.
5. Pressure-cooker conditions unspecified.
6. Units: µg/kg of **minced stewed meat** (not broth).

# Elmore, Mottram, Enser & Wood 1999 — EXTRACTION
### *"Effect of the Polyunsaturated Fatty Acid Composition of Beef Muscle on the Profile of Aroma Volatiles"*
### Longissimus lumborum steaks from Charolais steers fed four fat supplements (palm-oil control, linseed, fish oil, linseed + fish oil), 80 g in sealed 100 mL bottles, **autoclaved 140 °C / 30 min**, held 4 °C overnight, chopped; 60 g swept with N2 onto Tenax at 60 °C / 1 h; GC-MS vs 100 ng 1,2-dichlorobenzene, response factor 1; **ng/100 g, means of five animals, no SD printed, LSD letters**. Raising muscle n-3 PUFA (by tens of mg/100 g) raises the C18:1/C18:2-derived n-alkanals, 2-alkenals and 1-alkanols **~2–4×** (hexanal 545 → 1284–1381) while Maillard-only volatiles (pyrazines, Strecker aldehydes, furfurals, thiazoles) do not move. A fatty-acid-profile → aldehyde-slate lever, in meat, with **no Maillard lever arm**. Read 2026-11-14.

## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

Four groups of steers were fed concentrates carrying palm oil (control), bruised linseed, fish oil, or
linseed + fish oil. Muscle C18:3 n-3 went 22 → 43 mg/100 g with linseed; EPA 11 → 23 and DHA 2.2 → 4.6
with fish oil; linoleic (64–81) and oleic (1209–1471) did not differ significantly. Steaks pressure-
cooked 140 °C / 30 min gave **hexanal 545 (control) vs 1304 / 1284 / 1381 ng/100 g** (P = 0.028),
**heptanal 546 vs 2037 / 1732 / 2487**, pentanal, 1-hexanol, 1-heptanol, 2-octenal, 2-nonenal, 2-decenal
also up ~2–4×; **nonanal (270 vs 514 / 374 / 511), octanal, 2,4-decadienal (4–7) and 2-pentylfuran
(342 / 332 / 520 / 328) not significant**. The n-3-specific products (2-ethylfuran, 2-(2-pentenyl)furan,
octatrienes, nonadienes) rose most with fish oil. Pyrazines, 2-/3-methylbutanal, furfurals, thiazoles,
3-thiazolines did not differ. The authors' reading: the extra n-3 PUFA (the more easily initiated
chains) **promote the autoxidation of the far more abundant oleic and linoleic chains** during
cooking, so the aldehyde increase comes from C18:1/C18:2, not from the n-3 chains themselves.

  -ethanolamines lost 32 % of fatty acids, 62 % of PUFA and > 90 % of fatty aldehydes by hydrolysis.

## 5. USE IN THIS REPO

**Question it was downloaded for (L1 scoping, branch `lipid-lever-scoping`):** fatty-acid profile →
aldehyde slate, and the pea-isolate carrier's declared `linoleic_pct 50 / linolenic_pct 12 / oleic_pct
22` in `src/kinetic_core/parameters_lipid.py` `LIPID_CARRIERS["pea_protein_isolate"]`.

- **Distance from the P3 pot.** Temperature and time are **close** (140 °C / 30 min sealed vs 140 °C /
  20 min). Everything else is far: whole beef muscle (~3.5–4.3 g fatty acid/100 g, oleic-dominated,
  ~2 % linoleic) vs a 15 % pea-isolate aqueous pot whose lipid is linoleic-dominated; meat pH (not
  stated, typically 5.4–5.8) vs pH 6–7; no added sugar or sulfur source (the Maillard precursors are
  endogenous); 60 °C Tenax purge with response factor 1, an index rather than a concentration.
- **Pea carrier fatty-acid shares: cannot support.** No pea, no plant lipid; the beef shares
  (linoleic 1.5–2.3 %, linolenic 0.6–1.0 %) are an order of magnitude below the declared pea values and
  give no evidence for or against 50 / 12 / 22. Those stay `declared_assumption`.
- **What it does support (mechanism pointer, plus one within-study ratio):** at a fixed cook, a small
  shift towards more highly unsaturated chains raised hexanal by **+0.37 to +0.40 dex** and heptanal by
  +0.50 to +0.66 dex, with nonanal (+0.14 to +0.28, NS) and 2-pentylfuran (−0.02 to +0.18, NS) moving
  less. A lane whose aldehyde yield is a fixed per-fatty-acid split of a pre-existing hydroperoxide
  pool, with no initiation term, cannot reproduce this: the response is a **rate (initiation /
  chain) effect on the abundant C18:1/C18:2 chains**, not a stoichiometric product of the added n-3.
  This is evidence for the declared `LOOH_FORMATION_GAP` mattering, not a parameter value.
- **Maillard levers (sugar, sulfur source, pH): nothing.** No arm varies them. The paper shows the
  converse independence: Maillard-only volatiles are flat (|dex| ≤ 0.12) across a 2.5× hexanal swing,
  and the lipid–Maillard adducts (2-alkyl-3-thiazolines, ethylthiapyran, 2-ethylthiophene) rise only
  where the n-3 products rise.
- **Not usable for a declared band** on any P3 lever. At most, a check that a fatty-acid-profile
  sensitivity of the order of ±0.4 dex on hexanal is plausible when the unsaturation of the carrier
  is uncertain — i.e. it argues the carrier's FA band is a larger hexanal uncertainty than the
  sugar/pH levers (scoping note puts those at ±0.3 dex).
- **Hold-out discipline [!].** `farmer1992` and `elmore2002` are designated hold-outs; **do not pool
  this paper with them into any proposed band**. Note also that all three are the same Reading lab, so
  under a leave-one-lab-out split this paper falls in the same fold as the two hold-outs.

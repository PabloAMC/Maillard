## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

Grilled mutton contains **MFT 2.6-3.3 µg/kg, FFT 2.6-5.9 µg/kg, bis(2-methyl-3-furyl) disulfide 4.3-7.9
µg/kg, methional 7.0-10.4 µg/kg and 2-acetylthiazole 18-48 µg/kg** (per kg grilled meat). Brushing suet
raises every Maillard sulfur odorant by 1.3-2.7× and the lipid aldehydes by much more (octanal 10×,
(E)-2-octenal 10×); the authors attribute the sulfur rise to better heat transfer. The MFT disulfide
carries ~63-71 % of MFT-derived sulfur ([Z] molar, as MFT equivalents). **Hexanal was sniffed only at
log2FD 2 (A) and not detected (B) and was not quantified; DMDS and DMTS do not appear** (methanethiol at
log2FD 0/2 only). (E,E)-2,4-decadienal is at log2FD 1, which the authors attribute to loss in the grill
fumes. Recombinates of the 18 OAV ≥ 1 odorants scored 4.75 of 5 for both samples.

## 2. METHODS [M]

| item | as printed |
|---|---|
| Meat | hindquarter of 12-month-old sheep (Beijing Ershang Group; GB 2707-2016 slaughter); aged 0-4 °C 48 h; vacuum-packed, −20 °C |
| Preparation | fat and connective tissue removed; cubes ~2 cm³ [!] ("approximately 2 cm3"; probably 2 cm edge); **25 g per skewer** on 10 cm bamboo sticks |
| Cooking | "grilled with a barbecue grill at a distance of **10 cm from the charcoal fire at 240–250 °C for 10 min**, while turning over the clusters every 20 s". [!] Whether 240-250 °C is the fire, the grill air or the surface is not stated; no core temperature, no weight loss |
| Suet brushing (A) | **2.5 g** of the melted trimmed fat brushed per 25-g skewer during grilling ([Z] 10 % w/w). (B) no brushing |
| Replicates | 3 samples × (500 g meat in 20 skewers) per treatment |
| Isolation | grilled meat dropped into 300 mL CH2Cl2, minced, extracted (300 mL + 2 × 300 mL fresh), SAFE, Na2SO4, Vigreux to **0.5 mL** |
| GC-MS | DB-WAX, splitless; EI 70 eV; SIM for quantitation |
| GC-O | AEDA on HP-5, three sniffers, serial 1:2 dilutions; FD = mean of three |
| Quantification | odorants with high FD (log2FD ≥ 5) plus four sheep-odour compounds; **peak area vs 1,2-dichlorobenzene (37.5 µg/mL) calibration in CH2Cl2**; concentration in concentrate × extraction yield ÷ **recovery factor**, the RF "determined by spiking known amounts of authentic chemicals to the meat residue after dichloromethane extraction" (as Fan 2018). **Not stable-isotope dilution** |
| Thresholds | in water, van Gemert 2011 (4-methyloctanoic acid: citrate buffer pH 5.5) |
| Sensory | 12 graduate students; recombinate in propylene glycol at measured levels; triangle tests (ISO 4120:2004); served at 50 °C |

## 4. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

**Can anchor (external real-food levels, IS + recovery-factor class, same as Fan 2018):**
- **Grilled mutton MFT 2.6-3.3, FFT 2.6-5.9, MFT disulfide 4.3-7.9, methional 7-10 µg/kg meat.** This
  widens the cooked-meat band from Guth 1994 + Fan 2018 upward for MFT (1.1-1.5 -> up to 3.3) and FFT
  (0.5-2.7 -> up to 5.9), and downward for methional (13-160 -> from 7).
- **The disulfide share (63-71 % of MFT-derived sulfur)** agrees with Fan 2018's BYJ chicken (~60 %): two
  independent meats from the same lab, same method, put more MFT in the disulfide than as free thiol. As
  in Fan 2018, part of this may form during SAFE work-up; it is a within-method ratio.
- **Fat-addition direction:** +10 % suet raises MFT 1.3×, FFT 2.3×, methional 1.5×, and the lipid
  aldehydes 1.1-10×. A sign test for any lipid-Maillard coupling, not a magnitude (heat transfer
  changes too).

**Cannot anchor:**
- **No kinetics, no precursors in meat** (no ribose, cysteine, thiamine, IMP); only the suet fatty acids.
- **The thermal history is a charcoal grill**: 10 min, temperature of unclear reference, no core/surface
  temperature, no water loss. Not a temperature-defined pot.
- **Hexanal: no number.** Low FD on a grilled product; this paper cannot extend the hexanal band.
- **DMDS/DMTS: absent** from AEDA; methanethiol at FD ≤ 4. Absence in a SAFE extract of a 10-min grill.

**Caveats / flags:**
1. [!] Quantification is one IS with external calibration in CH2Cl2 and recovery factors (0.77-1.00)
   measured by spiking the solvent-extracted meat residue at the end of the process: losses during
   grilling and thiol-disulfide exchange during SAFE are not covered. Same class as Fan 2018; ±2× on the
   thiols (my judgement, ~70 %).
2. [!] **2-Acetylthiazole was quantified (48.38/17.79 µg/kg) although the text says it "was only detected
   using GC-O and not GC-MS"** owing to co-elution, and its ID column lacks MS. Its GC-MS-SIM number is
   internally contradictory; do not use it.
3. [!] Thresholds: 1-octen-3-ol 0.003 µg/kg (most compilations ~1 µg/kg) inflates its OAV ~300×;
   (E,Z)-2,6-nonadienal 0.0045 µg/kg is also at the low end. Concentrations are unaffected.
4. [!] Table 2 RIs are DB-WAX; Table 3 RIs are HP-5 (sniffing column). Not an inconsistency.
5. [!] "Brunt" (2-methylphenol odour) is a typo for "burnt"; "2 cm3" cubes as printed.
6. Numbers are per kg grilled meat (the meat as extracted), not per kg raw meat.

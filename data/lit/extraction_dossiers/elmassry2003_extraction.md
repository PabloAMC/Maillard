## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

**What was heated is ambiguous.** Methods: "An equal molar ratios of D-ribose and glutathione amino
acids content (glutamic acid, cysteine and glycine, as an equal molar ratios of each) were dissolved in
1 L. of deionized water and adjusted to pH 7.1 with 0.1 M sodium phosphate buffer solution" [M]; the
Discussion speaks of "glutathione mixture" [Q]. My reading: **ribose + an equimolar mix of free Glu,
Cys and Gly** standing in for glutathione [!] — the title's "glutathione" is not demonstrated. **No
amounts or concentrations are given** [!]. Refluxed 3 h (≈ 100 °C). The SDE extract gave 36 compounds
(Table 1, area %): **2-furylmethanethiol (FFT) 20.58 %, 2-methyl-3-furanthiol (MFT) 9.77 %**,
3-mercapto-2-pentanone 7.34 %, (E)/(Z)-3,5-dimethyl-1,2,4-trithiolane 5.69 + 4.36 %, 2-acetylthiazole
4.36 %, 2-formylthiophene 4.20 %, 3-mercapto-2-butanone 4.01 %, 2(3)-thiophenethiol 3.44 %; no
pyrazines. Sensory: boiled-meat and roasted-meat notes up, roasted/burnt down (Fig. 2, no numbers).
DPPH scavenging 61 % at 200 ppm (gallic acid 80 %, BHA 85 %, cinnamic acid 3 %); Cu²⁺-LDL lag time
940 min with 0.5 µmol/L of "model system" vs 259 min (GSH) and 311 min (ascorbic acid) [M].

## 4. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

**Model context.** Pentose + Cys → MFT/FFT is in the sulfur lane; GSH only via `r_gsh_h2s`. Nothing
here was run against the model.

1. **Not a glutathione source of evidence.** If the pot is ribose + free Cys + Glu + Gly (the
   literal Methods reading), it is a **free-cysteine + ribose system** and says nothing about GSH's
   fate; the high MFT share would then be expected (cf. `zhang1991_extraction.md`, IMP + Cys: MFT
   only from Cys). If it was GSH, the MFT/FFT share would contradict `zhou2023c` / `zhang1991`
   (little or no MFT from GSH) — but nothing in the paper lets one decide. **Do not use it to
   parameterise GSH.**
2. **No kinetics, no absolute amounts** (area % only, no IS, no concentrations, one time).
3. **Possible qualitative check only:** at pH 7.1, ~100 °C, 3 h, a pentose + Cys-containing pot makes
   MFT and FFT among its main SDE volatiles, FFT ≈ 2× MFT by area (detector response not corrected),
   no pyrazines. Weak; area % in an SDE extract is not a yield.

**Caveats.** Precursor identity and amounts unstated; reflux in air; phosphate buffer of unknown
strength; area % only; no replicates; antioxidant data on an undefined mixture.

## 1. Why it matters

Programme 6 wants the amino-acid identity of the Strecker step and its competition; this is the
corpus's only real-matrix experiment that adds Leu, Ile, Val, Met and Phe singly-targeted and reads
their own aldehydes back, with and without Gly + Ala competing for the same dicarbonyl pool. The
within-study ratios are clean order evidence: a 5-fold amino acid gives 4.2-5.0 times the aldehyde
(first order in the amino acid at fixed dicarbonyl supply), and adding Gly + Ala drops that to
3.2-3.7 times (shared pool). For the pyrazine lane it is the direct test of the aldehyde-addition
route (R28 + Strecker aldehyde → ethyl- and trimethyl-pyrazines): Gly + Ala together raise the
trisubstituted pyrazines 2-4.5-fold while singly they do nothing, and fed 2,3-pentanedione /
3,4-hexanedione make the ethyl-methyl and diethyl pyrazines. It is a level-only, end-of-bake paper:
validation rows and ratios, not rates.

## 2. Methods as they matter to a model

- **Dough (verbatim amounts).** 342 g potato flake + 90 g waxy maize starch + 4.5 g lecithin + 4.5 g
  NaCl + 9 g sucrose + 300 g tap water = 750 g; flake fraction 0.456 by mass. Additives dissolved in
  the water. Sheeted to 1.2 ± 0.06 mm, 5 cm disks, docked; 24 pieces per bake on an open mesh mat.
  Dough pH 5.92 ± 0.01 (unchanged by 5 % WPH).
- **Bake.** Main condition: **160 C for 6 min then 130 C for 10 min, full convection**. Preliminary:
  140 C 8 min + 110 C 15 min (lower), and 160 C 6 min + 140 C 8 min (higher). Cooled 5 min, foil
  pouches, -20 C.
- **Flake composition (printed).** Glucose 6.7 ± 0.24 g/kg = **37.2 mmol/kg flake**; fructose 3.7 ±
  0.04 g/kg = **20.5 mmol/kg**; asparagine 2552 mg/kg = **19.3 mmol/kg**, "34 %" of total free amino
  acids (so total free amino acids ≈ 7.5 g/kg flake, mine). The individual free amino acids are in
  Table S1 (not on disk).
- **Additions.** Ala, Leu, Ile, Val, Met at 4 × natural ("to make their concentrations 5 times that
  of the control"); Gly at 20 × or 100 × natural; Phe only in the "all amino acids × 2" recipe and in
  the PD/HD Strecker mixes. Printed absolute additions: **Gly 0.18 % and Ala 0.21 % on potato-flake
  basis** → Gly 1.8 g/kg flake = **24.0 mmol/kg flake** (natural Gly ≈ 18 mg/kg = 0.24 mmol/kg); Ala 2.1
  g/kg flake = **23.6 mmol/kg flake** (natural Ala ≈ 525 mg/kg = 5.9 mmol/kg). On the dough: Gly 0.82
  g/kg = 10.9 mmol/kg dough; Ala 0.96 g/kg = 10.8 mmol/kg dough. PD and HD **100 mg/kg dry basis** =
  1.00 and 0.876 mmol/kg dry. WPH 5 % dry basis (free amino acids in the WPH 75 mg/kg).
- **Amino-acid analysis.** Water extraction of ground flake, HILIC-ESI-MS/MS (Agilent 1200 / 6410,
  Syncronis HILIC), external calibration 1-50 µM in water:acetonitrile 2:8, MRM after Kocadağlı 2013.
- **Acrylamide.** 1 g + 2 mL 1 mg/L 13C3-acrylamide + 38 mL water; Hypercarb LC-ESI-MS/MS, MRM 72 →
  55 / 72 → 27, IS 75 → 58; external calibration 0.5-20 µg/L with 50 µg/L IS. Isotope dilution, absolute.
- **Volatiles.** 10 g ground product + 15 mL saturated NaCl in a 250 mL flask at 50 C; oxygen-free N2
  40 mL/min for 1 h onto Tenax TA (85 mg); **130.6 ng 1,2-dichlorobenzene** added to the trap as
  internal standard; dry-purge 100 mL/min 10 min; ATD (300 C desorb, -30 C cryofocus) GC-MS (7890A /
  5975C, ZB-Wax 30 m × 250 µm × 1 µm, 30 C 10 min → 250 C at 4 C/min; EI 70 eV; scan m/z 29-450). LRI
  with C5-C25 alkanes; identities by NIST, LRI and authentic standards (Tables S2-S3). **Semi-quantified
  against the internal standard with a response factor of 1**: the SI concentrations are relative
  headspace amounts, not calibrated concentrations. The main text reports only percentages of control.
- **Sensory.** Trained panel of 11 (QDA against an anchored control, 0-100 line, duplicate sessions);
  ANOVA + Fisher LSD at p = 0.05 (Table 1: amino-acid recipes; Table 2: PD/HD recipes, with a "cheesy"
  attribute added when Phe was present). Red light for the browner WPH samples.
- **Replicates / statistics.** Bake replicates not stated numerically ("higher baking variance
  between replicates in this set" for the WPH set); one-way ANOVA + Duncan on instrumental data.

## 5. Flags

1. **No absolute volatile numbers in the paper body.** Everything is relative to the control; the
   underlying amounts (Tables S2, S3) are IS-relative with a response factor of 1 and are not on
   disk. Nothing here is an end-of-cook concentration a model output can be compared to, except
   acrylamide.
2. **Level-only, one bake profile per set.** No time series, no temperature ladder (the two
   preliminary bakes are described qualitatively plus one acrylamide number each); no rate or barrier.
3. **Ratios carry a competition term.** The 4.2-5.0 × aldehyde response to 5 × amino acid is order
   evidence only at this flake's dicarbonyl supply; with Gly + Ala added (each ≈ 24 mmol/kg flake, i.e.
   together comparable to the flake's own total free amino acids, ≈ 7.5 g/kg ≈ 60 mmol/kg at a mean
   molar mass of ~130 g/mol) the response falls to 3.2-3.7 ×.
4. **Arithmetic inconsistency on WPH.** "The total concentration of free amino acids in whey protein
   hydrolysate was 75 mg/kg and they did not contribute to the free amino acid pool in potato flake,
   which was 1600-fold higher" implies 120 g/kg free amino acids in the flake; the Asn figure (2552
   mg/kg = 34 % of total) implies ≈ 7.5 g/kg. One of the two statements is off by ~16 ×; the Asn-based
   figure is the internally consistent one.
5. **Natural Gly level is inferred**: 0.18 % = 100 × natural gives 18 mg/kg flake, low for potato;
   Table S1 (not on disk) is the source.
6. **Matrix and moisture unstated during the bake**: dough water content, bake weight loss and the
   product's final aw are not given; the flake fraction (0.456) converts additions to a dough basis
   only before baking.
7. **The pyrazine effects are on trisubstituted isomers not in the registry** (2-ethyl-3,6-dimethyl-,
   2,3-diethyl-5-methyl-, the ethyl-methyl and diethyl pyrazines); rows are needed before any of these
   ratios can be keyed.
8. **Dynamic headspace at 50 C from a salted slurry** is a release-weighted measurement; relative
   changes within one method are sound, but the ratio between compounds of different volatility is not
   a ratio of amounts in the product.
9. **HD acrylamide artefact**: the +33 % with 3,4-hexanedione alone is attributed to a
   4-hydroxy-3-hexanone impurity; not a property of the diketone.
10. **Sensory** shows aldehydes-plus-pyrazines are needed together for "baked"; a useful hold-out
    statement for the panel, not a kinetic datum.

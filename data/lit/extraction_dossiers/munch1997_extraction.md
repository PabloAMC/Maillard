**Answers.**
1. **System** (confidence ~95 % in the reading). CYE: commercial baker's-yeast extract from an unnamed German
   manufacturer, composition not given beyond free amino acids (Table 9; total ~126 mg/g of the 15 listed) and free
   Cys 2.13 mg/g. SPYA: 300 g baker's yeast (90 g DW) autolysed 24 h at 50 °C with NaCl 5 g + ethyl acetate 4.5 g in
   1 L tap water (pH 5.6 → 5.2), supernatant freeze-dried. LMW-YE: cells milled in 0.1 M phosphate pH 7.0,
   ultrafiltered (< 1000 Da), freeze-dried; from fresh yeast or yeast "fermented" 12 h at 32 °C under O2 with glucose
   100 g/L, NaCl 35 g/L and nutrients (incl. **thiamine 44 mg/L** of medium, washed off before extraction). Heating:
   material in tap water **1:5 w/v**, laboratory autoclave (Roth type II, closed), **145 °C, 20 min**, ice-water quench;
   CYE also "boiled for 1 h at 100 °C" (vessel not stated). **pH of the heated extracts not measured**; the text calls
   pH 5.0 "the pH used in the processing experiments" [Q], which no method describes. No water activity, no
   headspace / O2 control, no heat-up time, no replicates. **No time or temperature series.**
2. **SIDA odorants: none in the yeast extracts** (confidence ~99 %). Only FFT is quantified by SIDA, and only in the
   model pots (Tables 4-6). Methional, 3-methylbutanal, phenylacetaldehyde, HDMF, sotolon, 2-AP etc. carry FD
   factors (Tables 1, 2, 8), which are a ranking of odour potency in air, not amounts.
3. **Precursor experiments** (confidence ~85 %).
   (a) FFT from Cys (33 mM) + sugar (100 mM): ribose ≫ glucose > rhamnose; ribose maximal at pH 3.0 (22.9 µg),
   hexoses at pH 5.0 (Table 4) [M]. (b) Fed furfural (10 mM if 100 mL) gives ~21× more FFT per mol than ribose at
   pH 5 with Cys [M text; Z: 0.0222 vs 0.00106 mol %], and **H2S instead of Cys raises it ×5.1 at 0.25 mmol and
   ×21.8 at 1 mmol H2S**; FFT is near-linear in H2S (×4.23 for ×4 H2S) [Z]. (c) C2 + C3 route: mercapto-2-propanone +
   hydroxyacetaldehyde gives FFT rising with pH (26.1 → 40.5 → 58.2 µg, pH 3 → 7) [M]; MGO + Cys + HA (no preformed
   mercaptoketone) gives 9.2 µg at pH 5 [M]. (d) In the extracts: CYE has 10× the free Cys of SPYA (2.13 vs
   0.21 mg/g) and ~2.75× the Cys charged per pot (209 vs 76 mg), matching the FD of FFT (2048 vs 64) [M/Q]; LMW-YEs
   with ~42-100× less Cys charged show no FFT odour [M/Q]. (e) Met ×3.9 and Phe ×7.3 in fermented vs fresh LMW-YE
   track methional and phenylacetaldehyde FD ×8 [M/Q]; Orn ×3.7 and Pro ×2.3 track 2-AP FD ×16, but the CYE, with more
   Orn and Pro charged, shows no 2-AP — the authors' conclusion: Orn does not correlate with 2-AP [Q]. **None of
   this bears on MFT from thiamine vs ribose + Cys, and nothing on methional is labelled.**
4. **Usability** (calibrated). The yeast-extract pots are **not simulable** (P(useful forced-input pot) ~0.03):
   reducing sugars, ribose / nucleotides, thiamine, dicarbonyls, pH and dry-matter basis are all missing, and the
   outputs are FD factors. As a **cross-system check for the Strecker layer or MFT route shares: no** (~0.97). The
   **model pots are simulable** with the sulfur lane (FUR + H2S → FFT exists, `r_fur_fft`), but **Tables 4, 5 expt 3
   and 6 expts 1-3 are the same runs Hofmann & Schieberle 1998 published** (sec. 6), already in the ledger and partly
   in fits. The genuinely new rows are three: furfural + Cys (25.3 µg), furfural + 0.25 mmol H2S (130.1 µg) and
   MGO + Cys + HA (9.2 µg). The two furfural rows extend the fitted furfural + H2S row into an H2S dose-response and
   a Cys-vs-H2S contrast (P(they constrain `k_fur_fft` usefully) ~0.4; pot volume conflict, sec. 5).
5. **Ledger entries**: sec. 7 — four (three sulfur, one aroma_other composition); FD tables not ledgered.

## 2. Conditions

- **Thermal treatment of extracts** [M]: CYE 100 g, SPYA 120 g (from 1200 g fresh yeast) or LMW-YE 5.1 g (from 240 g
  fresh yeast) in tap water 1:5 (w/v); laboratory autoclave Type II (Roth), **20 min at 145 °C**; ice-water cooling.
  Extract B: CYE "boiled for 1 h at 100 °C" [V]. Ramp / hold not distinguished (Hofmann 1995 describes the same
  autoclave run as a 20-min ramp 20 → 145 °C, `sulfur.yml` `hofmann1995_t1t2_aeda_sho_ranking`) [!].
- **Model FFT pots** [M]: precursor mixtures of Tables 4-6 "reacted for 20 min at 145 °C in phosphate buffer
  (100 mL; 0.5 M)" [V]. Table 4: Cys 3.3 mmol + carbohydrate 10 mmol (33 + 100 mM), pH 3.0 / 5.0 / 7.0. Table 5:
  furfural 1 mmol + Cys 1 mmol or H2S 0.25 / 1 mmol, **pH not printed**. Table 6: mercapto-2-propanone +
  hydroxyacetaldehyde 1 mmol each, pH 3 / 5 / 7; expt 4 MGO 1 mmol + Cys 4 mmol + HA 1 mmol, pH 5.0.
- **Fermentation medium** (per L tap water) [M]: glucose 100 g, NaCl 35 g, ZnSO4 28.17 g, (NH4)H2PO4 10 g, KH2PO4 4 g,
  CuSO4 1.35 g, Na citrate 1.14 g, MgSO4 0.8 g, Fe(NH4)2(SO4)2 15 mg, thiamine 44 mg, biotin 200 mg, pyridoxal 12 mg,
  m-inositol 100 mg, Ca pantothenate 5 mg; 100 g yeast, pH 5.0, 12 h, 32 °C, "under oxygen"; cells washed 3×.
- **Replicates**: none stated for any experiment; no ± anywhere [!].

## 3. Analytical methods

- **AEDA** [M]: ether extract, high-vacuum sublimation, acid / neutral-basic split; 1:1 serial dilutions; HRGC-O on
  FFAP and DB-5; FD = highest dilution still smelled. Identification by RI on two phases, MS-EI/CI, odour; 3-mercapto-
  2-pentanone identified without a clean MS [M fn c]. **FD factors are not concentrations** (odour potency in air,
  ±1 dilution step typical).
- **Free amino acids** [M]: ultrafiltrate (< 1000 Da) of ~200 µmol each amino acid, ion-exchange analyser (Biotronik
  LC 3000), ninhydrin. **Cysteine** [M]: 4-vinylpyridine derivatisation to pyridylethyl-Cys, same analyser, response
  factor from pure PEC. No LODs, no replicates.
- **FFT in model pots** [M]: SIDA with [2H]-FFT (Sen & Grosch 1991b), "according to Schieberle (1996)" [C]; details
  not repeated.
- **Basis** [M]: amino acids mg/g dry weight of CYE, or of the **baker's yeast** for SPYA and LMW-YE (Table 3, 7, 9
  footnotes); "mg reacted" = mg in the heated charge.

## 5. Flags and internal consistency

1. [!] **Table 9 glycine and histidine rows are identical in all four cells** (4.74 / 1.91 / 465 / 688), confirmed on
   the render; one row is very probably a copy error (P ~0.9). Do not use either value without that flag.
2. [!] **Same runs as Hofmann & Schieberle 1998** (Tables 4, 5 expt 3, 6 expts 1-3; sec. 6). Not independent data.
3. [!] **Pot volume / medium conflict**: Münch's Methods give 100 mL 0.5 M phosphate for all model pots; Hofmann 1998
   prints 50 mL "aqueous solution" for the furfural + H2S and C2 + C3 pots. The repo's fit row
   `hofmann1998_t3_fft_c5_h2s` uses 50 mL (20 mM). P(50 mL right) ~0.7 (table-specific footnotes vs one generic
   sentence that fits Table 4's 100 mL pots).
4. [!] Table 5 has **no pH**; Table 4 / text imply pH 5.0 is the comparison basis (P ~0.7 it was pH 5.0, as in Hofmann).
5. [!] Text "factors of 95 or 42" (Cys charged CYE vs LMW-YE I / II): Tables give 209 / 2.1 = 99.5 and 209 / 5 = 41.8.
   "About three times more cysteine" (CYE vs SPYA): 2.75 ✓. "10 times higher" Cys: 10.1 ✓. Amino-acid ratio claims
   (Orn 3.7, Pro 2.3, Phe 7.3 by mg reacted, Met 3.9) ✓. Table 5 expt 2 printed 0.12 % vs 0.114 [Z]; Table 6 expt 4
   0.007 vs 0.0081 [Z] — rounding-level.
6. [!] Extract labels flip between papers: here **I = fermented, II = fresh**; in `munch1998` Table 5 **I = fresh,
   II = fermented**.
7. [!] pH of heated extracts never measured; tap water (hardness unknown) as solvent; 1:5 w/v here vs 1 + 3 w/w in
   `munch1998` (so the 1998 SIDA pots are not the identical AEDA pots).
8. [!] Fermentation medium "under oxygen"; contains Cu²⁺ 1.35 g/L CuSO4 and Zn²⁺ — metals that could carry into the
   LMW fraction (not measured).

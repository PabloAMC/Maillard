## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

In the cysteine + glucose (+ C9 aldehyde) pots, **Cys-Amadori peaks at 10 min and is essentially gone
by 60 min**; the aldehydes lower that peak (M ≈ 34 > A ≈ B ≈ 25 > C ≈ D ≈ 15-16 g/L [F]).
**The aldehyde thiazolidines also peak early** — at 10 min for the two dienals (≈ 11-12 g/L) and at
15 min for nonanal and 2-nonenal (≈ 4.3-4.9 g/L) — fall to ≈ 1-1.7 g/L by 60 min, plateau to 120
min, and reach ≈ 0 at 180 min. The ranking (dienals > enal ≈ alkanal; double-bond position matters
little) is the paper's claim: unsaturation accelerates thiazolidine formation and inhibits
Cys-Amadori formation. **But the pots behind Figure 2 are not described in this paper** (charge,
temperature and volume are referred to "Du et al., 2019", a reference that is about youtiao), and on
the only plausible reading — the Du 2023 charge — the printed g/L are stoichiometrically impossible
(flag 1). Heating each purified thiazolidine (667 mmol/L, pH 6.5, 140 °C, 2 h) releases its parent
aldehyde and a large sulfur slate (trithiolanes/trithianes, 2-alkylthiophenes, alkylthiazoles,
2-alkylpyridines), semi-quantified at µg/L. **There is no TTCA (sugar-cysteine thiazolidine)
measurement and nothing from which a formation equilibrium constant can be computed.**

## 5. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

**The model's need.** The engine carries TTCA (the xylose-cysteine thiazolidine) only as a fed
reactant (`r_ttca_cys`: TTCA -> Cys + PENT; `r_ttca_deg`), with no formation equilibrium; Cao 2017
gives K_app = [TTCA]/([Cys][Xyl]) ≈ 60 L/mol at 90 °C, pH 4.2-4.7. The question was whether Du 2024
quantifies TTCA-type thiazolidines against time and conditions.

**Verdict: it does not.**
1. **Wrong thiazolidine.** The quantified thiazolidines are 2-alkyl(enyl)thiazolidine-4-carboxylic
   acids from **lipid aldehydes** (C9), not the sugar-cysteine 2-(polyhydroxyalkyl) thiazolidines
   (TTCA from xylose, its glucose analogue). The glucose-cysteine product measured is the
   **Cys-Amadori** compound; the paper does not report a glucose-derived thiazolidine.
2. **No reaction conditions.** The pots behind Figure 2 are referred to a citation that does not
   describe them (flag 2). Without [Cys]₀, [Glc]₀, [aldehyde]₀ and temperature, no K, no k_f, no
   k_r.
3. **The g/L axis fails a stoichiometry check** (flag 1).

**What survives (ordinal / shape only, if the axis is trusted in relative terms):**
- **Aldehyde-cysteine thiazolidines are transient** in a heated Cys + Glc pot: maximum at 10-15
  min, 60 % (A) to 90 % (D) below peak by 60 min, a plateau 60-120 min, ~0 at 180 min. Ratio peak : 120 min ≈ 3 (A, B)
  to ≈ 11 (C, D). If the engine ever adds a lipid-aldehyde + Cys reversible adduct (a Cys sink
  competing with Strecker/H2S release), the shape is a qualitative target: fast formation (<10 min),
  then decay as the adduct dissociates or degrades.
- **Dienals form the adduct faster and to ~2.5× higher peak than the 2-enal or the alkanal**;
  2,4- vs 2,6-dienal indistinguishable. Ordinal.
- **Every aldehyde lowers the Cys-Amadori peak** (M 34 -> A/B ~25 -> C/D ~15-16 [F]), i.e. the
  aldehydes compete with glucose for cysteine; by 30 min all systems converge (4-6 g/L). Ordinal,
  and consistent with Du 2023's suppression of sulfur volatiles by aldehydes.
- **Cys-Amadori is short-lived** at this (unstated) temperature: ~0 by 60-120 min. Contrast with
  Cao 2017, where at 90 °C TTCA isomerises to Cys-Amadori over hours.

**The volatile half (Table 1):** a 667 mmol/L thiazolidine heated 2 h at 140 °C releases the
parent aldehyde (R2 -> (E)-2-nonenal 167, R3 -> (E,E)-2,4-nonadienal 1528, R4 -> (E,Z)-2,6-
nonadienal 98 µg/L), i.e. the adduct is reversible on heating; and gives trithiolanes/trithianes
(acetaldehyde + H2S chemistry) as the dominant sulfur class in R1, R2 and R4. Semi-quantitative
(RF = 1 vs one IS) and at ~10⁻⁴ mol % of the charge [Z: 2-heptylthiophene 1627 µg/L = 8.9 µmol/L =
0.0013 mol % of R1]. Not a fit target.

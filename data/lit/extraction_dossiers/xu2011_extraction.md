| GC-MS | Agilent 6890N / 5975B; HP-5 (30 m × 0.25 mm × 0.25 µm); He 1.2 mL/min; 35 °C (5 min) → 3 °C/min → 130 °C → 5 °C/min → 220 °C (5 min); splitless 250 °C; EI 70 eV |

[Z] Molar charges: Cys 0.2 g / 121.16 = 1.65 mmol → **165 mmol/L**; Xyl 0.2 g / 150.13 = 1.33 mmol →

**4.73 µg/mL** of reaction mixture. Headspace 10 mL. Oxidised lard brings ≈ 0.1 g × 184 meq/kg =

### pH [M] (text p. 2244)

Final pH of the MRPs: **oxidised-lard pot 3.60, fresh-lard pot 3.65, "cysteine-ribose" pot 3.71**
(read: the Cys + Xyl control; "ribose" is a slip [!]). Initial pH 5.0 (buffer). Text: "a slight
decrease in pH during the reaction process was found in the lard-containing systems."

### Qualitative claims [Q]
- Lard suppresses most S-compounds; oxidised lard more than fresh. Authors attribute this to
  lipid carbonyls scavenging H₂S (after Elmore 2002, Mottram & Nobrega 2002).
- FFT formed from H₂S + furfural; furfural from the Amadori intermediates via 1,2-enolisation at
  low pH; furfural similar with fresh and oxidised lard.
- 2-Pentylthiophene (oxidised lard only) from H₂S + (2E,4E)-nonadienal / 2,4-decadienal.
- The Maillard system lowers lipid aldehydes (hexanal, heptanal, (2E)-heptenal,
  (2E,4E)-heptadienal absent) and raises alcohols, alkylfurans and acids (antioxidant MRPs).

| furfural | 0.43 | 0.39 |
| 2-methylthiophene | 0.25 | 0.21 |
| 3-methylthiophene | 0.096 | 0.047 |
| 2-methylfuran | 0.38 | 0.27 |

FFT / furfural: control 5.1, fresh lard 3.9, oxidised lard 2.0 (mass ratio) — FFT falls only a
little more than furfural, whereas MFT falls 10-20× more than any other species.

**Apparent molar yields, control (RF = 1 assumed by the authors):**
MFT 560.71 µg/mL / 114.17 = **4.91 mmol/L = 3.0 % of cysteine**; FFT 116.21 / 114.17 = **1.02 mmol/L =
0.62 % of cysteine, 0.77 % of xylose**; furfural 22.95 / 96.08 = 0.24 mmol/L = 0.18 % of xylose;
MFT : FFT = **4.8 (molar)**. **[!] These yields are implausible as absolute values**: 3 % MFT is
~100× de Roos 2005's Likens-Nickerson MFT yield (3.2 × 10⁻⁴ mol/mol Cys from Cys + Xyl) and far
above every calibrated Cys + pentose pot in the corpus. A DVB/CAR/PDMS fibre at 60 °C responds to a
thiol very differently from cyclohexanone; RF = 1 against a ketone IS with no thiol standard makes
every Table 1 number a **relative** response, not a concentration.

**Stoichiometry check on the oxidised-lard H₂S-scavenging story:** oxidised lard brings ~0.9 mmol/L
hydroperoxide and lipid aldehydes of order 0.1-1 mmol/L (Table 2 sum of aldehydes ≈ 300 µg/mL in
the lard-alone pot → ~2-3 mmol/L if RF = 1); the apparent MFT loss (4.9 mmol/L) is of the same order,
so the scavenging story is not excluded on stoichiometry but cannot be tested with RF = 1 numbers.

## 4. What this can and cannot anchor in the model

**Can (within-study ratios only):**
1. **Direction and rough size of a lipid suppression of the sulfur lane at 120 °C, pH 5 → 3.6:**
   MFT ÷21 (1 % fresh lard) / ÷143 (1 % oxidised lard, PV 184); FFT ÷3 / ÷6; furfural ÷2.3-2.5.
   Usable as a qualitative hold-out for any lipid-carbonyl × H₂S/thiol cross-term the engine might
   add (the sulfur and lipid lanes are currently separate). The ordering **MFT ≫ thiophenes > FFT ≈
   furfural** in sensitivity is the informative part.
2. **The pH crash**: 0.2 M phosphate at pH 5 does not hold pH against 165 mM cysteine + 133 mM
   xylose at 120 °C for 60 min: **5.0 → 3.71** (control). Useful for any pH-trajectory sanity check
   (cf. Cao 2017, 5.49 → 4.15 in 5 h at 90 °C).
3. **MFT : FFT ≈ 4.8 (molar)** in the control at 120 °C / 60 min — within-study, same fibre, same RF
   assumption for two isomeric thiols (MW identical, similar volatility), so this ratio is more
   defensible than either level. Compare de Roos 2005 Table I (≈ 11 at pH 5, open boiling SDE) and
   Cao 2017 Table 1 relative areas.

**Cannot:**
- **No absolute level**: RF = 1 against cyclohexanone; no thiol calibration; values imply
  physically implausible 3 % MFT yields.
- **No kinetics**: one time point (60 min), one temperature.
- **No TTCA/Amadori, no free Cys, no H₂S, no dicarbonyls.**
- **Matrix artefact not separable from chemistry:** 1 % lard is a separate hydrophobic phase at the
  60 °C SPME step; lipophilic volatiles (thiophenes, MFT, 2-pentylfuran) partition into the fat and
  out of the headspace. Part of the "suppression" may be headspace partitioning, not reaction. The
  paper has no spike-recovery control with lard present.
- Lipid aldehyde levels (Table 2) are from a 0.1 g lard aliquot heated in buffer, RF = 1, and do
  not anchor the lipid lane's absolute hexanal.

## 5. Flags

1. **RF = 1 semi-quantification** for all compounds against cyclohexanone; no sulfur standards;
   MFT/FFT identified by MS + literature RI only.
2. **Absolute values implausible** (MFT 3 % of Cys) — treat every µg/mL as relative.
3. **"Cysteine-ribose" in the pH sentence** refers to the Cys + Xyl control (slip).
4. **(2E)-Octenal, Xyl-Cys-fresh lard cell is blank on the page** (neither value nor "n.d.").
5. Cys : Xyl is 1.24 : 1 by mole (equal masses), not equimolar.
6. "2-Formyl-2,3-dihydrothiophene" at RI 1836 and "2-thiophenemethanol" at 1956 on HP-5 are
   implausibly high RIs for these structures (both should elute near 1000-1100) — identities or
   RIs are suspect [!].
7. Single pot each, unstirred autoclave; come-up time not stated.

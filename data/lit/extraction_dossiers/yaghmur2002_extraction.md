### Qualitative claims [Q]
- In water, FFT: "only traces were detected"; FFT is "enhanced in the O/W microemulsion with
  increasing aqueous phase content".
- **Main product in O/W: 2-(2-furanyl)thiazolidine (B)**, the furfural-cysteine thiazolidine
  (direct aldehyde + cysteine condensation; the furfural analogue of TTCA). A, C, D form slowly
  and rise "slightly with time".
- B may react with mercaptoacetaldehyde (from Cys Strecker degradation) to give D (citing
  Vauthey 2000).
- Pseudophase model: reaction at the interfacial film; V₀ rises with α (interfacial furfural).
- O/W (Tween 60) favours B; sucrose-laurate W/O (Fanun 2001) favours D.

## 3. Derived quantities [Z]

**Pseudo-first-order furfural loss from Fig. 4** (k = −ln(1 − X)/t):

| aqueous wt % | 1 h | 2 h | 4 h | 8 h | carry |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 90 (W/PG 1:1) | 0.60 | 0.52 | 0.60 | 0.58 | **≈ 0.58 h⁻¹** |
| 60 (W/PG 1:1) | 0.33 | 0.32 | 0.26 | 0.29 | **≈ 0.30 h⁻¹** |

**Units of V₀ [!].** With [FUR]₀ ≈ 0.14 mol/kg (90 %) and 0.09 mol/kg (60 %), k·[FUR]₀ ≈ 2.2 × 10⁻⁵
and 7.5 × 10⁻⁶ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹, against plotted values 11.7 and 6.5. The plotted numbers are therefore
consistent with **V₀ ≈ (plotted) × 10⁻⁶ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹** (within a factor ~2), **not** with the
literal label (plotted × 10⁻³ or ÷ 10³ → 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹, which would consume the furfural in under a
minute).

**Cross-check with Yaghmur 2005 Table 1** (O/W, 60 wt % aqueous, water/PG 2/1, 65 °C: k_obs = 0.486
h⁻¹): this paper's 60 wt %, W/PG 1:1 k ≈ 0.30 h⁻¹, scaled by the Fig. 6 2/1 : 1/1 V₀ ratio (8.0/6.5
= 1.23) → **≈ 0.37 h⁻¹**, i.e. within ×1.3 of the 2005 value. Against the 2005 **water** arm (0.126
h⁻¹ at 65 °C), the microemulsion accelerates furfural loss by **×2.4 (60 wt %) to ×4.6 (90 wt %)**.

**Product ratios.** FFT/B ≤ 0.09 at 8 h even at 90 wt % aqueous; B dominates every pot. If B were
~all of the converted furfural at 8 h (≈ 99 % conversion at 90 wt %), FFT would be ≤ ~9 % of
furfural (peak-area basis) — consistent with Yaghmur 2005's printed 4.9-7.3 % FFT conversion at 15 h.

## 4. What this can and cannot anchor in the model

**Model context.** `parameters_sulfur.py` carries `YAGHMUR_FFT_SHARE_CEILING = 0.012` (from the
2005 water arm) and `r_fur_fft`, `r_fur_fft_hs`, `r_fur_decay` in `sulfur.py`.

**Can:**
1. **Identify the dominant furfural sink with cysteine at pH 5: the thiazolidine
   2-(2-furanyl)thiazolidine (B)**, i.e. FUR + Cys ⇌ furanyl-thiazolidine, the same chemistry as
   Cys + PENT ⇌ TTCA. In the O/W media B is "the main reaction product" and FFT/B ≤ 0.09. The 2005
   dossier concluded the water-arm furfural loss is "a sink the paper never identifies"; this paper
   names it (for the microemulsion; the 2005 paper says that in water the main *sulfur* compound is
   A = FFT, so the water sink is still unassigned). Supports `r_fur_decay` being, in part, a
   **cysteine-dependent** sink (thiazolidine formation), which would make it second-order in Cys —
   consistent with 2005's k_obs linear in [Cys]₀.
2. **Matrix-acceleration band** for FUR loss: ×2.4-4.6 over water at 65 °C, 60-90 wt % aqueous;
   ×1.5-1.9 from raising water/PG 1:1 → 3:1. Hold-out only; the engine has no microemulsion.
3. **Time courses** (Fig. 4) as a hold-out for any FUR + Cys sink rate at 65 °C, pH 5.0, in a
   matrix — with the caveat that they are microemulsion data.

**Cannot:**
- **No FFT yield** (only peak-area ratios to B) and **no water-arm numbers** — the 1.2 % ceiling
  stays sourced from the 2005 paper. This paper's "only traces in water" is qualitatively
  consistent with it.
- No temperature dependence, no H₂S, no cysteine measurement, no pH trajectory.
- V₀ units are mislabelled (Flag 1).
- The "conversion of furfural to sulfur-containing flavors" in the text is in fact **furfural
  disappearance** (Fig. 4 y-axis), not product formation.

## 5. Flags

1. **V₀ axis label "(moles/(L*sec))*10^3"** is inconsistent with the Fig. 4 kinetics by ~10³; the
   numbers fit V₀ ≈ plotted × 10⁻⁶ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹.
2. **Fig. 5 reaction time not stated**; it matches Fig. 4 at 4 h.
3. **Tween 20 in text vs Tween 80 in Fig. 7.**
4. Compound C is "2-(2-furanyl)thiazoline" in the abstract and Table 1 but "2-(2-furanyl)thiazole"
   in one text sentence.
5. Table 1 ratio basis (FID area? mole?) not stated; no response factors.
6. All identifications are **tentative** (library + literature).
7. The paper's mass bookkeeping (total mass per pot) is never printed; my concentrations rest on
   reading "buffer 10 g" as the water part of a 1:1 water/PG aqueous phase.

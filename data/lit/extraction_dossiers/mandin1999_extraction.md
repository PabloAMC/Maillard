# Mandin, Duckham & Ames 1999 — EXTRACTION
### *"Volatile Compounds from Potato-like Model Systems"*
### Water 400 mL with glucose 0.0721 M, L-methionine 0.1415 M, linoleic acid 0.5 g/L and potato starch 50 g/L in nine combinations, refluxed 100-105 °C for 2 h at pH held at 6 inside a modified Likens-Nickerson SDE (volatiles stripped as they form). One end point, µg recovered per 400 mL (internal standard in the solvent, RF assumed 1). Methional only with glucose (36 µg), ×3-4 with linoleic acid (106-145 µg); DMDS/DMTS ×13-19 in the four-component pots; methionine suppresses 2,4-decadienal ÷4-5. Read 2026-10-28.

## 1. Methods [M]

| item | as printed |
|---|---|
| Reagents | α-D-glucose 99+ % (ACS), L-methionine 98+ %, potato starch (soluble) — Aldrich; linoleic acid (cis-9,cis-12) 99+ % — Sigma; redistilled pentane, diethyl ether |
| Composition | **water 400 mL**; **glucose 13 g/L (0.0721 mol/L)**, **methionine 20 g/L (0.1415 mol/L)**, **linoleic acid 0.5 g/L**, **starch 50 g/L**; combinations per Table 1; blank = water |
| Rationale | ≈ raw-potato totals: sugars 1.3 g/100 g, amino acids 1.909 g/100 g, fatty acids ~0.06 g/100 g; starch ≈ one-third of raw potato (14.8-16.6 g/100 g), the maximum without charring |
| Mixing | linoleic-acid pots homogenised with an Ultra-Turrax T25 before heating; starch added once boiling |
| Heating / extraction | **refluxed 100-105 °C for 2 h** in a 1 L round-bottom flask of a **modified Likens-Nickerson SDE**; solvent **pentane : ether 9 : 1, 30 mL**, containing **0.1 mL of 1,2-dichlorobenzene in ether (130.6 mg/L) = 13.1 µg IS** [Z] |
| pH | before heating **6.3** (blank and G 5.3; S 7.0); monitored with an autoclavable electrode and **held at pH 6** with 0.5 M NaOH/HCl during heating |
| Work-up | water frozen out (−18 °C, 18 h); Kuderna-Danish to 0.5-1.0 mL at 45 °C; GC-MS within 1 week |
| GC-MS | HP 5890 II / 5972; BPX5 50 m × 0.32 mm × 0.25 µm; He 1.0 mL/min; 50 °C (5 min) → 4 °C/min → 250 °C (5 min); EI 70 eV, 35-400 amu; LRI vs C6-C22 alkanes |
| Quantification | against 1,2-dichlorobenzene; **no response factors stated** (RF = 1 implied); **IS added to the solvent, not to the sample**, so SDE recovery is not corrected |
| Replicates | triplicate model systems; RSD **2-25 %** (Table 2 footnote f) |

[Z] Molar charges per 400 mL: methionine **56.6 mmol**, glucose **28.8 mmol**, linoleic acid 0.20 g =
**0.713 mmol** (MW 280.45), starch 20 g.

### Text claims [Q/M]
- DMDS/DMTS: "2-6-fold higher in M/G and L/M" than M; "increased greatly, by 13-19-fold, in L/M/G
  and L/M/G/S"; DMDS second only to methional in L/M/G(/S).
- **Methional absent from M** → "the Shigematsu reaction did not occur" (no methionine-only
  methional at 100-105 °C, pH 6); methional is "the major component" of M/G, L/M/G, L/M/G/S;
  its rise with linoleic acid is attributed to more α-dicarbonyls.
- Hexanal "relatively similar levels (15-28 µg)" in all linoleic pots; 2,4-decadienal 30-119 µg;
  2-pentylfuran nd-18 µg. Lowest decadienal in L/M (methionine either promotes its degradation to
  2-pentylfuran or binds it non-volatilely).
- No pyrazines, pyridines, thiazoles, oxazoles, thiophenes (except 2-hexylthiophene) or HMF: low
  temperature / high moisture / pH 6. Glucose alone gives nothing at 100 °C.
- Starch: no binding of volatiles; acts as extra reactive carbohydrate (hydrolysis).

## 4. What this can and cannot anchor in the model

**Model context.** The methionine lane (`parameters_methionine.py`) carries Strecker formation of
methional from methionine + dicarbonyl, retro-Michael release of methanethiol from methional, and
MeSH → DMDS (Ea 81 kJ/mol, Pan 2025); the lipid lane carries hexanal etc. separately.

**Can (qualitative / within-study ratios, ~100 °C, pH 6):**
1. **No methional from methionine alone at 100-105 °C, pH 6, 2 h** — a clean null: the Strecker
   route needs a carbonyl partner; any thermal Met → methional channel must be negligible at
   100 °C (contrast Yu & Ho 1995 at 180 °C).
2. **DMDS/DMTS from methionine alone (M: 3 + 1 µg)** — a small but non-zero sugar-independent
   MeSH source at 100 °C (or impurity/oxidation); glucose doubles it.
3. **Lipid amplifies the methionine lane** (methional ×2.9; DMDS ×6.5 vs M/G) — a hold-out direction
   for any cross-term in which lipid-oxidation carbonyls act as Strecker partners or oxidants.
   The engine's lanes are separate, so today it would predict ×1.
4. **Methionine depletes 2,4-decadienal** (÷4) but not hexanal — a lipid-lane × amino-acid
   cross-term direction (decadienal is an α,β,γ,δ-unsaturated aldehyde, Michael acceptor).
5. **The methional : polysulfide sulfur split** at 100 °C (≈ 61 : 39 in M/G) — weakly usable as a
   within-pot ratio, subject to differential SDE recovery (methional is less steam-volatile and more
   water-soluble than DMDS).

**Cannot:**
- **No absolute formation yields**: SDE recovery uncorrected (IS in the solvent), RF = 1, no
  calibration; continuous stripping removes products from the pot as they form (no standing
  concentrations, no secondary losses).
- **No kinetics**: one 2 h end point, one temperature.
- No dicarbonyl, no methionine or glucose consumption, no MeSH (too volatile/lost in KD).
- Glucose, not a pentose; no cysteine (irrelevant to the TTCA/sulfur-lane routing question).
- Linoleic acid as free fatty acid (not triglyceride or phospholipid), at 0.5 g/L, homogenised —
  oxidation state at t = 0 not characterised.

## 5. Flags

1. **Quantities are "recovered", not formed**: 2 h SDE, IS in solvent, RF = 1, RSD up to 25 %.
2. **Text "2,4-decadienal (30-119 µg)"** — the table's E,Z + E,E sum gives 20 (L/M) to 119 (L/G) µg;
   the lower bound in the text does not match L/M (20 µg) [!].
3. Methional's experimental LRI 924 vs reference 909 and 2,4-decadienal (E,E) 1334 vs 1318 —
   larger offsets than for other compounds; identities accepted (authentic standards) but note.
4. pH held at 6 by titration during heating — the amount of base/acid added is not reported.
5. 2-Hexylthiophene in a methionine (not cysteine) system implies H₂S from methionine at 100 °C;
   1 µg, tentative.

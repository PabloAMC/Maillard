## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

4 mmol xylose + 4 mmol cysteine in 50 mL phosphate pH 5.5 (**80 + 80 mmol/L** [Z]), 120 °C for 60 min.
**Phospholipid lowers MFT**: 15.04 µg/kg (no lipid) → 8.95 (lecithin 0.24 %, −40 %), 5.36 (cephalin
0.07 %, −64 %), 6.40 (both 0.24 %, −57 %) [Z]. **FFT does not follow**: 153.59 → 176.73 (+15 %), 106.79
(−30 %), 205.14 (+34 %), with overlapping letters (abc / ab / c / a). Lecithin brings 2-pentylfuran
(49 µg/kg) and a lipid-carbon thiophene, "2-(4-methylpentyl)thiophene" (221 µg/kg, vs 4.8 without
lipid). "Reheating" (5 days at 4 °C, then boiling 30 min) **lowers MFT in three of four groups (−18 to
−39 %)** and FFT in three of four (−43 % without lipid), raises decanal in all four, and lowers the sensory
"meat-like" score. Molar MFT yield is tiny: **~1.6 × 10⁻⁶ mol/mol cysteine** [Z]. Quantification is
IS-ratio with RF = 1, analytes in µg/kg of reaction liquid.

## 4. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

**Model context.** Cys + pentose → MFT/FFT is the sulfur lane's core; the lipid lane is separate; no
phospholipid species, no lipid-aldehyde + H₂S or + Cys sink. Nothing here was run against the model.

**(1) Lipid × sulfur direction test (usable, semi-quantitative).** At 80 mM Cys + 80 mM xylose, pH 5.5,
120 °C, 60 min, **phospholipid at 0.07–0.24 % lowers MFT by 40–64 %** (letters a vs bc/cd). FFT is not
consistently lowered (−30 % to +34 %). Same direction for MFT as `zhao2019b_extraction.md` (fat −38 %,
oxidised fat −65 %, GSH + glucose), opposite to `du2025_extraction.md` (free C18 acids +19–30 %). A model
coupling of lipid aldehydes to the sulfur lane would be expected to suppress MFT at ~0.1–0.2 % phospholipid
by a factor 1.5–3; that is the size of the effect, not a rate. Cephalin (PE, a primary amine) at 0.07 %
suppresses MFT more than lecithin (PC) at 0.24 % — compatible with PE's amine competing for carbonyls, a
chemistry the model does not carry.

**(2) Lipid-carbon thiophenes as an H₂S/Cys sink (qualitative).** Lecithin brings
2-(4-methylpentyl)thiophene to ~221 µg/kg, ~25 × the MFT in that pot and ~1.2 × the FFT. Identity is a
library match without reference RI [!]; the magnitude says lipid-carbon S-heterocycles can rival the meaty
thiols at 0.24 % phospholipid.

**(3) Reheating / storage (direction only).** 5 days at 4 °C + 30 min boiling lowers MFT in A, B and D
(−18 to −39 %) and FFT in A, B, D (−16 to −43 %); C goes the other way. This is a loss-on-storage +
re-boil direction, consistent with thiol oxidation/volatilisation; storage atmosphere and vessel are not
stated, so it cannot calibrate a thiol loss rate.

**(4) Yield level (weak external check).** MFT ~1.6 × 10⁻⁶ and FFT ~1.7 × 10⁻⁵ mol/mol Cys at 120 °C,
60 min, pH 5.5, measured after cooling + 24 h at 4 °C (or longer) — an IS-ratio, RF = 1 value. It is a

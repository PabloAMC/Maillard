# Hincelin, Ames, Apriyantono & Elmore 1992 — EXTRACTION
### *"The effect of xylose on the generation of volatiles from heated thiamine"*
### Thiamine (0.05 mol) ± xylose (1 mol) in 250 mL water, held at pH 7 with NaOH, boiled 1 h in a Likens–Nickerson steam-distillation/extraction head. Adding xylose raises 2-methyl-3-furanthiol from 2 % to 14 % of the isolate, a 4–5-fold rise in absolute yield; H2S doubles (1 % → 3 %). Relative peak areas only, one temperature, one time, open (stripping) system. Read 2026-10-28.

## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

Three systems, each **heated 1 h in a modified Likens–Nickerson apparatus** (continuous steam
distillation–solvent extraction into 15 mL diethyl ether, so volatiles are **stripped out as they
form**): **xylose alone, thiamine alone, and thiamine HCl 0.05 mol + xylose 1 mol in 250 mL
water held at pH 7** (~71 mL 3 M NaOH added before and during heating). Relative abundances (RA,
% of total isolate peak area, blank-corrected) are given per isolate. Isolate strengths: thiamine
alone ≈ **1.5×** the xylose/thiamine isolate; xylose alone ≈ **0.25×**. **2-Methylfuran-3-thiol:
RA 2 (thiamine) vs 14 (xylose/thiamine), "a 4–5-fold increase in absolute yield".** H2S: RA 1 vs 3.
2-Methyl-4,5-dihydrothiophen dominates the thiamine isolate (RA 57; 27 with xylose).
2-Methyltetrahydrothiophen-3-one falls from 12 to 2. Thirteen compounds are new thiamine
products, including 2-methylthiophen-3-thiol, 2-methylthiophen-4-thiol and their dihydro
analogues; 16 compounds are attributed to xylose–thiamine interaction (15 with sulfur), among them
bis(2-methyl-3-furyl) trisulfide, 2-methyl-3-(methyldithio)furan, kahweofuran, trithiolanes and
lenthionine. The authors propose that the extra MFT comes from **4-hydroxy-5-methyl-3(2H)-furanone
(norfuraneol, from xylose) + H2S (from thiamine)**, but neither norfuraneol nor thiamine loss
was measured. **No concentrations, no kinetics, one temperature (~100 °C, boiling), one time.**

±50 % or more.

- **Sulfur shift with xylose:** the thiamine isolate is ~81 % (by RA) the thiophen/thiophenone
  skeleton (19 + 28 + 38 + 54 + 56 + 61 + 12 + 33 + 42 = 57 + 12 + 3 + 3 + 1 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 1); with
  xylose the same set is RA ~34 against 1.5 × 81 ≈ 122, a ~3.6× fall in absolute terms while MFT, H2S, dithiols (8, 10), trithiolanes and polysulfides rise. Read
  qualitatively: xylose diverts thiamine-derived sulfur (or the HMP-type C5 fragment) from
  thiophens to furanthiol and H2S-derived species.
- **No yield per mol thiamine** can be formed (no internal standard, no thiamine loss measured).

## 5. WHAT THE MODEL CAN TAKE FROM IT

**Context.** THI → HMP (`r_thi_hmp`) → MFT; THI → MeSH (`r_thi_mesh`); THI → H2S as the B50 share
(0.39 at pH 7, ~100 °C, Dwivedi & Arnold 1971); pentoses → norfuraneol; norfuraneol + H2S → MFT
(furanic edge). A pentose + thiamine pot at pH 7 therefore makes MFT by two routes in the model.

**Verdict: (c) qualitative, with one directional ratio.**
- **G5 (thiamine degradation with H2S at ≥ 2 temperatures): NO.** One temperature (boiling, ~100 °C,
  not printed), one time, H2S only as RA of an ether isolate (H2S is poorly retained in 15 mL ether
  under continuous extraction), thiamine loss not measured. It confirms H2S formation from thiamine
  alone at pH 7 (if the thiamine-only run was held at pH 7, which is not stated), consistent in
  direction with B50, and **cannot check the 0.39 share**.
- **G1 (thiol loss kinetics): NO.** Continuous stripping removes MFT from the pot as it forms, which
  is the opposite of a thiol-loss experiment.
- **Directional check the model can be held to:** at pH 7, ~100 °C, 1 h, xylose:thiamine 20:1,
  **MFT(xylose + thiamine) / MFT(thiamine) ≈ 4.7** and **H2S ratio ≈ 2**, both on extraction-
  integrated amounts. If the model's pentose + thiamine pot shows MFT enhancement of ~1 (no
  norfuraneol × thiamine-H2S coupling) or ≫ 10, that is a qualitative miss. Because SDE strips
  volatiles continuously and the loadings are molar, this is **not a fit target** and the size of
  the ratio is not transferable to a closed pot.
- **Structural gaps it points to, qualitative only:** the thiophen/thiophenone family (2-methyl-
  4,5-dihydrothiophen RA 57, 2-methyltetrahydrothiophen-3-one RA 12) is the dominant volatile
  sulfur sink of thiamine at pH 7 here, and the model has no such species; its decrease with
  xylose is consistent with xylose competing for the C5 sulfur fragment or for H2S.

**Not usable:** RA as concentrations; any ratio from "tr" entries; odour descriptions.

**Caveats.** Very high loadings (0.2 M thiamine, ~3–4 M xylose); pH held by NaOH only in the
mixture as described; open system with continuous extraction; purity of thiamine HCl 90–95 %;
blank-corrected RA without response factors; temperature not printed.

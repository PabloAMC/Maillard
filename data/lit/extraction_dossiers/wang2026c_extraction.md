## 1. Methods

- **Raw material (§2.1, p. 2).** Soy protein isolate (Shansong Biological Products, Linyi): 91.6 % protein,
  4.56 % moisture. Nothing else in the feed (no oil, sugar or flavouring is stated).
- **Extrusion (§2.2-2.3, p. 2).** AHT36-32D twin-screw extruder: screw speed 420 rpm, screw diameter 36 mm,
  L/D 32:1, cooling module 60 °C. **Feed rate is not stated.**
  - *Moisture series:* seven barrel zones at 70, 80, 120, 140, 150, 160 and 140 °C; moisture 40, 50, 60, 70
    and 80 % (HMMA-40% to HMMA-80%).
  - *Temperature series:* moisture 70 %; the first six zones at 70, 80, 120, 140, 150 and 160 °C; the last
    zone at 120, 130, 140, 150, 160, 170 or 180 °C (HMMA-120 °C to HMMA-180 °C).
  - Extrudates were stored at -18 °C.
  - **The sample used for AEDA, quantitation, recombination and omission is HMMA at 70 % moisture and
    160 °C** (§3.5.1, p. 16).
- **Isolation (§2.8).** Dichloromethane extraction, 25 µL of 2-methyl-3-heptanone (7.31 mg/mL) as internal
  standard, SAFE at 50 °C, concentrated to 1 mL; triplicate.
- **GC-MS (§2.9).** TG-Wax column, ISQ single quadrupole, EI 70 eV, m/z 40-550. Identification (§2.11) was
  by MS (NIST 20), RI (C6-C28 alkanes) and authentic standards; the "Identification" column of Tables 2-3
  marks each compound MS, RI or S.
- **GC-O and AEDA (§2.10).** Sniff port (ODP3) at a 1:1 split. Extract diluted stepwise 1:2 in
  dichloromethane up to 1:8192. **Three trained panellists**; an odour counted as active only if at least 2
  of the 3 detected it.
- **Quantitation (§2.11, Table 5).** Standard curves of the area ratio to 2-methyl-3-heptanone, in SIM mode.
  R² ranges from 0.9914 to 0.9999. **The matrix of the calibration (model matrix or solvent) is not stated.**
- **OAVs (§2.12, Table 5).** Concentration divided by "aroma thresholds in water". The Table 5 column
  header gives the threshold unit as **µg/kg**; the footnote says "Odor thresholds in water were from
  reference (Van Gemert, 2018)" and "/" means not found.
- **Sensory (§2.7).** **15 trained judges** (6 males, 9 females), all with QDA experience, at 25 °C. Eight
  descriptors: bean-like, beany, sweet, milky, grassy, fatty, cereal, fermented. Each was scored on a
  9-point scale (1-3 weak, 4-6 medium, 7-9 strong), sniffing only.
- **Recombination and omission (§2.13, p. 3).** All aroma-active compounds were mixed into an "HMMA mock
  matrix (water)" at their measured concentrations. Recombinate and HMMA were compared by descriptive
  analysis. Omission models each removed **one** compound, tested by **triangle test** with the same panel.
- **Statistics (§2.14).** Means of triplicates ± SD; one-way ANOVA with Duncan's test (P < 0.05).

## 2. Findings that matter

### 2a. Extrusion conditions and the off-flavour load (§3.1-3.2; Figs. 1D, 3D)

- **Moisture series.** Total volatiles were highest at HMMA-60% (27,812.64 ± 2293.44 µg/kg) and lowest at
  HMMA-80% (20,406.59 ± 971.9 µg/kg) (p. 4). HMMA-70% had the lowest total and proportion of the eleven
  tracked off-flavour compounds (p. 8). The **off-flavour proportion is printed on Fig. 1D** as 7.46, 7.36,
  7.34, **4.87** and 6.81 % for 40 to 80 % moisture. The totals, read from graph, approx., are about 1640,
  1750, 2040, 1340 and 1390 µg/kg.
- **Temperature series.** The off-flavour proportion **printed on Fig. 3D** is 6.88, 6.8, 8.04, 6.01,
  **5.16**, 7.71 and 7.35 % for 120 to 180 °C. The totals, read from graph, approx., are about 2150, 1830,
  2370, 1790, **900**, 1290 and 1400 µg/kg. Lipid oxidation peaked at 140 °C, where hexanal is highest
  (Table 3: 1566.78 ± 82.75 µg/kg). At 160 °C, Table 3 gives:
  - hexanal 577.8 ± 45.28 µg/kg
  - (E,E)-2,4-decadienal 8.89 ± 0.96
  - maltol 4235.27 ± 244.27
  - 2-furanmethanol 1663.15 ± 66.79
  - furaneol 14.15 ± 0.97
  - hydroxyacetone 240.16 ± 6.82
  - 2-heptanone 113.91 ± 13.92
- 160 °C was chosen as the balance of fibre structure (Fig. 4 SEM) and off-flavour (p. 14, 16). The
  abstract states it as "70% moisture content and 160 °C extrusion temperature … yielded the lowest total
  volatile off-flavor compounds". The texture data are in Tables S1-S2, not on disk.
- **SPI vs HMMA (p. 15-16).** 98 compounds in HMMA, 63 of them absent from SPI. Maltol rose from 21.62 µg/kg
  in SPI to 4235.27 µg/kg ("over 195-times"; derived here 4235.27/21.62 = 196). Hexanal rose from 22.7 to
  577.8 µg/kg ("approximately 25-times"; derived 25.5). 2-Furanmethanol, furaneol and ethylpyrazine were
  absent from SPI. The authors' reading: the key odorants are process-generated.

Checks and inconsistencies (derived here):
- The printed OAVs reproduce to rounding, e.g. 14,147.30/4.5 = 3143.8 and 5.18/0.1 = 51.8.
- **The text says "14 compounds were above their reported odor thresholds"** (p. 16). Table 5 prints

- The text's "furfural (sweat, burnt, OAV 7)" (p. 16) matches Table 5 row 30, **2-furanmethanol**.
  Furfural is not in Table 5.
- **Table 5 concentrations differ from the Table 3 (160 °C column) concentrations of the same sample by
  0.6× to 24×** (Table 5 ÷ Table 3):
  - hexanal 14,147.30 / 577.8 = 24.5
  - 2-furanmethanol 13,036.63 / 1663.15 = 7.8
  - furaneol 67.58 / 14.15 = 4.8
  - maltol 16,525.44 / 4235.27 = 3.9
  - hydroxyacetone 930.80 / 240.16 = 3.9
  - 2-heptanone 243.71 / 113.91 = 2.1
  - vanillin 278.91 / 209.1 = 1.3
  - (E,E)-2,4-decadienal 5.18 / 8.89 = 0.58

- Furaneol has the highest FD (8192) but an OAV of 3. The authors attribute its non-significant omission to

## 3. What it means for the model and for formulation

**Target profile.** A plain soy-isolate HMMA, with no added fat, sugar or flavouring, extruded at 70 %
moisture and 160 °C, smells sweet/caramel and cereal (furaneol, maltol, ethylpyrazine and the furanones)
over a beany/grassy base. The base is driven by **hexanal** (OAV 3144, the only odorant whose omission was
significant at OAV ≫ 1), 1-octen-3-ol, (E,E)-2,4-decadienal and 2-pentylfuran. **It has no meaty sulfur
odorant at all** (§2b).

Read against the beef targets (Christlbauer 2011 for stew, Thong 2024 for burgers), the implied work on a
soy extrudate is:
1. **Suppress the soy lipid-oxidation set**, hexanal first, then 1-octen-3-ol, 2-pentylfuran and
   valeraldehyde. Process choice moves it about twofold: off-flavour totals of roughly 900 to 2370 µg/kg
   across the temperature series (read from graph).
2. **Add the sulfur/meaty layer**, which extrusion of SPI alone does not make here: MFT, FFT, methional
   and DMTS. That means precursor dosing (cysteine, thiamine, ribose) or flavouring.
3. Keep the caramel/sweet furanones in proportion. They are the extrudate's own Maillard signature, not a
   meat note.

The single-omission results (§2e) say the panel noticed hexanal and several low-OAV
sweet/milky compounds. That is a soy-extrudate sensory map, not evidence about meatiness.

**What the kinetic model emits** (grep of `src/kinetic_core/species*.py`, 2026-10-09):
- *Emitted as species:*
  - furaneol (`DMHF`)
  - hydroxyacetone (`ACETOL`, an intermediate of the proline/2-acetyl-1-pyrroline chain)
  - hexanal, trans,trans-2,4-decadienal and 2-pentylfuran (lipid lane, from linoleate hydroperoxides)
  - acetic acid (`AA`)
  - furfural (`FUR`) and HMF
  - 2-methylpyrazine and 2,5-dimethylpyrazine (not ethylpyrazine)
- *Not emitted:*
  - maltol, 2-furanmethanol, (5-methyl-2-furyl)methanol, (±)-3-hydroxy-γ-butyrolactone, γ-butyrolactone,
    2(5H)-furanone, 5-ethyldihydro-2(3H)-furanone, the pyranone (DDMP)
  - ethylpyrazine
  - 1-octen-3-ol, 3,5-octadien-2-one, 1-pentanol
  - valeraldehyde (pentanal) and 2-heptanone: carried only as threshold and protein-binding records in
    `matrix_oav.py` / `parameters_matrix.py`, not formed by any lane
  - 1-hexanol (in the live `engine.unrepresented_compounds` of
    `results/validation/core_prediction_uncertainty.json`)
  - vanillin, the phenols, 2-methylnaphthalene and the lactones

The model's sulfur lane (MFT, FFT, methional, disulfides) has nothing to compare against in this paper,
because no sulfur odorant was detected. Its lipid lane assumes a fat substrate, while this extrudate's lipid
is only the residual lipid of the SPI (not quantified).

## What it does not give

- Feed rate, specific mechanical energy, die pressure, residence time, or product temperature at the die.
- Lipid, sugar, cysteine, methionine or thiamine content of the SPI: the precursor side is absent.
- The matrix of the calibration curves, and an explanation of the 0.6× to 24× gap between Table 3 and
  Table 5 concentrations for the same sample.
- Triangle-test counts, or the number of judges per omission test.
- Any meat comparison: no beef reference was run, and the sensory vocabulary has no "meaty" descriptor.
- Thresholds in a protein or extrudate matrix: all are water values from a compilation, and many are
  missing ("/").
- Texture numbers (Tables S1-S2, not on disk).

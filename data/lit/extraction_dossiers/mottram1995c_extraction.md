## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

On a cellulose carrier at 185 °C, sulfur heterocycles from cysteine self-degradation (3,5-dimethyl-1,2,4-
trithiolanes, 3-methyl-1,2,4-trithiane, tetrathianes, thienothiophenes, alkylthiazoles) dominate, as in
the companion low-moisture paper. **The cellulose is not inert:** heated alone it gives 2-furfural,
2-acetylfuran, 5-methylfurfural, 1-(2-furanyl)butan-2-one and 2,2′-methylenebisfuran, and "in the presence
of cellulose, considerable cysteine degradation occurred" — cellulose + cysteine (no ribose) gives
**++++** trithiolanes, more than cellulose + cysteine + ribose. Dihydro-2-methyl-3(2H)-thiophenone appears
only with cellulose (attributed to bound water). The three methylthio-furans/thiophenes appear only with
lecithin (methanethiol proposed from H2S + choline). Excess lecithin "had an overall quenching effect".
**Furanthiols:** "In the latter [aqueous 140 °C] systems, the volatiles were dominated by thiols and
thiophenones, which were absent from the dry systems reported in this present work or were only found in
trace quantities." Neither MFT nor FFT is listed in Table 1.

## 2. METHODS [M]

| item | as printed |
|---|---|
| Carrier mix | "Cysteine (**40 mg**) and ribose (**36 mg**) were dissolved in 0.5 M phosphate buffer (**2 mL**), and the solution was added to water (**8 mL**)"; pH measured **5.8**; dry cellulose powder (**2 g**, pre-dried 108 °C / 5 days) added, 10 min at 20 °C; water removed under vacuum at 60 °C (rotary evaporator), heat + vacuum 1 h more; ground; **500 mg portions** into tubes |
| [Z] stoichiometry | Cys 0.330 mmol, ribose 0.240 mmol → **Cys:R 1.38:1, not "equimolar"** as the abstract says [!]. Solids ≈ 2.0 g cellulose + 0.076 g reactants + ~0.13 g phosphate salts ≈ 2.2 g, so a 500 mg portion holds ≈ 9 mg Cys (75 µmol) and ≈ 8 mg ribose (55 µmol) |

## 4. DERIVED [Z]

- **MFT/FFT:** absent from the table; their disulfides are tr–"+" (< 0.65 to 6.5 ng-equivalent per
  ~9 mg Cys portion). [Z] upper bound on total MFT (as disulfide) ≲ 6.5 ng / 75 µmol Cys ≈ < 4 × 10⁻⁷
  mol/mol Cys — at least two orders of magnitude below the aqueous Hofmann 145 °C value (5 × 10⁻⁵), same
  caveats as `mottram1995b` §4.
- **Cellulose + cysteine alone ≥ cellulose + cysteine + ribose** for trithiolanes (++++ vs ++) and
  thienothiophene (+++ vs ++): ribose diverts cysteine away from the polysulfur heterocycles even dry.

## 5. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

- **Cannot:** any MFT/FFT level, rate, or yield; no water-content series (one "dry on cellulose" state and
  one "neat" state, plus possible bound water); no temperature series.
- **Can (qualitative):** (i) in non-aqueous roasting conditions the furanthiol channel is essentially off;
  (ii) a polysaccharide carrier is itself a furan source (furfural, acetylfuran, methylfurfural) and
  catalyses cysteine degradation — **a cellulose-containing plant matrix is not a neutral diluent for the
  sulfur lane**; (iii) PC + H2S → methanethiol-derived methylthio-heterocycles (lipid-dependent sulfur sink
  not in the engine).
- **Matrix relevance:** the lipid–aldehyde products (2-pentylthiapyran, 2-pentylpyridine, alkylthiophenes)
  are low dry and high wet (cf. `farmer1990_extraction.md`), i.e. the aldehyde-trapping by H2S/NH3 the
  lipid lane might need scales with water; directional only.

## 6. FLAGS

1. **Stem/DOI mismatch with the brief** (see top). This is jf00053a033.
2. **"Equimolar" is false by the printed masses** (40 mg Cys, 36 mg ribose → 1.38:1).
3. **No-cellulose arm masses not stated**; the "without phosphate salts" arm also changes the catalyst,
   so cellulose and phosphate are confounded in the cellulose-present vs -absent comparison.
4. **Text layer of Table 1 unusable**; image-read cells marked (?) are uncertain between "+" and "++".
5. Column "C alone" here = cellulose + cysteine (cellulose-present block); dry cysteine without cellulose
   gave "no significant volatile compounds".
6. No replicates, no SD, no response factors.

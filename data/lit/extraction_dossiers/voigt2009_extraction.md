**Answer for the evidence-map lead (2.1: "Voigt & Glomb 2009 is the lead" for amine acceleration): it is not.** The
paper has no amine-free arm, so it cannot measure an amine factor on 1-DG enolisation or cleavage (confidence ~0.97
that no such arm exists in the paper or is implied by a footnote). What it adds:
1. **Beta-dicarbonyl cleavage of 1-DG is fast and dominant even at 37 °C with an amino acid present** (acetic acid
   0.55-0.65 mol/mol in 24 h) [M]; oxidative alpha-cleavage is "of minor relevance or even nonexistent" [V] here.
2. **1-DG makes almost no MGO** at 37-50 °C [Q]. This bears on prereg sec. 2's finding that the fit drains MGO out of
   DPO (1-DX) as a compensation: in the hexose analogue at low temperature, 1-DG is not an MGO parent.
3. **O2 changes the C4 / C3 product mix by up to 4x** -- relevant to hub H6 (oxygen), not to the split.

## 6. Disagreements found

1. **Evidence map 2.1 vs this paper:** the map (and smuda2010_extraction.md:55-56, quoted there) expects an
   amine-free vs amine comparison; the paper has none. A correction for the map, not a chemical conflict.
2. **Internal:** Fig. 2's caption says 24 h; the text calls the same chromatogram "after 12 h" [!]. Glyceric acid
   lower at 50 °C than at 37 °C under air (Fig. 3) [!].
3. **Same laboratory, same 1-DG, buffer, concentration, temperature and acid method, different amine: Smuda 2010
   (Boc-Lys) vs Voigt 2009 (Ala).** Acetic acid at 24 h, 37 °C, aerated: **~250 mmol/mol** with Boc-Lys
   (smuda2010_extraction.md, its Fig. [F]) vs **550** with Ala here [M] (x2.2); glyceric acid, aerated: 88 at 7 d (Smuda)
   vs 171 at 12 h and ~256 at 48 h here (x2-3). Candidate causes: (i) the amine's identity changes the 1-DG fragmentation
   yield (Ala's free alpha-amino / carboxylate vs Boc-Lys's epsilon-amine) -- which would itself be an amine effect on
   beta-cleavage, the quantity evidence map 2.1 calls NONE; (ii) different 1-DG batches (each deprotected fresh; purity
   not printed in either); (iii) a different competing sink (Boc-Lys amides are < 1 % per Smuda, so not that). Two
   papers, one laboratory: provisional, but it is the nearest thing on disk to an amine-identity factor on 1-deoxyosone
   cleavage.
4. **Hou 2026 vs Voigt 2009 on deoxyosone retro-aldol:** Hou proposes retro-aldol of deoxyosones to GO / MGO as a main
   fate under cystine [P]; Voigt finds negligible MGO from 1-DG (hexose, 37-50 °C). Different sugar, temperature and
   amine; not a direct contradiction, but Hou's proposal has no fed-intermediate support and this paper's is a fed test.

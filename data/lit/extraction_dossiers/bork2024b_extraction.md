## 3. Assays [M]

- **CA, FA:** HPLC-DAD 310 nm (Prodigy ODS-3, 0.5 % acetic acid / MeCN), 1:75 dilution, **relative to t = 0**.
- **GO, MGO, DA:** OPD 50 mM (1:1, 24 h dark RT) → quinoxaline / 2-methylquinoxaline / 2,3-dimethylquinoxaline,
  HPLC-DAD 318 nm (Nucleosil 120-5 C18), 1:75, **relative to unheated samples**. No calibration, **no LOD / LOQ**.
- **Replicate noise** (SD, error bars): ±1-5 % for the dicarbonyls, ±1-3 % for FA.
- [!] Relative-to-t = 0 quantification of a dicarbonyl from a 40 wt% stock (oligomer / hydrate mixture) measures
  OPD-reactive MGO, not monomer.

### 4.3 Colour [M from text]

Colour index highest for CA/GO and FA/GO (CA/GO twice FA/GO at 5 and 10 min); MGO pots about one third of GO's maximum,
reached by 5 min; DA pots show no browning (CA/DA below CA alone). No engine observable.

### 4.4 Arithmetic [Z]

1. **MGO alone, 220 °C, near-dry**: if first order, k(0-5) = ln(100/17)/5 = 0.35 min⁻¹, k(5-10) = ln(17/9)/5 = 0.13
   min⁻¹; decelerating, no come-up correction; the water evaporates or reacts within the first minutes. Not a rate
   constant for any aqueous lane.
2. GO 23 → 26 % (5 → 10 min) is flat within SD: a fast initial loss then a plateau (oligomer / hydrate re-release on
   OPD derivatisation [P]).

## 5. What it can and cannot test in the model

**Can:** nothing quantitative in scope. Qualitatively: aldehydic α-dicarbonyls (GO, MGO) are consumed by phenols much
faster than the diketone DA, which the authors attribute to steric hindrance at the methyl-substituted carbonyls [Q].

**Cannot:** no NF; no water phase worth the name, no buffer, no pH, no O2 statement; relative assays without LOD; a
220 °C roasting regime outside every lane's domain.

## 6. Conflicts and agreements with the repo's records

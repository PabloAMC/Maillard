## 1. Methods

Synthesised intermediates (Gly-Amadori, TTCA, Cys-Amadori), 0.15 mmol each (Table 1: Gly-Amadori/Cys,
TTCA/Gly, Cys-Amadori/Gly, and each alone) in 5 mL 0.2 M sodium phosphate pH 5.5 (= 30 mmol/L), 15 mL
pressure vials sealed under ambient air, 140 C with stirring for 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 min; UV/Vis
(294 / 420 nm) and pH followed; volatiles of the 30-min solutions by HS-SPME GC-MS/GC-O, reported as PEAK
AREAS (x 1e5) with odour dilution factors (Table 2).

## 2. Findings that matter

Reaction rate: Gly-Amadori > Cys-Amadori > TTCA (TTCA is the stable thiazolidine; consistent with Zhai
2020/2021). Cys-Amadori/Gly gives the most meaty volatiles and browning; TTCA/Gly produces more
sulfur volatiles than Gly-Amadori/Cys; TTCA alone and Cys-Amadori alone both form MFT, 3-mercapto-
2-pentanone, FFT, 2-methylthiophene etc. Units are peak areas, so no concentration or rate transfers.

## 3. What the repo takes

Support for the W6 species decision only: the core charges the xylose-cysteine intermediate as TTCA and
cannot distinguish the Amadori isomer, and this paper shows the isomer matters (Cys-Amadori reacts
faster than TTCA). No panel claim (peak areas; the two isomers map to one core species). Vessel stated:
5 mL in a 15 mL vial under air (headspace 10 mL) — a vessel entry for any future TTCA row from this lab.

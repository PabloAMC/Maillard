## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

**The triage verdict holds.** The paper is an identification study: 152 headspace components of a
cysteine + ribose reaction, with and without egg phosphatidylcholine, are listed in elution order with
LRI, identification method, new mass spectra and FTIR bands, and a peak-size code **"+++ large; ++
medium; + small; − not detected"** for each system. **No concentrations, peak areas, internal standard,
replicate count or SD appear anywhere**; the abstract's "major volatile products" is literal. The
ordinal code gives three usable things: (i) **presence/absence** per compound in a cysteine + ribose
pot (e.g. MFT, 2-furanmethanethiol, 2-thiophenethiol, 2-methyl-3-thiophenethiol, 3-mercapto-2-pentanone,
2-acetylthiazole all "+++" without lipid; **bis(2-methyl-3-furyl) disulfide is not listed at all**);
(ii) **the lipid-specific products** (hexanal, 2-pentylpyridine, 2-alkylthiophenes, 1-alkanethiols,
alkenylthiophenes: "−" without PL, "++/+++" with); (iii) a coarse **direction of the PL effect** for
compounds coded differently in the two columns (e.g. furfural ++ → +). For every quantitative use,
**`farmer1990_extraction.md` supersedes this paper**: same laboratory, same 41/30 mM pot, 140 °C 1 h,
with numbers and SDs.

### 3.2 Qualitative results [Q] (pp. 358–366)

- Overall odour of both pots "sulphurous, rubbery" close up; cysteine + ribose "some underlying meaty
  odour"; with PL "much more pronounced 'cooked meat, beefy'", noticeable at several metres.
- In the cysteine + ribose pot, non-sulfur Maillard products are "reduced" relative to glycine + ribose
  (Salter 1988); "the alkyl furfurals … were present in very small amounts or were not detected; only
  2-furfural was present in sufficient quantities to justify inclusion".
- Among the major early peaks without lipid: MFT (29) and 2-furanmethanethiol (53); 2-thiophenethiol (80)
  and 2-methyl-3-thiophenethiol (86) "among the dominant peaks".
- Proposed routes (literature-based, not tested here): MFT and 4/5-methyl-3-furanthiol from H2S +
  sugar-derived furanones; FFT from H2S + furfural; thiophenethiols from α-dicarbonyls or furanones +
  H2S; 3,5-dimethyl-1,2,4-trithiolane from ethanal + H2S.
- Lipid-specific products: 2-alkylthiophenes (32, 41, 57, 77, 99), 2-pentylpyridine (85), alkenyl
  thiophenes (110, 114, 130), 1-heptanethiol and 1-octanethiol (18, 40; "probably arise from the action
  of H2S on lipid breakdown products").
- GC-odour: meaty odours at 5.1, 10.7, 13.0, 20.3, 27.0, 31.7 min with PL; only the 13.0 min one (MFT)
  without PL. MFT "acrid, sulphurous" at the peak apex, "meaty" on the tail; FFT probably the "pungent,
  garlic" at 18 min.

## 5. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

**Cannot anchor anything numeric.** No rate, yield, concentration, ratio or pH/temperature/time axis.
One pot, one condition (140 °C, 1 h, pH 5.7 nominal, 41 mM Cys / 30 mM ribose, 15 g/L egg PC).
Whatever ordinal codes say about the PL effect is better taken, with numbers, from
`farmer1990_extraction.md` (MFT ×0.27 with PC, FFT ×0.62–0.72, mercaptoketones ×~0.5; 0.5 M phosphate
pH 5.6 there, 0.2 M pH 5.7 here [!]).

**Can serve as qualitative (presence/absence) checks on the sulfur and lipid lanes, cysteine + ribose,
no thiamine:**

1. **MFT (29), FFT (53), 2-acetylthiazole (97), furfural (61), 3-mercapto-2-pentanone (43) and
   1-mercapto-3-pentanone (71) are all formed** in a thiamine-free cysteine + ribose pot.
   - `species_sulfur.py` labels **MP2P (3-mercapto-2-pentanone) "the THIAMINE-diagnostic isomer"** (Cerny
     2007: 77–90 % thiamine-derived in a pot that contains thiamine). Here it is **+++ with no thiamine
     at all**, and `farmer1990_extraction.md` row 46 quantifies it at a level equal to its isomer MP3P
     (28 200 vs 27 100 relative area). The label is about *share in a thiamine pot*, not *exclusive
     origin*, so this is not a contradiction — but the lane must produce MP2P from the pentose/cysteine
     route as well; a thiamine-only MP2P route would fail this check. Note the identification is by
     literature spectrum + accurate mass, **not an authentic standard**, and **2-mercapto-3-pentanone is
     not listed** in Table 1 although Farmer 1990 resolved both isomers in the same pot: on this
     CPWax column the two may co-elute under entry 43 [!].
2. **Bis(2-methyl-3-furyl) disulfide (`MFTD`) is absent from Table 1** although MFT is "+++". A C10
   disulfide elutes late on a wax phase (I expect beyond ~2100, unverified here), and the list ends at
   2103, so it may simply have eluted past the window or below the threshold for inclusion; **absence from this list is not evidence of non-formation** [!].
3. **Lipid-only products** (hexanal, heptanal, nonanal, 2,4-decadienal, 1-octen-3-ol, 2-pentylfuran,
   2-pentylpyridine, 2-alkylthiophenes, 1-alkanethiols) are "−" without PL and "++/+++" with it. For the
   lipid lane this is a trivially satisfied sanity check; the lipid × sulfur cross-products
   (alkylthiophenes, alkanethiols, pentylpyridine) are not modelled.
4. **Ordinal direction of the PL effect** where the code changes: furfural ++ → +, MFT +++ → +++ (no
   change visible, whereas Farmer 1990 measures ×0.27 with PC — **a three-step code cannot resolve a
   ~4× change**, which is the strongest reason not to use the codes as data).

**Caveats:** cross-contamination footnote c (nine lipid compounds in the lipid-free pot); no replicates
stated; abundance coded from the MS total-ion chromatogram, so response differences between compound
classes are folded into the code; the PL is ~70 % PC / up to 30 % PE, not pure PC.

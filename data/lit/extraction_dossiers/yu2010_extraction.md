## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

Ascorbic acid (ASA) + cysteine at 141 °C for 2 h gives a sulfur-dominated headspace — alicyclic polysulfur
compounds (3,5-dimethyl- and 3,5-diethyl-1,2,4-trithiolanes, 4,6-dimethyl-1,2,3-trithianes,
1,2,5,6-tetrathiocane), thienothiophenes (thieno[3,2-b]thiophene largest), 3-(vinylthio)thiophene,
acetylthiophenes, thiazoles (2-acetylthiazole largest) and, above pH 6, pyrazines. **Thienothiophenes and
thiophenones fall with pH; thiazoles, pyrazines and thiophenes rise; dimethyltrithiolanes peak at pH 6
and fall ~3× by pH 8.** No furans, no thiols of any kind are reported. Peak areas only, no internal
standard, duplicates without SD, no final pH.

## 5. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

- **MFT/FFT: nothing.** No sugar, no furanthiols reported; the ASA furan route (furfural from ASA) did not
  yield detectable FFT under these conditions — itself a qualitative observation (ASA + Cys ≠ pentose + Cys
  for the thiol channel), but absence in a TIC-threshold SPME screen is weak evidence.
- **No benchmark-quality row:** peak areas only, no internal standard, two fibres that disagree by up to
  ~5×, no final pH (cysteine added after adjusting pH in only 0.2 M phosphate with 100 mM acids — the pot
  pH will drift).
- **Directional pH-shape HOLD-OUT (if an ascorbic-acid carbonyl source is ever added):** pyrazines and
  2-acetylthiazole rise monotonically to pH 8, thiophenones fall, dimethyltrithiolanes peak at pH 6. The
  sulfur lane has no ascorbic-acid species, so this is currently out of scope.
- **Cysteine-degradation chemistry without sugar** (cf. `mottram1995b` cysteine + water) — polysulfur
  heterocycles are a large H2S/acetaldehyde sink; qualitative support for such a sink in the sulfur lane.

## 6. FLAGS

1. **Nominal pH ≠ reaction pH**: pH set on the ASA solution before cysteine was added; no final pH.
2. **No internal standard**; area counts at an integration threshold; duplicates, no SD.
3. **Two fibres disagree** in magnitude and occasionally in sign of trend (e.g. 2-ethylthieno[2,3-b]thiophene).
4. **Peaks 37 and 38 are both "3,5-diethyl-1,2,4-trithiolane"** (cis/trans; LRI 1332 / 1340) — counted
   separately; the text calls them one pair.
5. "1,2,5,6-Tetrathioctane" and "4-hydroxybenzothiophene" are MS-only tentative identities; BHT (peak 42) is
   almost certainly a fibre/septum contaminant, not a reaction product.
6. The text says 2,4-dimethylthiazole is from pyruvaldehyde; it is ND at pH 9 while present at pH 6–8
   (single cell, unexplained).

## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

Model orange juice (MOJ, pH 3.8: 5 % sucrose, 2.5 % fructose, 2.5 % glucose, 1 % citric acid, 0.5 %
tripotassium citrate) with **0.024 mM thiamine HCl**, 50 mL in 120-mL amber vials, **N₂ flushed
into the headspace**, sealed, **35 °C in the dark for up to 8 weeks**; sampled at days 0, 1, 7, 14,
28, 42, 56. Volatiles by headspace SPME (40 °C, 30 min) with GC-O, GC-PFPD, GC-MS. **MFT
(GC-PFPD, external calibration in thiamine-free MOJ): ~1.0 × 10⁻⁴ mM at day 7, 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ at 14,
5.0 × 10⁻⁴ at 28, 9.8 × 10⁻⁴ at 42, 7.0 × 10⁻⁴ at 56. MFT-MFT: first PFPD detection at day 14
(2.0 × 10⁻⁵ mM), ~3 × 10⁻⁴ mM at days 28, 42 and 56.** GC-O detects both from day 7, before the
PFPD does. Thirteen aroma-active peaks; MFT + MFT-MFT carry 33 % of total aroma at day 7 and 48 %
at day 42. A small H2S peak appears on the PFPD at day 7 (Fig. 2), not quantified. Controls exclude
injector artefacts (no dimer from 0.1 ppm MFT at 160–200 °C injector), microbes (< 10 cfu/mL at
days 0 and 56) and the norfuraneol route (none in the 56-day thiamine-free control). **Thiamine
itself was never measured.**

## 4. DERIVED QUANTITIES [Z] (mine, not printed)

**Sulfur in MFT + MFT-MFT as a share of initial thiamine (0.024 mM):**

| day | MFT + 2 × MFT-MFT (mM) | % of initial thiamine | dimer's share of the MFT sulfur |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 0.98 × 10⁻⁴ | 0.41 % | – |
| 14 | 2.9 × 10⁻⁴ | 1.2 % | 14 % |
| 28 | 1.07 × 10⁻³ | 4.4 % | 54 % |
| 42 | 1.54 × 10⁻³ | **6.4 %** | 36 % |
| 56 | 1.30 × 10⁻³ | 5.4 % | 46 % |

**Apparent formation rate, days 0–42:** close to linear, ~3.5–3.8 × 10⁻⁵ mM/day of MFT-sulfur,
i.e. **k_app(THI → MFT-S) ≈ 1.5 × 10⁻³ day⁻¹ ≈ 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ s⁻¹ at 35 °C, pH 3.8**, if thiamine stays
near its initial value (not measured; a lower bound on the channel constant if thiamine is
being depleted).

**Apparent MFT loss, days 42 → 56 (G1-relevant, weak):** MFT falls 9.87 → 6.95 × 10⁻⁴ mM while the
dimer does not rise. If formation stopped at day 42, k_loss(MFT) ≥ ln(9.87/6.95)/14 d = **0.025 d⁻¹
≈ 2.9 × 10⁻⁷ s⁻¹**; for the total MFT sulfur, ≥ 0.012 d⁻¹ (1.4 × 10⁻⁷ s⁻¹). If formation continued
at the day 0–42 rate, the loss constant on the total MFT sulfur is ~0.04 d⁻¹ (~4 × 10⁻⁷ s⁻¹).
**The day-42 and day-56 error bars overlap** (7.0 × 10⁻⁴ – 1.26 × 10⁻³ vs 4.85 – 9.07 × 10⁻⁴), and
the day-42 point is above the top standard, so **the decline is not established at the paper's own
precision**. Read as: a net MFT loss at 35 °C, pH 3.8 of order 10⁻⁷ s⁻¹ is allowed; zero is not
excluded.

**Comparison with the ³⁵S balance** (`dwivedi1972_extraction.md`): at 121 °C, pH 3.5–6.0, ≥ 96 % of
degraded thiamine's sulfur stays in the thiazole, so **all non-thiazole sulfur ≤ 4 % of degraded
thiamine**. Here MFT alone carries **≥ 6.4 % of the initial thiamine** at pH 3.8, 35 °C, i.e. more
than the 121 °C bound allows **even if all thiamine had degraded**. Either the sulfur fate of
thiamine depends on temperature (the MFT channel relatively favoured at 35 °C, i.e. a lower Ea
than bridge cleavage), the citrate/sugar matrix opens a route absent in phosphate buffer, or the
SPME-PFPD calibration overstates MFT (external standards in thiamine-free MOJ, top point exceeded,
PFPD response non-linear). **Not resolvable from either paper.**

## 5. WHAT THE MODEL CAN TAKE FROM IT

**Context.** THI → HMP → MFT (`r_thi_hmp`), THI → MeSH, THI → H2S (B50 share, zero below pH 6.25);
MFT is consumed by `ch_dimer_mft` (oxidant, thiolate-weighted), `ch_thiolate_loss_mft` (thiolate,
B2.1), `ch_mft_decay` (unassigned), with the dimer decaying by `k_dimer_decay` and (B17, inert)
released by `k_dimer_release`. The engine's validated range is 100–145 °C.

**Verdict: (b) a time-resolved product series at one low temperature; no constant transfers
directly.**
- **G5 (thiamine degradation with H2S at ≥ 2 temperatures): NO.** One temperature (35 °C), thiamine
  loss not measured, H2S only a PFPD peak of unknown size and unknown source (control not shown).
  Qualitatively, an H2S peak at **pH 3.8** with thiamine present is in mild tension with B50's zero
  share below pH 6.25 only if it comes from thiamine; nothing here says how much.
- **G1 (quantified thiol loss kinetics): only a weak bound.** The day 42 → 56 MFT decline gives
  k_loss(MFT, 35 °C, pH 3.8, N₂-flushed headspace) ≈ 1–4 × 10⁻⁷ s⁻¹ **if real**, and the paper's
  own error bars do not exclude zero. **It cannot identify the sink.** It can sharpen one point
  about the model's declared mechanism: at pH 3.8 and 35 °C the model's thiolate fraction is
  ~4 × 10⁻⁵ (pKa 8.33 at 25 °C moved to ~8.18 by the module's ΔH [C, `parameters_sulfur.py`]), so a
  sink acting only through the thiolate would need an intrinsic constant of ≳ 7 × 10⁻³ s⁻¹ at 35 °C
  to produce the apparent loss. If the loss is real, an acid-pH, low-temperature MFT sink that is
  not thiolate-gated (or a non-oxidative route) is indicated. The dimer plateau with MFT still
  rising also says the disulfide is not a terminal store at 35 °C (the authors' reading), in
  line with Guth et al. 1995's hydrolysis at 100 °C (`guth1995_extraction.md`), but no rate follows.
- **Usable as a low-temperature hold-out check (not a fit target):** the MFT and MFT-MFT time
  series at 35 °C, pH 3.8, 0.024 mM thiamine, with the shape "MFT-S ~linear in time to 6 % of
  thiamine by day 42; dimer ~30–50 % of MFT-S from day 28". A model carrying `k_thi_hmp` with its
  Arrhenius barrier from ≥ 100 °C down to 35 °C either lands within an order of magnitude of
  1.7 × 10⁻⁸ s⁻¹ (apparent THI → MFT-S) or it does not. The extrapolation lies outside the engine's
  validated range, so a miss would be a statement about the barrier, not a scoring failure.
- **Contradiction worth recording:** MFT-sulfur ≥ 6.4 % of initial thiamine at pH 3.8 versus the ≤ 4 %
  non-thiazole bound at 121 °C (Dwivedi & Arnold 1972). If both are right, the THI → HMP/MFT share is
  temperature-dependent in the opposite direction to what a single shared barrier would give.

**Not usable:** the GC-O intensities (normalised, saturated); the odour-activity multiples as
concentrations; any H2S number (none).

**Caveats.** Thiamine never measured; triplicate PFPD with unstated error statistic; day-42 MFT above
the top standard; PFPD sulfur response is non-linear (S₂ emission), calibrated only at 3–4 levels;
headspace N₂ flush without degassing the liquid, so dissolved O₂ was present at the start; sugars
and citrate (possible metal chelation) make the matrix different from the phosphate pots the model
is built on.

## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

IMP (free acid) supplies ribose by phosphate and glycosidic hydrolysis at the low pH of the
unbuffered mix (pH 2.3 Cys, 2.2 GSH, measured). After 1 h at 180 °C: **furfural 1619 (Cys) vs 1070
(GSH) mg/mol; 2-furfurylthiol 384.43 vs 180.49 mg/mol; 2-methyl-3-furanthiol 9.60 mg/mol from Cys
and not detected from GSH** [M]. Per mol of thiol precursor [Z]: **FFT 3.37 × 10⁻³ (Cys) vs 1.58 ×
10⁻³ (GSH) mol/mol (ratio 2.1); MFT 8.4 × 10⁻⁵ (Cys) vs < LOD (GSH)**. Furfural 16.9 vs 11.1 mmol/mol.
Of the 47 compounds in Table I, IMP–Cys shows 42 and IMP–GSH 24 [Z: row count]; no pyrazines; trithiolanes only in IMP–GSH (2.6 mg/mol),
none in IMP–Cys. The authors attribute the lower diversity with GSH to cysteine "or its degradation
products" favouring IMP hydrolysis [Q]. They note the opposite ranking reported by Grosch et al.
1990 for ribose + Cys/GSH at pH 5.7 (GSH gave *more* MFT than Cys), and conclude pH controls the
outcome [Q].

## 4. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

**Model context.** MFT and FFT are formed in the sulfur lane from free-Cys H2S with pentose-derived
intermediates; GSH enters only as `r_gsh_h2s`. No IMP chemistry in the engine. Nothing here was run
against the model.

1. **No kinetics, no rates, no Ea.** One temperature (180 °C), one time, no H2S, no residual IMP,
   ribose, Cys or GSH.
2. **MFT/FFT ratio test at very low pH (usable as direction only).** At pH ~2.2, 180 °C: GSH gives
   **no detectable MFT** and **~half the FFT** of Cys. Same sign as `zhou2023c_extraction.md` at
   pH 7.5, 120 °C (MFT 18× lower, FFT 1.3× lower from GSH than Cys with an Ala-xylose ARP), so the
   **MFT-is-the-Cys-sensitive, FFT-is-the-furfural-sensitive** asymmetry holds across pH 2–7.5. A
   model that makes MFT and FFT from GSH only via H2S would have to reproduce both ratios through
   one H2S supply; this paper suggests FFT ∝ furfural × some H2S, MFT needing more.
3. **FFT/furfural ~0.14–0.20 at 180 °C, pH 2** — an order-of-magnitude check on a furfural + H2S → FFT
   branch, not a constant (SDE recovery of thiols is poor and FFT oxidises to the disulfide, which
   is listed separately).
4. **Not usable:** absolute yields (FID/IS, response factor 1, single run); anything about IMP
   hydrolysis kinetics (not measured; see the authors' citations of Matoba 1988, Shaoul & Sporns 1987).

**Caveats.** Unbuffered pH 2.2–2.3 (the pH most unlike food); 180 °C; air headspace presumed; SDE
artefacts; no replicates; two dithiane assignments inconsistent between tables.

## 1. Methods — NOT aqueous

1 mmol sugar (glucose / maltose / maltotriose) + 1 mmol o-phenylenediamine (the trapping agent, added
IN SITU) mixed dry, with or without 1 mmol glycine, heated in sealed tubes at 100 C for up to 480 min.
Quinoxalines by HPLC, confirmed by GC/MS; results in mol % of the starting sugar.

## 2. Glucose alone (caramelisation-resembling; Figure 1, text)

After 180 min: methylglyoxal ~2.5 mol % predominates; 3-deoxyhexosulose, 1-deoxyhexosulose and
1,4-dideoxyhexosulose each < 1 mol %. Fructose up to 6.1 mol % at 30 min (isomerisation). With glycine
the deoxyhexosuloses reach 11 mol % and the fragments 7 mol % at 180 min; glucose > 60 mol % converted
after 30 min in the Maillard mixture (~15 mol % remaining at 180 min). Table 1 (maltose) is re-typed in
the text layer; glucose values are figure-only.

## 3. Reading and use

A dry melt with the trap inside the reaction, so the numbers are formation FLUXES into the trap, not
free concentrations, and the trap itself perturbs the chemistry (the authors discuss Hofmann 1999's
objection). Within those limits the ordering MGO > 3-DG, 1-DG in glucose alone at 100 C is the
opposite of Leitzen's aqueous ordering (3-DG >> MGO), which is itself informative: in water the
deoxyosones accumulate, in a dry melt they fragment. Recorded; no claim written, because the trunk has
no dry-melt a_w state below the Pereyra Gonzales table and the trap changes the observable.

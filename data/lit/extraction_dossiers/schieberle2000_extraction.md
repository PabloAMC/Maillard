## 1. Why it matters

Table IV is a TIME SERIES AT 100 C of the very pot the sulfur fit's Hofmann 1998 constants come from

## 3. What it says about the sinks

Both thiols still RISE between 6 and 12 h at 100 C (MFT x1.15, FFT x1.20) — the removal steps are slow
relative to formation at 100 C; the thiazine PEAKS at 60 min and is gone by 6 h. The 30 -> 60 min step
(MFT x3.1, FFT x1.6) and the 60 -> 360 min step (MFT x11, FFT x35) are the within-study shape the
core's formation/sink balance must reproduce.

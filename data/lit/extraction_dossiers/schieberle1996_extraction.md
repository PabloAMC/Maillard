## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

The first SIDA quantitation of the Hofmann ribose/cysteine pot. **Table 3 prints MFT 198 µg/L and FFT
121 µg/L** (OAV 28 286 and 12 100) together with ten other odorants, including the only printed levels
for 2-mercapto-3-pentanone (253), 3-mercapto-2-butanone (342), 2-methyl-3-thiophenethiol (7),
2-thenylthiol (5), sotolon (16), furaneol (185) and **norfuraneol (545 300 µg/L)**. A recombinate of the
twelve at these concentrations in pH 5.0 buffer was "nearly identical" to the reaction mixture
("beef broth-like"). Table 4 gives OAVs at pH 3.0 / 5.0 / 7.0; dividing by the thresholds implied by
Table 3 back-calculates the Hofmann 1998 JAFC pH series **exactly** (MFT 553/198/25 µg/L = 55.3/19.8/2.5
µg per 100 mL). Table 2 shows that conventional ether extraction destroys MFT entirely ("d.n.p."),
while 2-vinylpyridine derivatisation and purge-and-trap agree within ~20 % (17 vs 21 µg/L).

## 5. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

- **MFT/FFT at 145 °C, pH 3/5/7: NOT new rows.** They are the Hofmann 1998 JAFC rows the fit already
  carries (pH 5.0 FIT, pH 3/7 HOLD-OUT under `k3_final_parameter_inventory.md`). **Do not ingest them a
  second time as an independent source** — same laboratory, same pot, same numbers.
- **New absolute levels on the same pot (pH 5.0, measured, SIDA):** 2-mercapto-3-pentanone 253,
  3-mercapto-2-butanone 342, 2-methyl-3-thiophenethiol 7, 2-thenylthiol 5, sotolon 16, furaneol 185,
  norfuraneol 545 300 µg/L. Of these, the sulfur lane carries mercaptoketones and norfuraneol; the
  3-mercapto-2-pentanone (599) and thiazine (424) are already in `schieberle1998_extraction.md`.
  **Norfuraneol 4.8 mol % of ribose is a strong mass-balance anchor** for the NF pool feeding MFT.
- **Glucose pot 3-mercapto-2-butanone ~28 µg/L (three methods)** — a measured hexose mercaptoketone level;
  pot conditions not printed here [!].
- **pH 3/7 back-calculated levels** (§4) for mercaptopentanones, thiazine, furaneol, acetylthiazoline:
  usable only as directional HOLD-OUT shape tests, tagged [Z]; the mercaptopentanones peak at pH 5 (non-
  monotone), unlike MFT/FFT which fall monotonically with pH.
- **Workup caution (Table 2):** any MFT number in the corpus obtained by plain solvent extraction +
  distillation without a labelled standard, derivatisation or headspace trapping is suspect — here MFT was
  not determinable at all by Method I.
- **Thresholds:** the implied water thresholds (MFT 0.0070, FFT 0.010 µg/L) are second-hand; they match
  `schieberle1998` Table IV's printed values (0.007, 0.01). Not a new threshold source.

## 6. FLAGS

1. **Same-run duplication**: MFT/FFT here = Hofmann 1998 JAFC Tables 1–2 = `schieberle1998` Table IV
   column II. One measurement, three printings.
2. **2-Thenylthiol pH 5.0 OAV is 119 in Table 3 and 19 in Table 4**; one is a typo. With 5 µg/L, 119 implies
   threshold 0.042 µg/L, 19 implies 0.26 µg/L. Unresolved; the pH 3/7 back-calculation for this row is
   unreliable.
3. **3-Mercapto-2-pentanone pH 3.0 OAV (361) equals 2-mercapto-3-pentanone pH 5.0 OAV (361)** — possibly a
   coincidence, possibly a row-copy error; cannot be tested.
4. Table 3 title says "Eleven" odorants; twelve rows; text says "twelve".
5. pH 3.0 and 7.0 runs: no final pH, no statement that the 0.5 M phosphate held the pH (the 1998 JAFC
   paper is the source for that).
6. No replicates or SD printed anywhere.

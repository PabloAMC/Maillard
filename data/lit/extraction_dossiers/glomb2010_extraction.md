### 4.3 Arithmetic [Z]

1. Fed-dicarbonyl first-order constants from the half-lives (50 °C, pH 7.4, with Lys): 1-DG ln2 / 30 min = **0.023
   min⁻¹**; glucosone 1.4 × 10⁻³ min⁻¹; 3-DG 2.9 × 10⁻⁴ min⁻¹.
2. 1-DPO / 1-DG standing ratio after 7 d: 0.6 (aerated), 0.05 (de-aerated). 1-DPO follows O2 (×3 up with air) while 1-DG
   falls with air (×4.4 down): **1-DPO tracks the oxidative glucosone supply, not 1-DG.**

## 5. What it can and cannot test in the model

**Can:**
- **The carbon origin of hexose C5 dicarbonyls** at 50 °C / pH 7.4 with a lysine amine: C-1 is lost; the C5 skeleton is
  glucose C2-C6. The engine's `r_odg_dpo` (1-DG → 1-DPO + C1, "the C-6 loss", inactive in B60) predicts the opposite
  label pattern (C-1 kept as the methyl). Glomb's labelling sees no such product (no floor printed).
- 1-DG's lifetime at 50 °C (t½ 0.5 h) and its main exits (cited: acetic acid ≤ 65 mol %, C4 fragments).
- An O2 contrast on the glucose dicarbonyl pool (Table I).

**Cannot:**
- Anything at process temperature (50 °C only) or other pH (7.4 only); no thiol, so no MFT / NF / FFT; LODs absent;
  "strictly" has no stated label-resolution floor; the labelled-glucose data themselves (isotopologue shares) are not
  printed, only the conclusion.

## 6. Conflicts and agreements with the repo's records

1. **Agrees with `tressl1994`** (positional [1-13C] / [6-13C] glucose + Cys, 160 °C): glucose-derived C5 heterocycles
   carry C2-C6 (C-1 lost, via 3-DG). Glomb adds a C-1-lost **1-deoxypentosone** (a methyl ketone, the direct precursor
   class of norfuraneol and MFT in the pentose route), formed from **glucosone** (oxidative), at 50 °C.
2. **Agrees with `voigt2009` / `smuda2010`** (same laboratory): 1-DG's exits are acids and C4 fragments; no C5 product
   from 1-DG is reported.
3. Bears on `HEXOSE_C5_ENTRY` / `r_odg_dpo` (sulfur.py, "the C-6 loss"): no measurement on disk shows 1-DG → 1-DPO;
   Glomb shows the hexose 1-DPO that does form comes another way.
4. [!] Internal: the label shares are asserted ("strictly"), not tabulated. For MGO the authors themselves note that
   their label data (only 32 % from the upper part, C1-C3) contradict the literature's retro-aldol origin from 1-DG /
   3-DG, and assign most MGO to the C4 / C5 fragments [Q]. Bears on R3's C2 + C3 supply only at 50 °C / pH 7.4.

## M4 role (case 8660c005)

**Role fixed before reading:** band source for 1-DG's exits and for `r_odg_dpo`'s ceiling (labelled sugars, O2 on / off);
never decisive (no thiol); measurement that would count: "a 1-DG -> C5 product (or its absence with a floor)"; P ~0.3.

- **What it supplies.** Coordinate: `r_odg_dpo` (sec. 5.2 band "0 to a ceiling: the share of fed 1-DG not accounted for
  by the measured exits … barrier 40 - 135 kJ/mol").
  - **A 1-DG → C5 product: absent, without a floor.** The only hexose C5 dicarbonyls (1-DPO 0.01-0.03, 3-DPO 0.10-0.21
    mmol/mol glucose after 7 d) are glucose C2-C6 and rise with O2, so none is assigned to 1-DG. Read as a ceiling
    [Z]: at 50 °C / pH 7.4 / Lys, 1-DPO from **any** route ≤ 0.03 mmol/mol glucose standing, against 1-DG 0.05-0.22;
    the C-6-loss share of that 1-DPO is "none observed" (no label floor printed). It narrows the band's ceiling only
    qualitatively; the band stays "numerical above zero" as the case says.
  - 1-DG's lifetime: t½ 0.5 h at 50 °C, pH 7.4, Lys (k ≈ 0.023 min⁻¹). Exits cited from Voigt 2009 (already in the case).
  - Transfer: 50 °C, pH 7.4, Lys (protein-amine model), 0.1 M phosphate, 7 d; far from Hofmann's 145 °C / pH 3-7 / Cys.
- **Unexpected, bearing on the which-carbon gap (not decisive).** Case sec. 3 notes that "a route through a C-1-lost C5
  that is then reduced to a 2-methylfuran is not proposed by any source on disk". Glomb proposes, and supports with
  [1-/6-13C]glucose, a C-1-lost **1-deoxypentosone** — already methyl-bearing (the methyl is glucose C-2), no reduction
  needed — formed from glucosone by β-cleavage under air. An MFT made from it would carry glucose C2-C6 with the methyl
  from C-2: [1-13C]glucose would give unlabelled MFT, where R1 / R2 predict a labelled methyl. **It does not meet a
  decisive criterion** (no thiol, no MFT, 50 °C; criterion (c) needs a label on MFT or a fed hexose intermediate giving
  MFT), and it is not re-roled here. It names an unlisted route that the case's designed arm 1 (positional label on
  MFT) would separate from R1 / R2, and an O2 dependence (Table I: 1-DPO ×3 with air) that the design's N2 / air arms
  (sec. 4.4, arm 2) would expose if they were also run on arm 1.

**Answers, weighed.**
1. **A diamine without a thiol reproduces the "cysteine trap" signature** (the strongest finding for B7; my
   confidence that the observation is real ~0.75, that it means signature (iii) is not thiol-specific ~0.6). Caveats:
   one laboratory, one pH, triplicates; **5 mM L-cystine exceeds its room-temperature water solubility (0.168 g/L,
   0.70 mM at 25 C, Carta 1996; not in the paper) about sevenfold** [!] [corrected 2026-11-05: this line read "~0.11
   g/L, ~0.5 mM, my value ... about tenfold"; Carta 1996 Table 1 measures 0.69 - 0.71 mmol/L in water over initial pH
   3.0 - 8.6 at 25 C only (carta1996 dossier, Arithmetic 1: 0.70 mmol/L x 240.3 g/mol = 0.168 g/L); 5 / 0.70 = 7.1x;
   the solubility at 120 C is not measured], so the pot starts as a suspension and the dissolved
   concentration during heating is unknown; cystine can disproportionate or be reduced to cysteine during heating
   (not measured in the model pot). The cystine pot is less acidic (pH 6.07 vs 5.05 at 20 min), so the 3-DX loss is
   co-varying with pH, i.e. it could be the base-favoured sink of prereg sec. 19-20 acting through pH, or an amine
   reaction of the free alpha-amino groups.
2. **The pH drift is large and fast.** A 5 mM ARP in water loses two pH units in 20 min at 120 °C (measured after
   cooling). Every unbuffered "pH 7" pot of this laboratory (Wei 2022, Zhou 2022 / 2024b, Zhai 2019 / 2021 / 2023) should
   be read as a drifting-pH pot unless its own pH record says otherwise [Z].
3. **The deoxyosone ratio climbs as the pH falls** (2.6 -> 10.9), the direction H3 predicts (lower pH favours 3-DX /
   disfavours 1-DX), here inside ONE pot. With cystine the climb is slower (1.5 -> 3.8) and so is the pH fall.
4. **Both deoxyosones are short-lived** at 120 °C (1-DX tens of minutes at most, 3-DX ~1-2 h): hub H1's direction.

1. **Same laboratory, same nominal pot as Wei 2022** (GX-ARP 5 mM, water, initial pH 7.0, 120 °C, ex situ OPD with
   12 h derivatisation, internal standard; wei2022_extraction.md Fig. 3 / Fig. 2b), in mol % of ARP:

   |---|---|---|---|

   | 1-DX at 90-120 min | 15.2 % (90) | 1.4 % (100) / 0.9 % (120) | **~0.06-0.09** |
   | GO at 20 / 60 min | 6.9 / 10.4 % | 0.7 / 3.4 % | 0.1 / 0.33 |
   | MGO at 20 / 60 min | 1.9 / 4.8 % | 1.0 / 1.7 % | 0.5 / 0.35 |

   The ARP (calibrated in both) decays ~1.5-2x slower in Hou; Wei's 1-DX does not decay over 90 min while Hou's falls
   to <1 %. Candidate causes: ARP batch / purity (unstated in Hou; "99.05" / "99.5" % in Wei); Wei's panel-labelling
   flags (its Fig. 3 caption swaps 1-DX / 3-DX; even swapped, Wei's late 1-DX or 3-DX does not reach Hou's ~1 %);
   different pH trajectories (Hou measured, Wei did not); headspace / O2 (unstated in both); semi-quantitative DX scale
   (CF-062) -- cannot explain the calibrated ARP or GO / MGO gaps. Same family as CF-060 (the AX-ARP scale spread).
2. **Wei 2022's "one pH (7.0)" is a drifting pH** [Z]: on Hou's record the same pot is at pH ~5 by 20 min. The wei2022
   dossier's statement that the paper "cannot show a trap that grows with pH" stands, but its pots are not pH-7 pots.
3. **Authors' claim vs their mass balance** (retro-aldol, sec. 1 (e)) [!].
4. **Engine:** no amine (non-thiol) 3-DX sink; no in-situ pH for unbuffered pots in the deoxyosone rows (the engine uses
   the label pH; cf. CF-076).

## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

At 120 °C, initial pH 7, AX-ARP alone (20 mM) makes furfural (443 -> 1261 µg/L over 30-120 min),
2-acetylfuran, 2-propionylfuran and **2,3-butanedione (36-74 µg/L)** but **no pyrazine**. With
20 mM Cys the furans halve, **2,3-butanedione disappears (ND at every time)**, and the pot makes
**MFT 63 -> 345 µg/L and FFT 75 -> 419 µg/L** (monotone over 30-120 min), bis(2-methyl-3-furyl)
disulfide 35-44 µg/L, thiophenes, thiazoles (2-acetylthiazole 6-12 µg/L) and pyrazine + methyl-
pyrazine (7.6 -> 16.5 µg/L). The figure data show why: Cys **slows ARP loss** (7.2 vs 6.2 mM left at
120 min), **cuts 3-DP ~5-8× and 1-DP ~3×, GO ~3× and MGO ~8×**, raises the recovered Ala (8.2 vs
5.0 mM), and is itself **consumed almost completely** (5 % left at 120 min, vs 74 % left when Cys is
heated alone at the same 120 °C, pH 7). At pH 8 (60 min) ARP alone makes 3.5× more butanedione
(126 µg/L) and some pyrazines; Cys still suppresses butanedione (to 16 % at 20 mM) and raises
pyrazines ~20-30×, and 2,4,5-trimethylthiazole appears (pH 8 only). Heating 2,3-butanedione with
cysteine pre-degraded at 160 °C, pH 5 gives more tetramethylpyrazine, trimethyloxazole and
trimethylthiazole the longer the Cys was pre-degraded.

**Apparent first-order constants from Fig. 1 [F]:**
- **Cys heated alone, 120 °C, initial pH 7, unbuffered:** 74 % left at 120 min -> k ≈ 2.5 × 10⁻³
  min⁻¹ (4.2 × 10⁻⁵ s⁻¹). Total loss, not H2S release.
- **Cys alone, 160 °C, pH 5** (Fig. 2a): 39 % left at 120 min -> k ≈ 7.8 × 10⁻³ min⁻¹
  (1.3 × 10⁻⁴ s⁻¹). Only 3× faster than at 120 °C, pH 7, across +40 K: pH 5 is slower per kelvin
  than pH 7, contrary to the text's "vigorous degradation ... near its isoelectric point".
- **Cys in AX-ARP/Cys:** ln(100/47)/30 = 0.0252; ln(100/20)/60 = 0.0268; ln(100/11)/90 = 0.0245;
  ln(100/5)/120 = 0.0250 min⁻¹ -> **clean pseudo-first order, k ≈ 0.025 min⁻¹ (4.2 × 10⁻⁴ s⁻¹), 10×
  the Cys-alone rate**. With [ARP] falling 20 -> 7 mM over the same time, this is not k₂[ARP][Cys]
  with ARP as the partner (the rate would slow ~2.5×); the partner is something produced and
  replenished (the deoxyosones, GO/MGO) or the reaction is effectively Cys-limited first order.
- **ARP loss without Cys:** 20 -> 12.0 in 30 min (k₀₋₃₀ ≈ 0.017 min⁻¹), then strongly decelerating
  (6.2 at 120 min). Not first order; the text attributes it to acidification by the released Ala
  and acids (pH not measured over time).

**Carbon / nitrogen bookkeeping at 120 min [Z]:** ARP lost 13.8 (−Cys) vs 12.8 mM (+Cys); Ala
recovered 5.0 vs 8.2 mM; 3-DP + 1-DP + GO + MGO standing 7.4 vs 1.2 mM. With Cys, ~11 mM of the
lost ARP carbon is not in any measured pool; Cys (19 mM consumed) is the obvious partner (TTCA-type
or Cys-dicarbonyl adducts, not measured).

**Butanedione mass balance in the Cys + butanedione pot [Z]:** 20 mM charged, 5.2 mM left after
60 min at 120 °C even with *undegraded* Cys (Fig. 2b, 0 min) — 74 % lost — while the volatile
products in Table 2 total ≈ 2 µM in that 0-min column and ≈ 8 µM in the 120-min column
(tetramethylpyrazine 530 µg/L = 3.9 µM is the largest). >99.9 %
of the lost butanedione is unaccounted for (Cys adducts, aldol oligomers, headspace loss of a
volatile with bp 88 °C in a 120 °C bottle). [!]

## 6. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

**Can anchor:**
1. **A time-resolved fed-ARP pot at the engine's own `ARP` feed condition.** The engine charges
   `ARP` from Zhou 2023 (20 mM, 120 °C, 60 min, pH 6/7/8 single time). This paper adds **four
   time points (30-120 min) at pH 7** for MFT, FFT, MFT-SS, furfural, 2-acetylthiazole, pyrazines,
   and — figure-only — ARP, 3-DP, 1-DP, GO, MGO, Ala, Cys. Candidate uses:
   - **shape** (not level) of MFT and FFT growth: near-linear after 30 min; 120/60 ratio = 1.70
     (MFT) and 2.24 (FFT). Shape is robust to the calibration problem in §5 if the bias is a
     constant factor;
   - **ARP decay with vs without Cys** and the **3-DP / 1-DP partition** (3-DP ≫ 1-DP: ~7:1 peak
     without Cys) as a check on the engine's two-enolisation split for ARP at pH 7;
   - **Cys consumption** in the ARP pot (pseudo-first order 4.2 × 10⁻⁴ s⁻¹) vs Cys alone (4.2 × 10⁻⁵
     s⁻¹) — a direct test of whether the engine consumes Cys at the right rate when ARP-derived
     dicarbonyls are present;
   - **Cys suppresses furfural ~2×** at every time: ordinal check on the TDP -> furfural vs
     TDP + Cys/H2S competition.
2. **pH 7 -> 8 at 60 min** (Table 3): ARP alone butanedione ×3.5; with Cys, pyrazine ×14 (11.97 ->
   169) — ordinal pH checks for the pyrazine module (the engine's `PYRAZINE_*` parameters),
   consistent in sign with `zhou2023_extraction.md`.
3. **Cys dose series** (0/6/14/20 mM, Table 3): monotone butanedione suppression and pyrazine
   promotion with Cys at both pH; a within-study dose-response shape.

**Cannot anchor:**
- **No absolute-level benchmark at the precision of the Hofmann SIDA pots.** HS-SPME with external
  calibration (curves not on disk), unbuffered pots, unstated fill volume, and the 4-8× disagreement
  with Zhou 2023 at the same condition (§5). If used as levels, tolerance ≥ 8×.
- **No temperature series, no Arrhenius**: 120 °C only (plus a 160 °C Cys-alone curve at pH 5).
- **No pH trajectory**: only the initial pH is set; unbuffered ARP degradation acidifies the pot.
- **3-DP and 1-DP are semi-quantitative** (internal standard, no authentic quinoxaline standards).
- **Figure data** are ±2-3 % of full scale; the 1-DP and MGO "with Cys" curves sit near the axis.
- **No TTCA / Cys-sugar adduct measurement** — the ~11 mM missing ARP carbon with Cys (§4) is
  exactly where a TTCA-type equilibrium would sit, but the paper does not measure it.

**Flags:**
1. [!] §5: 4-8× thiol disagreement with Zhou 2023 at the same nominal pot.
2. [!] Fig. 2b: 74 % of 20 mM 2,3-butanedione lost in 60 min at 120 °C even with fresh Cys, with
   µM products; mass balance not closed; volatility loss likely.
3. [!] Text says Cys alone "was degraded by about 30% after 2 h" (120 °C; figure: 26 %) and at 160 °C
   "degraded to about 30% after 120 min" (figure: 39 % remaining). The figure values are used here.
4. [!] Legend-symbol swap between Fig. 1a and Fig. 1c-f (see Figure 1 note).
5. [!] Table 3 title says "2,4,5-Dimethylthiazole"; the row is 2,4,5-trimethylthiazole.
6. [!] No buffer; NaOH-adjusted initial pH only.
7. AX-ARP purity not stated; prepared by the same route as Zhou 2023/2024.

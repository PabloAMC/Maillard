**Answers.**
1. **A lipid hydroxyalkenal gives the Strecker aldehyde at ~17 % of Phe in 21 h at 37 °C** in a closed vial [M], with
   no aminoketone (the nitrogen ends in a lipid-derived amine) [P]. Within Seville, HNE = ED in this paper [M].
2. **It is not a second laboratory** for anything in the evidence map (Seville again), and it cannot test the
   forced-input sugar layer (different donor class, solvent, temperature).
3. **Magnitude beside the sugar donors** [Z]: ~17 % at 37 °C in 21 h vs Hofmann MGO + Phe at 98 °C / 60 min: PA 1.1 %
   (argon), plateau 1.35 % at 240 min: **1.1-1.2 dex higher** per mol Phe, in a closed vial without steam stripping.
   It shows that a ~1 % ceiling is not universal for Strecker-type donors in closed pots; it does not adjudicate
   CF-073 (different donor chemistry: no aminoketone, the PA stable for days).
4. **[!] More PA than donor at low dose** [F] -> [Z]: at 1 µmol HNE (Fig. 5A, 37 °C) PA is ~2.3-3.6 µmol, i.e.
   ~1.7-2.8 µmol above the 0-HNE point (~0.6-0.9 µmol) — **more than one PA per HNE**. Either HNE turns over
   (catalytic: e.g. amine 10 or another product is itself a Strecker reagent, or the reaction is oxidative), or
   the low-dose points / blank are biased. At 50 µmol the yield per HNE is 0.17. The paper does not comment.
5. **Blank:** 0 µmol HNE gives ~0.6-0.9 (37 °C), ~1.5 (60 °C), 1.1-2.9 µmol (80 °C) PA from 50 µmol Phe [F] (1-6 % of
   Phe): Phe alone (or the injector) makes PA here. Table 2's values are not blank-corrected (not stated).

## 2. Conditions [M]

- **Pots:** 50 µmol aldehyde (HNE, prepared after Gardner 1992; or ED from 2,4-decadienal) + 50 µmol L-Phe (or amine)
  in 1 mL acetonitrile-water (2:1, 1:1 or 1:2): **50 mmol/L each**; 4-mL microreaction vessels "closed with a cap";
  37, 60 or 80 °C; 21 h. pH ~5, unchanged on incubation. Dose series: 0-50 µmol HNE, 50 µmol Phe.
- **Browning series:** 0.1 mmol HNE + 0.1 mmol Phe in 2 mL ACN-water 2:1, 2 h at 37 °C, then 120 µL 2 N KOH in
  methanol and 5 days at 37 °C (pH 9); ΔE from CIELAB.
- **Work-up:** + 300 µL ethanol; 10 µL diluted with 190 µL solvent + 25 µL IS (337 µg/mL 3-(Z)-nonenol in methanol);
  direct injection.
- **PA by GC-FID** (HP-5MS; injector 250 °C); calibration 5-90 nmol PA in the 225 µL injected solution, five levels,
  r > 0.99, CV < 5 %. Identification by GC-MS (EI 70 eV).
- **Replicates:** "at least three determinations", mean +- SD; ANOVA + Student-Newman-Keuls.

## 4. Mechanism evidence and its kind

Laboratory: **Instituto de la Grasa (CSIC), Seville — Hidalgo / Zamora.**
- **Fed intermediate (kind 2):** synthesised HNE (and ED) fed to Phe; PA quantified [M]. Product identification of
  pyrroles 5 / 6 (5 by NMR after isolation), 2-pentylfuran, amine 10 (tentative, MS), 2-pentyl-1H-pyrrole 11 (MS vs
  synthesised standard) [M / Q]; NaBH4 reduction of the HNE-nonylamine mixture to trap the imine as amine 7
  (tentative by MS) — a trapping experiment for the imine, with amines, not with Phe.
- **Within-study factors (kind 6):** donor dose, temperature (37 / 60 / 80 °C), solvent polarity.
- **Proposed [P]:** the decarboxylation / hydrolysis route (Scheme 2) and amine 10 as a pyrrole and browning
  precursor; no labelling, no CO2 or mass balance; whether PA forms in the pot or in the 250 °C injector is not
  separately tested here (hidalgo2004 did, with cysteamine trapping, for ED).

## 6. Disagreements found

1. **Within Seville, the same donor (4,5-epoxy-2-decenal) + Phe, 50 mmol/L each, 37 °C, 21 h: PA 174 +- 18 µmol/mmol
   here vs ~5.1 µmol/mmol in Zamora, Navarro, Gallardo & Hidalgo 2006 (`zamora2006_extraction.md`, Figs. 2 / 4):
   34x, 1.53 dex.** Conditions differ: here ACN-water 2:1, unbuffered pH ~5, GC-FID; there ACN-50 mM sodium citrate
   2:1 at pH 3, GC-MS. pH cannot explain it on Zamora 2006's own pH series (PA 4.2 at pH 5 vs 5.1 at pH 3). Candidate
   causes: buffer (citrate) vs none; ED batch / purity (both made from 2,4-decadienal by mCPBA); detector and
   calibration (FID vs MS; both IS 3-(Z)-nonenol); vessel headspace (1 mL in 4 mL here; 0.5 mL, vessel not stated
   there); blank handling. Zamora 2015 (140 °C, sand, pH 3, air) gives 12.3 % from ED, closer to this paper.
2. **[!] Internal:** > 1 PA per HNE at 1 µmol HNE (sec. 1, item 4), and a non-zero 0-HNE blank of 1-6 % of Phe. Not
   discussed by the authors.
3. hidalgo2004_extraction.md has ED + Phe PA as area ratios only (no yield): no number to compare; agrees on the
   37 °C formation.

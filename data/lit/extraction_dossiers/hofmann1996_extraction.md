## What it holds, and why it is NOT the aqueous sink measurement

Five thiols (MFT, FFT, 3-mercapto-2-butanone, 2-mercapto-3-pentanone, 3-mercapto-2-pentanone), 80 ug
each, dissolved in DIETHYL ETHER (500 uL) and stored at 6 C for up to 10 days (Tables 3-5), or refluxed
for 1 h; disulfides and mixed disulfides identified (Table 1-2) and their formation followed; MFT
oxidises fastest, BHA slows it (Table 4). The medium is an organic solvent at refrigerator temperature:
the numbers describe work-up and storage artefacts, which is the paper's purpose, not the thermal sink
in a buffered pot. The corpus's aqueous thiol-loss measurements remain Hofmann 2002 (80 C brew) and
Kumazawa 2003 (121 C grid); the 50 C phosphate loss rates in Winkel's chapter (not obtained) would be
the low-temperature anchor.

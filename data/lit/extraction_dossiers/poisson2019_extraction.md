## 1. SYSTEM DEFINITION — the "in-bean" biomimetic recombinate, verbatim

This is **not** a model solution. It is a **real coffee bean that has been hollowed out and
re-filled**, which is what makes it worth reading and what limits what transfers.

### 1.1 Raw material
> "**Green coffee beans (*Coffea arabica*, Costa Rica, La Giorgia, wet processed)** were used."

### 1.2 Water extraction of the green beans — making the "exhausted bean" (EB)
> "**10 kg of green coffee beans was mixed with 20 L of water** in a Scanima batch mixer and heated
> to **60 °C for 1 h**. The obtained extract was drained, and the coffee beans were extracted
> **another 4 times with 20 L of demineralized water at 60 °C for a total of 4 h** (total 100 L of
> extract). **The resulting extraction yield was found at 20.5 % (23.2 % on a dry matter basis).**
> In a second step, the exhausted green coffee beans were dried … by increasing the temperature of
> the double jacket to **110 °C and applying a vacuum of 150 mbar**. During the first 2 h of drying,
> the product temperature was raised from 60 to 80 °C and then the final temperature was held
> constant for another 4 h (**total 6 h of drying time**). To prevent bean breakage, the
> agitator/mixer was regulated at the lowest possible rotation speed (24 rpm agitator, 100 rpm

### 1.4 Recombination and the CAMOLA feed
> "The water exhausted green coffee beans (**EB, 50 g**) were soaked with the BRE at **50 °C for 5 h
> and then overnight at room temperature** … the treated beans were **frozen to −80 °C and then
> freeze-dried for 24 h at 0.1 mbar** … to reach a **moisture content of 10 ± 0.5 %**."

> "**To reach an equimolar 1:1 ratio of unlabeled and labeled sucrose, the level of unlabeled sucrose
> in BRE was adjusted to compensate for the residual level of sucrose in EB (499 mg/50 g of EB).**
> Consequently, the BRE omitted in all sugars was spiked with **[¹³C₁₂]sucrose (2590 mg)** and
> **unlabeled sucrose (2003 mg)** prior its incorporation into 50 g of EB."

**Arithmetic check on the 1:1 claim:** 2003 mg unlabelled BRE sucrose + 499 mg residual EB sucrose =
**2502 mg unlabelled**, MW 342.3 → 7.31 mmol. 2590 mg of [¹³C₁₂]sucrose, MW 354.2 → **7.31 mmol**.
**Exactly 1:1. The design closes.** ★ This matters, because the entire pathway arithmetic in Tables
4 and 7 rests on the 1:1 assumption.

*(⚠️ Note an internal inconsistency: the Methods say the exhausted beans contained **499 mg/50 g** of
residual sucrose, while the Results say *"**the exhausted beans contained 10 % of residual

## 7. KINETICS — **NONE, and Table 2 is the reason it is worse than it looks**

The paper's own keyword list says **"kinetics"** and its title says **"Impact of Roast Degree"**, so
it is easy to read Tables 5, 8 and 10 as kinetic data. **They are not.**

1. **No absolute concentration exists anywhere in the paper** (§0, §8). A relative isotopologue share
   has no units and cannot be differentiated into a rate.
2. **Temperature and time are confounded by design** (Table 2, §1.5): the nine "time points" span
   171 → 220 °C. A trend across them is a joint T-and-t trend and **cannot be decomposed into an
   Arrhenius form** without an absolute concentration series at fixed T, which does not exist here.
3. The measured temperature is **above the fluidised bed**, not in the bean.
4. There is a **120 s cooling tail** (220 → 60 °C) after the nominal 400 s end point.

**No rate constant, no activation energy, no half-life, no isothermal series. Nothing in this paper
can be made into a kinetic parameter.**

## 8. ⚠️ THE REPLICATION GAP — read before assigning any tolerance

**I searched the full text for `n =`, `replicate`, `duplicate`, `triplicate`, `standard deviation`,
`SD`, `error`, `± `. There is NOTHING.** No table footnote states a replicate count. No figure
carries an error bar. The Methods' analytical section ends at the correction rules and never
mentions repetition.

**Every one of the ~150 numbers in Tables 3−10 is, on the face of the paper, a single
determination.** The only stated uncertainty of any kind is the **0.5 percentage-point flooring
rule** (§1.6), which is a reporting threshold and not a precision estimate.

**Consequence: this paper cannot support a tight numerical contract.** Its value is entirely in the
**structural** results (§4.4) and the **directional** trends, both of which are robust to
single-determination noise because they span 60-fold and 12-fold ranges. **Do not attach a σ to any
Poisson 2019 cell; the paper does not license one.** *(Compare Blank 1997, which states "maximum
SD ≤ 10 %, n ≥ 2 assays × 2 injections", and Wang & Ho 2008, which gives SDs from n = 3.)*

## 14. DECLARED GAPS — verbatim, for `k3` §C

> **"This paper contains no absolute concentrations of any kind.** Every one of the ~150 numbers in

> **"No replicate count, no standard deviation and no error bar appears anywhere in this paper.**
> The Methods state no repetition, no table footnote states an *n*, and no figure carries error
> bars. Every reported value must be treated as a single determination of unknown precision. **No
> σ may be assigned to a Poisson 2019 cell.**"

> **"Every blank cell in Tables 3, 6 and 9 means `< 0.5 %`, not zero.** Methods, verbatim: 'All
> results were corrected for the ¹³C content of the natural isotope. The obtained percentage after
> correction lower than 0.5 % was set to 0 % by definition.' The HDMF result in particular — that
> only two of seven isotopologues are populated at most roast times — is a statement about a
> 0.5 percentage-point floor, not an absolute absence."

> **"Temperature and time are confounded by design.** Table 2's nine 'roasting times' (50−400 s)

> reported times do not include."

> **"The roast colour of the samples was never measured.** Verbatim: 'The color of the roasted

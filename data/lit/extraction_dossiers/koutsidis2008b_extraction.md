## 1. Methods (from the abstract)

M. longissimus lumborum stored 21 days at 4 °C; glycolytic metabolites, nucleotide degradation
products, free amino acids and other amino compounds followed.

## 2. Findings that matter (from the abstract)

- Ribose increased about SIXFOLD over 21 days.
- Total reducing sugars increased about 15 %.
- Methionine increased about sevenfold; the largest amino-acid increases fell between days 7
  and 14.

## 3. What the repo takes

Only the sixfold ribose span. Combined with the one absolute beef ribose value on file
(0.26 mg/g, Aliani 2013 as cited in Hwang 2026; see `hwang2026_extraction.md`), the box's beef
ribose range is set at 0.4 to 2.5 mM: the cited point as the upper corner, one sixth of it as
the lower. This is a secondary construction and is labelled so in the box. The first thing a
reader with the PDF should do is replace it with Table values.

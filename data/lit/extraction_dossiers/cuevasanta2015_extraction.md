## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

HS⁻ attacks a disulfide in a concerted SN2 step to give a persulfide and a thiol:
HS⁻ + RSSR → RSS⁻ + RSH. For **cystine, k = 0.6 ± 0.1 M⁻¹ s⁻¹ at pH 7.4, 25 °C**, i.e. 0.9 ± 0.2
per HS⁻. pH enters only through the HS⁻ fraction (pKa(H2S) = 7 used). The rate falls with the pKa
of the leaving thiol (Brønsted slope −0.75). There is **no temperature series** and **no measured
reverse rate or equilibrium constant**; the authors note "The reactions are likely to reach
equilibrium situations". In a heated cysteine pot, cystine only exists if cysteine is oxidised
(air headspace); at 1-2.85 mM cystine the 25 °C pseudo-first-order HS⁻ half-life is already
9-25 min **[Z]**, so cystine is kinetically able to be a significant sink — but it is an exchange
whose reverse (persulfide + cysteine thiolate → cystine + HS⁻) this paper does not measure.

## 2. SYSTEM AND CONDITIONS [M], quoted

- **Na2S solutions:** "(a) Stock solutions of Na2S·9H2O ... were prepared daily in distilled water in
  sealed vials and used immediately. Concentrations were verified by iodometric titration ... (b)
  Anhydrous Na2S ... stored in a glove box (<1 ppm O2 and <1 ppm H2O). Stock solutions (100 mM) were
  prepared in the glove box using argon-bubbled nano-pure water pretreated with Chelex resin-100".
  "Gas-tight Hamilton syringes were used throughout the study."
- **Disulfides:** "cystine (Cys-Cys) and homocystine (Hcy-Hcy) were diluted in 0.1 N NaOH"; GSSG,
  cystine dimethyl ester, HED in water; DTNB in 95 % ethanol.
- **UV kinetics:** "Reactions were initiated by the addition of low amounts (30–500 µM) of Na2S to
  disulfides in pseudo-first order excess in borate (100 mM, pH 9.5, 0.1 mM DTPA) or phosphate
  (100 mM, pH 7.4, 0.1 mM DTPA) buffer using sealed quartz cuvettes with minimal head space. UV-visible
  spectra were registered in a Varian Cary 50 spectrophotometer at 25 °C, and increases in absorbance
  at 350 nm due to the appearance of sulfane sulfur were fitted to single exponential functions".
- **Discrete H2S measurements:** "Reaction mixtures containing excess disulfides in phosphate buffer
  (100 mM, pH 7.4, 0.1 mM DTPA) were incubated in closed vials with minimal head space at 25 °C.
  Aliquots were removed using a gas-tight syringe and mixed with 4 mM zinc acetate to quench the
  reaction. The H2S concentration of the aliquots was determined using the methylene blue method".
- **Amperometric:** "Na2S (10 µM) was injected into phosphate buffer (50 mM, pH 7.4) ... selective H2S
  electrode"; HSA disulfides injected at the maximum; first-order decay at 25 °C.
- **Second-order constants** "from the slopes of plots of kobs versus disulfide concentration. The
  pH-independent rate constants were calculated by correction for HS⁻ taking into account the pH and
  the pKa of H2S" (pKa = 7, ref. 2).
- **Temperature:** 25 °C throughout (peroxynitrite runs 20 °C; HSA-SOH prep 37 °C).

## 3. ANALYTICAL METHOD [M]

Three independent readouts of the same reaction: sulfane-sulfur absorbance at 350 nm; methylene blue
on Zn-quenched aliquots; H2S electrode. "Results obtained by initial rate processing or by UV-visible
absorbance measurements were consistent with H2S consumption measurements." Errors are ± values as
printed (fit or replicate SD; n not given per entry). Peroxynitrite: "At least five kinetic runs".
Controls: "using HSA-SH to rule out the reduction of structural disulfides as well as other decay
pathways of H2S such as autoxidation". No LOD / recovery given (kinetic paper).

### Text values

- Prior literature for cystine (ref. 42): "(6.2 ± 0.7) × 10⁻² and 1.3 × 10⁻² M⁻¹ s⁻¹ ... for the
  reaction of cystine with excess H2S in 50 mM borate buffer at pH 10.0 or NaOH 0.1 M, respectively",
  "lower, by factors of 15–70, than the pH-independent rate constant determined in this work",
  explained because "at alkaline pH values the predominant species is cystine²⁻ and not the
  zwitterion" and HS⁻ may ionise to S²⁻.
- Brønsted slopes: β_c + β_lg = **−0.75** (symmetric LMW disulfides); **−0.6** (HSA mixed disulfides).
  HS⁻ intrinsic reactivity ~1 order of magnitude (20-25× vs DTNB/GSSG) below alkyl thiolates of
  comparable pKa; β_nuc 0.30 / 0.38 / 0.46 (thiocarboxyls / aryl / alkyl thiols with DTNB), 0.36
  (alkyl thiols with GSSG).
- HS⁻/H2S ratio "is 2.51 at pH 7.4"; thiolate/thiol 0.13 (cysteine), 0.03 (glutathione).
- **Activation free energy from experiment:** "the experimental one of 17.5 kcal mol⁻¹ that can be
  derived from the rate constant by applying the Eyring-Polanyi equation" — this is ΔG‡ at 25 °C
  only, **not** a ΔH‡ or Ea; it carries no temperature dependence.
- **HS⁻ + HSA-SOH (sulfenic acid):** (2.7 ± 0.8) × 10² M⁻¹ s⁻¹ at pH 7.4; pH-independent
  (4 ± 1) × 10² (competition with TNB, 0.1 M phosphate pH 7.4, 0.1 mM DTPA, 25 °C); second method
  (DTDPy amplitudes, Gepasi fit with HSA-SOH decay 5.6 × 10⁻⁴ s⁻¹ at 25 °C): (4.7 ± 0.2) × 10².
- **HSA-SSH nucleophilicity:** + peroxynitrite (1.2 ± 0.4) × 10⁴ M⁻¹ s⁻¹ at 20 °C (vs HSA-SH
  (2.7 ± 0.3) × 10³); + DTDPy (1.7 ± 0.1) × 10⁴ M⁻¹ s⁻¹ (pH 7.4, 25 °C) vs HSA-SH (7.6 ± 0.4) × 10²
  and HSA-SOH prep (6.4 ± 0.1) × 10²; pH-independent ~2 × 10⁴ (HSA-SSH) vs ~7 × 10³ (HSA-SH, pKa 8.1).
  Nitrotyrosine yield 0.041 vs 0.071 mol/mol protein.
- Plasma estimate: with ~250 µM total LMW + HSA disulfides, "a global half-life of 26 min".
- Sulfide:quinone oxidoreductase kcat/Km,sulfide 10⁷ M⁻¹ s⁻¹ vs "free cystine (0.6 M⁻¹ s⁻¹)".

## 5. HEATED-POT RELEVANCE [Z]

Pseudo-first-order HS⁻ consumption by cystine at 25 °C, pH 7.0 (HS⁻ fraction 0.51 with pK1 6.98):
k' = 0.9 × 0.51 × [cystine] = 0.46 M⁻¹ s⁻¹ × [cystine].

| [cystine] | k' (s⁻¹) | t½ at 25 °C |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 mM | 2.3 × 10⁻⁴ | 50 min |
| 1.0 mM | 4.6 × 10⁻⁴ | 25 min |
| 2.85 mM (all of 5.7 mM Cys oxidised) | 1.3 × 10⁻³ | 8.8 min |

At 90-145 °C the rate is unknown (no Ea); thiol-disulfide exchanges typically speed up with
temperature, so these half-lives are upper bounds on the forward step **if** the persulfide does not
return HS⁻. In a pot where cysteine is in excess, the persulfide meets cysteine thiolate and the
reverse exchange (CysSS⁻ + CysSH → CysSSCys + HS⁻) regenerates sulfide; the net sink is whatever
leaves the persulfide pool irreversibly (S⁰/polysulfide, trisulfide, oxidation). This paper
measures neither the reverse rate nor that onward chemistry.

## 6. WHAT THE PAPER DOES NOT STATE THAT A KINETIC MODEL NEEDS

1. **Temperature dependence** (all at 25 °C; ΔG‡ only, no ΔH‡ / Ea).
2. **Reverse rate / equilibrium constant** of HS⁻ + RSSR ⇌ RSS⁻ + RSH.
3. **Fate of the persulfide** (decay, disproportionation) at food temperatures.
4. **pH dependence beyond the HS⁻ fraction** at pH < 7 (only pH 7.4 and 9.5 conditions; pH 9.5
   data not tabulated separately).
5. **Ionic strength / buffer effects** (100 mM phosphate only; DTPA present to suppress metals).

## Use in the model

- **Candidate measured constant for an HS⁻ + cystine → persulfide step** (k = 0.9 M⁻¹ s⁻¹ per HS⁻ at
  25 °C), if the sulfur lane gains a cystine species (Zhang 2024's cystine-fed pots and any
  air-headspace cysteine pot). It would need a declared Ea band (none measured) and a reverse step.
- **Sizing the Li 2022 120-min deficit:** with ≥ 1 mM cystine from headspace-O2 oxidation of 5.7 mM
  cysteine, the forward step alone has t½ ≤ 25 min at 25 °C — fast enough to matter at 90 °C over
  120 min; whether it is a net sink depends on the unmeasured reverse step.
- In N2-flushed pots (Zheng & Ho) cystine is not formed oxidatively and this channel is negligible.

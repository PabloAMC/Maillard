# Zhang & Ho 1991b (10.1021/jf00004a029) — EXTRACTION
### *"Comparison of the Volatile Compounds Formed from the Thermal Reaction of Glucose with Cysteine and Glutathione"*
### Glucose + cysteine or glucose + glutathione, 50 + 50 mmol/L, water, pH 7.5 (start), sealed 0.3 L stainless cylinder, 180 °C, 1 h. One table of 62 SDE volatiles in mg per mol of Cys or GSH, and one summary table pooling the authors' four earlier systems (Cys, GSH, ± 2,4-decadienal). Cys + glucose 969 vs GSH + glucose 404 mg/mol; cyclic polysulfides 205 vs 7 (29×), thiazoles 126 vs 38, pyrazines 118 vs 32. **No sulfur-substituted furans at all — no MFT, no FFT — in either pot.** No kinetics. ## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

The glucose member of the Zhang–Ho 1988–1991 series (same cylinder, 180 °C, 1 h, pH 7.5). With
glucose, **cysteine's volatiles fall from 3783 (alone) to 969 mg/mol, glutathione's from 816 to
404 mg/mol** (Table II) [M]. Cys + glucose (CG) is dominated by thiophenes (401), polysulfides
(205; syn/anti trithiolane 187), thiazoles (126; 2-acetylthiazole 74) and pyrazines (118); GSH +
glucose (GG) by furans (159; 5-methylfurfural 111) and thiophenes (142), with polysulfides almost
gone (7.0). The authors: "about 3 times the amount of thiazoles and thiophenes, 4 times the amount
of pyrazines, and 30 times the amount of polysulfur-containing compounds were produced in the CG
system than in the GG" [M]; GSH "was less reactive than cysteine in the reaction with carbonyl
compounds" [Q]. **No sulfur-containing furans (MFT, FFT, ...) were detected** in either system [M];
furfural 3.3 (CG) vs 10.0 (GG) mg/mol. DMHF not detected (unstable at 180 °C) [Q].

## 4. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

**Model context.** Hexose + Cys chemistry is in the trunk/sulfur lanes; GSH only via `r_gsh_h2s`.
Nothing here was run against the model.

1. **No kinetics** (single 1 h point at 180 °C, no reactant or H2S data).
2. **Direction test (usable): with a hexose, GSH gives ~3× fewer thiazoles/thiophenes, ~4× fewer
   pyrazines and ~30× fewer polysulfides than Cys, and more sugar-only furans.** I.e. GSH is a much
   weaker H2S/NH3 donor than Cys in a sugar system, and glucose's own furans survive because less
   H2S/amine is available to consume them. Same direction as `sun2019b_extraction.md` (GSH-xylose
   Amadori compound: FFT 1.02 ng/mL, no MFT at 105 °C) and `zhou2023c_extraction.md`.
3. **No S-furans with glucose at pH 7.5, 180 °C, from either thiol** — a negative control: MFT/FFT in
   the model should come mainly from pentose routes; a hexose pot at this pH should not produce
   measurable MFT/FFT through the GSH branch.
4. **Not usable:** absolute yields (IS/FID, SDE, single run).

**Caveats.** 180 °C; start-pH only (glucose degradation acidifies); air headspace presumed; SDE
artefacts; no replicates; the summary table partly re-uses earlier papers with small inconsistencies.

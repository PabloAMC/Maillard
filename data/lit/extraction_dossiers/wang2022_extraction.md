## 1. Methods (sec. 2.2, verbatim where it matters)

Table 1: Cys-Xyl = cysteine 0.242 g + xylose 0.300 g (+ 1 % or 2 % methyl linoleate by total mass) "and 5 mL
sodium phosphate buffer (pH 5.5, 0.2 mol/L) were placed in a 15 mL pressure vial", heated on a Parallel
Synthesis Poly-block 4 "at 100, 110, 120, 130 or 140 C for 30, 60, 90, 120, 150 or 180 min", stirred at
700 rpm, cooled with tap water. Charge: cysteine 0.242 g / 121.16 / 5 mL = 400 mmol/L; xylose 0.300 g /
150.13 / 5 mL = 400 mmol/L. Vessel: 5 mL liquid, 10 mL headspace, sealed under ambient air. HS-SPME
(50 C, 20 min) GC-MS, semi-quantitative in ng/g against an internal standard; two replicates.
Cys-Amadori and cysteine by HPLC-ELSD (Supplementary Figure 1).

## 3. The temperature-time shapes (Figures 1-3 are heat maps; the numbers are not in the text layer)

Text, sec. 3.3: "at the lower reaction temperatures of 100 and 110 C, their levels increased with an
increase in the reaction time. However, at higher reaction temperatures of 120-140 C, their levels tended
to first increase and then decrease with reaction time" (for the total Maillard flavour and for MFT, FFT
and 2-thiophenethiol); at 140 C "the amounts of 2-methyl-3-furanethiol and 2-furfurylthiol began to
decrease at 60 min ... due to the poor stability of sulfur-containing compounds". Cys-Amadori rises then
is consumed with time.

## 4. What the repo takes

Four SHAPE claims, independent (a third laboratory, a stated vessel): WANG22-T-01 / -02 MFT and FFT
INCREASING 30 -> 180 min at 100 C; WANG22-T-03 / -04 MFT and FFT PEAKING within 30-180 min at 140 C. These
are exactly the low-temperature-sink question B16 could not settle (the Schieberle pot at 100 C, the Liu
2023 pot at 168 C), now from a pot with both a stated vessel and a five-temperature grid. The heat-map
values would give the folds (fit rows) if the authors' data were obtained; the ng/g levels are IS-relative.

## 1. The one thing to know before using any number from this paper

**It carries two quantification regimes and they disagree by up to thirteenfold on the same sample.**
NOR-SF hexanal is **2877.34 ± 90.34 ng/g in Table 2** (single internal standard) and **37485.16 ±
3679.37 ng/g in Table 1** (isotope dilution). 1-Octen-3-ol is 4125.62 against 1783.51; octanal 24.53
against 212.04. The paper does not comment on the discrepancy. **The isotope-dilution numbers are the
defensible ones and the two tables must never be merged into one series.**

## 3. Where the flour-to-extrudate series actually lives, and why it cannot be taken

**Figure 3** is a heat map of "key off-flavor compound concentrations in eight protein samples" whose
cells carry **no numbers**; its colour bar is labelled 10.00 / 138.95 / 1930.70 / 19348.70 / 40000.00
ng/g on a non-linear scale. **Figure-only; estimating from colour is not transcription.**

**Figure 4** does print data labels, and they are **class sums**, in ng/g:

| class | NOR-SF | NOR-SM | NOR-SPI | NOR-TSP | HOA-SF | HOA-SM | HOA-SPI | HOA-TSP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Aldehydes | 45052.30 | 17539.03 | 14560.47 | 10825.90 | 15859.48 | 6929.49 | 5656.33 | 7249.43 |
| Alcohols | 6213.72 | 6130.80 | 1234.64 | 4215.97 | 2528.42 | 1538.81 | 353.72 | 753.47 |
| Heterocycles | 353.25 | 2855.52 | 604.65 | 401.95 | 67.94 | 118.60 | 73.42 | 249.01 |

The membership of each class is **not stated**. 2-Pentylfuran is the only heterocycle among the ten
key odorants, so the heterocycle row is *probably* 2-pentylfuran alone — recording it as such would
be a reconstruction and is not done. The aldehyde members of Table 1 sum to 45928.02 for NOR-SF
against Figure 4's 45052.30, so the classes are not exactly decomposable either.

Only three per-compound numbers for a processed sample are printed anywhere, all in running text:
**heptanal in HOA-SM = 54.59 ng/g** and **octanal in NOR-SM = 53.71 ng/g**, with hexanal in NOR-SF
given as "approximately 39,000 ng/g" (an author's rounding of the Table 1 value; use Table 1).

## 4. And the process parameters are absent

No extrusion temperature, no residence time, no moisture, no screw speed; no defatting or toasting
conditions; no pH or temperature for the alkali-solubilisation and acid-precipitation step. The
stages are named only in prose. **The paper never states that the flour is unheated**, so treating
NOR-SF as an unheated control is an assumption it does not license.

## 5. Verdict

The best-quantified soybean **flour** in the corpus, and a strong candidate as the declared starting
state for a soy pot — Amendment 37's clause, if a bundle is ever built on this material. The
flour-to-extrudate series that the title promises is locked in a numberless heat map. Recorded so
that a future reader does not fetch it again expecting the series.

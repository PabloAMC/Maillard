## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

Equal 50-mL volumes of 0.1 M or 0.033 M D-ribose and L-cysteine, each in 0.2 M pyrophosphate pH 5.6,
were mixed (final, [Z]: **3:1 = 50 mM ribose + 16.7 mM Cys; 1:1 = 50 + 50 mM; 1:3 = 16.7 + 50 mM**;
ribose-only and cysteine-only 50 mM) and heated **1 h at 140 °C** in a 300-mL steel vessel, with or
without CO2 at 40 MPa. Final pH 5.5–5.7 in every arm. In the conventional arms, **MFT 0 (<0.07) /
16.62 / 7.30**, **FFT 0 / 22.09 / 0.85**, **3-thiophenethiol 0 / 25.55 / 26.99** ng/mL headspace for
3:1 / 1:1 / 1:3; total volatiles 752 / 1040 / 295 ng/mL, dominated by thienothiophenes and (at 3:1)
2,3-dihydro-6-methylthieno[2,3-c]furan. Norfuraneol (HPLC) tr / 2 / 2 µg/mL. SC-CO2 lowers most
volatiles but raises MFT, FFT, norfuraneol and furfural (attributed to a transient pH drop from
carbonic acid). **Nothing is time-resolved; there is no H2S or cysteine assay.**

**The design is two one-factor steps, not a ratio series at constant total.**

| arm | ribose (mM) | Cys (mM) | step relative to 1:1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3:1 | 50 | 16.7 | **Cys ÷ 3 at fixed ribose** |
| 1:1 | 50 | 50 | reference |
| 1:3 | 16.7 | 50 | **ribose ÷ 3 at fixed Cys** |
| R alone | 50 | 0 | |
| C alone | 0 | 50 | |

| 2-Methyl-3-thiophenethiol | < 0.07 → 25.37 | > 5 | 27.23 → 25.37 | ~0 |

| 2,3-Dihydro-6-methylthieno[2,3-c]furan | 199.78 → 56.08: 0.28× | −1.2 | 1.94 → 56.08: 29× | 3.1 |

Norfuraneol as a yield: 2 µg/mL = **17.5 µM**, i.e. **0.035 mol %** of 50 mM ribose (1:1) and **0.10 mol %**
of 16.7 mM ribose (1:3). It is **not proportional to ribose** and is **lowest at the highest ribose
(3:1)**: norfuraneol formation here is limited by cysteine (the only amine), not by sugar.

**MFT/FFT ratio** (conventional): 1:1 **0.75**; 1:3 **8.6**. Lowering ribose 3× at fixed Cys cuts FFT
26× but MFT only 2.3× — FFT (furfural route) is far more ribose-sensitive than MFT.

**What the TTCA equilibrium would do to these pots (arithmetic only).** With Cao 2017's
K_app ≈ 60 L/mol (`cao2017_extraction.md`; measured at **90 °C, pH 4.2–4.7, cysteine + xylose**, so a
transfer to ribose, 140 °C, pH 5.6 is an assumption):

| arm | TTCA at equilibrium | free Cys | free ribose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3:1 (50 + 16.7 mM) | 11.6 mM | **5.0 mM** | 38.4 mM |
| 1:1 (50 + 50 mM) | 28.3 mM | **21.7 mM** | 21.7 mM |
| 1:3 (16.7 + 50 mM) | 11.6 mM | 38.4 mM | **5.0 mM** |

Free cysteine differs 4.3× between 3:1 and 1:1; MFT differs > 230×. **Sequestration as TTCA alone
cannot produce a step that steep**; something else consumes the sulfur or the thiols when cysteine is
the minor partner (or the 3:1 conventional MFT "–" is an artefact — see Flag 3).

## 5. WHAT THE MODEL CAN TAKE FROM IT

- **(a) Levels: no.** HS-SPME headspace ng/mL with an undescribed quantitation (deferred to another
  paper), number of replicates unstated, no dispersion; most identifications ms-only for the fused
  bicyclics. Norfuraneol (HPLC, external calibration, duplicate ± SE) is the only quantity with a real
  calibration, and it sits at 2–3× its LOQ (0.8 µg/mL).
- **(b) Within-study dose shapes (conventional arm only), directional:** at pH 5.6, 140 °C, 1 h,
  - tripling Cys at 50 mM ribose turns MFT and FFT on from below detection (> 230×, > 300×);
  - tripling ribose at 50 mM Cys raises FFT 26× but MFT only 2.3×;
  - furfural falls 4.8× when Cys is tripled (furfural consumed by cysteine/H2S → FFT and other
    products), and is 217 ng/mL with no cysteine at all.
  These are the cleanest one-factor sugar/thiol dose steps in a buffered pentose + cysteine pot in
  the corpus. They test the **relative orders** of the MFT and FFT routes in the model's sulfur lane,
  and specifically the TTCA sequestration + thiol-sink structure: with K ≈ 60 L/mol, free Cys changes
  only 4.3× across the Cys step, so the model needs a strongly non-linear consumer of sulfur (or of
  MFT/FFT) at low Cys:ribose to reproduce > 230×.
- **(c) Norfuraneol limited by cysteine, not ribose** (tr at 3:1, 2 µg/mL at both 1:1 and 1:3):
  a direct qualitative test of the model's amine-catalysed pentose → norfuraneol step with cysteine as
  the amine.
- **(d) Cysteine alone at pH 5.6, 140 °C, 1 h** (50 mM): trithiolanes 41.2, tetrathianes 20.8,
  3-thiophenethiol 11.3, 3-methyl-1,2,4-trithiane 9.7, thieno[2,3-b]thiophene 7.0, 5-ethyl-2-methyl-
  thiazole 4.6 ng/mL. Qualitatively consistent with Shu 1985's pH 5.1 profile (trithiolanes dominant).
  The model's cysteine lane produces only H2S, so not scorable.
- **(e) pH: no axis.** Every conventional arm is at pH 5.5–5.7 before and after. The SC-CO2 arm's
  claimed "decline of pH during the reaction" (carbonic acid under 40 MPa) is **inferred, not measured**,
  so the SC-CO2 columns cannot be used as a low-pH arm.
- **(f) SC-CO2 columns: not usable for an aqueous model** (CO2 as reactant/medium, pressure 40 MPa,
  volatiles partly stripped into three absorption buffers whose contents are analysed separately and
  not reported in Table 2).

## 6. FLAGS

1. **"Concentrated" column header** for the conventional control throughout Tables 1–2; the text calls
   it "conventional control". Almost certainly a mistranslation; read as "conventional".
2. **Ratio confounded with dose** (Section 4): the three ratios do not share a total reactant
   concentration. The authors discuss them as ratio effects.
3. **Conventional 3:1 shows no MFT, FFT, 3-TT, MTT, trithiolanes or disulfides at all, yet 513 ng/mL of
   fused bicyclics and 207 of thiophenes.** The SC-CO2 3:1 arm has MFT 9.79 and FFT 5.06. A total
   absence of every thiol in one conventional arm (16.7 mM Cys) is surprising given 3-TT is 11.3 in
   cysteine alone; treat the > 230× ratios as resting on a single "–" cell with no replicate
   information.
4. **Table 1 "nd, not determined"** must mean "not detected" (LOD 0.32 µg/mL); "tr" is between LOD
   and LOQ.
5. **HS-SPME quantitation not described** (fibre, temperature, time, internal standard all deferred to
   Xu et al. 2008 JSFA 88:328). Headspace ng/mL depends on each compound's partition; comparisons are
   only valid same-compound across arms (same buffer, so the matrix is constant).
6. **SC-CO2 volatiles**: the CO2 released on depressurisation was bubbled through three 50-mL buffer
   traps that were "collected separately"; Table 2 reports the reaction mixtures only. Volatile
   losses in the SC-CO2 arm are therefore not comparable with the closed conventional arm.
7. **Signs lost in the text layer**: cooling bath "30 % ethanol ( 3 °C)" and storage "below 18 °C" —
   presumably −3 °C and −18 °C (not resolvable; the image shows the same blanks).
8. **Identifications**: most fused bicyclics and several thiophenes are "ms" (library only) or
   positional isomers labelled "a-" (unassigned). MFT, FFT, furfural, 2-acetylthiazole,
   methylpyrazine, the mercaptobutanone/pentanone are MS + LRI against standards.
9. **Registry (`data/keys/compounds.yml`)**: `2_methyl_3_furanthiol`, `2_furfurylthiol`, `furfural`,
   `norfuraneol`, `methylpyrazine` keyed; **2-acetylthiazole is not keyed** although the engine carries
   `ACTZ`.

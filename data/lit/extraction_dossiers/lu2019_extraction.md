## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

**GSH's fate at 110 °C (the only GSH-alone time course besides `zhou2023c`).** A 2.14 mg/mL
(6.96 mM [Z]) GSH solution heated 0–120 min at 110 °C loses **26.58 %** of its GSH; **18.98 points
appear as GSSG and 7.6 points are "thermal degradation"** [M]. Apparent first-order total loss
**k ≈ 2.6 × 10⁻³ min⁻¹** [Z], decelerating (3.2 × 10⁻³ in 0–60 min, 1.9 × 10⁻³ in 60–120 min);
oxidation-only ≈ 1.8 × 10⁻³ min⁻¹, non-oxidative ≈ 0.7 × 10⁻³ min⁻¹ [Z]. **Oxidation to GSSG, not
H2S release, is the main loss channel here**, in an unstated (presumably air-containing) vessel at
unstated pH [!]. In Gly-Gly (≈ 100 mM) + arabinose (≈ 200 mM) at pH 7.5, 110 °C, 2 h, adding GSH
(0 → 40 mg/mL ≈ 0 → 130 mM) **raises the residual ARP 0.41 → 1.88 mg/mL, cuts GO 43.7 → 10.2 µg/mL,
MGO 16.4 → 3.1 µg/mL and furfural 18.1 → 0.3 µg/mL**, and browning A420 0.58 → 0.14 [F]; GSSG at the
same mass lowers A420 only at 5 mg/mL. The purified ARP (≈ 8.9 mM) degrades slightly slower with GSH
(0.64 vs 0.82 mg/mL left at 80 min) [F]. LC-MS shows a **GSH + deoxypentosone adduct (m/z 440.14
[M+H]⁺, losing 2 H2O)** and no GSH–ARP adduct [M]. The authors' mechanism: GSH's thiol traps
deoxypentosones (as thioacetal/thioketal), blocking GO/MGO/furfural formation [Q].

## 4. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

**Model context.** GSH → H2S + fragments (`r_gsh_h2s`, Zheng & Ho 1994) is GSH's only sink; there is
no GSH oxidation, no GSH–deoxyosone trapping and no GSH-Amadori branch. Nothing here was run
against the model.

1. **GSH disappearance at 110 °C (usable with heavy caveats).** Total ≈ 2.6 × 10⁻³ min⁻¹, of which
   ~70 % is oxidation to GSSG. Against Zheng & Ho 1994 Table III at 110 °C (`zheng1994_extraction.md`):
   H2S release 4.57 × 10⁻⁵ (pH 3), 7.46 × 10⁻⁵ (pH 5), 3.89 × 10⁻⁴ (pH 7), 1.09 × 10⁻³ (pH 9)
   min⁻¹. Even the **non-oxidative part (~7 × 10⁻⁴ min⁻¹) is ~2× the pH 7 H2S constant and ~16× the
   pH 3 one** (an unadjusted GSH solution is pH ≈ 3, but the pH is not stated). Together with
   `zhou2023c_extraction.md` (120 °C, pH 7.5 start: 8.2 × 10⁻³ min⁻¹, pGlu + Cys-Gly the main
   identified products, ~50 % of the sulfur unaccounted and suspected GSSG), this is **the second
   independent line showing GSH leaves mainly by routes other than H2S**, and **the first that
   measures GSSG** — confirming the oxidation pool zhou2023c could not see. A model with
   `r_gsh_h2s` as the only sink overstates GSH persistence and misassigns its sulfur.
   Caveats: one temperature, pH/vessel/O2 unstated, ~7 mM GSH; oxidation is O2-limited and so not a
   transferable first-order constant (it depends on headspace O2 and metal traces).
2. **GSH as a deoxyosone/dicarbonyl trap (usable as direction + dose-response).** 130 mM GSH cuts GO
   4.3×, MGO 5.3×, furfural ~60× and raises surviving ARP 4.6× at 110 °C / 2 h. Same direction as
   `zhou2023c_extraction.md` (GSH lowers 3-DP 2.8×, GO 2.6× at 120 °C, slows Ala-xylose ARP loss
   0.022 → 0.014 min⁻¹) and Fig. 5 here (ARP 10–80 min loss 1.44 → 1.11 × 10⁻² min⁻¹). The
   furfural suppression matters for FFT: a GSH-rich pot starves the furfural → FFT route, consistent
   with GSH's low FFT yields in `sun2019b` and `zhang1991`. No rate constants for the trapping step.
3. **GSSG does not inhibit browning (usable, qualitative):** the free thiol, not the amine, is the
   active group; GSSG behaves like an extra amine (A420 rises with dose above 5 mg/mL).
4. **Not usable:** 3-DP/1-DP and Fig. 6c–d GO/MGO as concentrations (area ratios only); the printed
   14.22 % ARP yield; any MFT/FFT/H2S (none measured).

**Caveats, collected.** Just Accepted manuscript, not the version of record; GSH-alone experiment
without pH, vessel or headspace; "six parts" for five levels; Fig. 5 conditions unstated; ARP yield
text vs figure inconsistent; figure reads ±1–3 % of full scale.

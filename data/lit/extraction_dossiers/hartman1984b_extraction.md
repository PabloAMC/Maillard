# Hartman, Scheide & Ho 1984 (J. Food Sci.) — EXTRACTION
### *"Effect of Water Activity on the Major Volatiles Produced in a Model System Approximating Cooked Meat"*
### Thiamine + cystine + ascorbic acid + MSG in water/propylene glycol, 135 °C, 30 min, five solvent mixes mapped to a_w 0 – 0.75. 2-Methyl-3-furanthiol is ≤ ~2 ppm (TMP-equivalent) at a_w ≤ 0.39, peaks at 7–16 ppm at a_w 0.72 and falls to ~2.6 ppm in the all-water system. All sulfur volatiles peak near a_w 0.72. H2S not measured. One temperature, no time course, FID response relative to tetramethylpyrazine. Read 2026-10-28.

## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

**18.9 g MSG, 3.1 g thiamine HCl, 3.1 g ascorbic acid, 1.7 g L-cystine in 70 g water:PG at 0, 25,
50, 75, 100 % water (pH 5.5)**, sealed 0.3-L stainless cylinder, **135 °C, 30 min**, stirred. a_w is
*calculated* from the vessel pressures and comes out at **0.00, 0.39, 0.49, 0.72 and 0.73–0.75**
for the five mixes [F, mapping Z]. Total volatiles (FID peak-area sum) are flat from a_w 0 to 0.39
(31–32 % of maximum), rise to 63 % at 0.49 and **100 % at 0.72**, then **fall to 45 % in the
all-water system**. Seven peaks favoured by high a_w (Fig. 6), including **2-methyl-3-furanthiol
(RT 40.47)**, 2-methyl-4,5-dihydro-3(2H)-thiophenone, 3-methyl-4-oxo-1,2-dithiane, pentan-2-one,
butan-2-one, ethyl acetate and furfuryl alcohol, all peak at a_w 0.72. Eleven peaks favoured by low
a_w (Fig. 5) include **2-methylthiophene, 4-hydroxy-5-methyl-3(2H)-furanone (norfuraneol)**, three
PG dioxolanes and 2,5-dimethyl-3-ethylpyrazine; they decline monotonically to ~zero by a_w 0.49.
The switch is "at approximately a_w 0.4". The authors infer "higher a_w favors release of hydrogen
sulfide" from the sulfur volatiles; **H2S was not measured**.

## 2. METHODS [M] (pp. 607–608)

| item | as printed |
|---|---|
| Recipe | **18.9 g** monosodium-L-glutamate, **3.1 g** thiamin-HCl, **3.1 g** L-ascorbic acid, **1.7 g** cystine, in **70 g** water:PG [Z: thiamine 9.19 mmol, cystine 7.07 mmol (14.1 mmol cysteine-S), ascorbic acid 17.6 mmol, MSG 0.10–0.11 mol (hydrate not stated); solids 26.8 g] |
| Solvent | 0, 25, 50, 75, 100 % water (rest PG) |
| pH | "(pH 5.5)" for all preparations; when and how measured not stated; no buffer [!] |
| Vessel / heating | 0.3-L Hoke SS-DOT cylinder with 90 psi gauge, loaded at atmospheric pressure (air), sealed; **oil bath 135 °C, 30 min**, magnetic stirring; pressure logged every 2.5 min and after cooling to 25 °C; solvent-only blanks at 135 °C |
| a_w | a_w = [P_total − (P_PG + P_atm,T2 + P_gas,T2)] / (P_pure-water blank − P_atm,T2), with P_PG = %PG × (P_PG blank − P_atm,T2), P_atm,T2 = P_atm,T1 × T2/T1 − P_atm,T1, P_gas,T2 = P_gas,T1 × T2/T1; P_atm,T1 = 14.7 psia, T1 = 298 K, T2 = 408 K, P_atm,T2 = 5.4 psig |
| Isolation | 25 g aliquots diluted to 25 g PG + 125 g water; vacuum steam distillation (0.3 mmHg, flask 4 °C), four-stage cold finger (−70 °C); aqueous isolate (105 mL) NaCl-saturated, 100 mL ether, back-washed 2×, Na₂SO₄, ~5 mL (spinning band), weighed. **"reproducible to ±10 %"** |
| GC | HP 5840A FID, 10 ft × 2 mm, 10 % OV-351; 50 °C/3 min, 5 °C/min to 225 °C/20 min. **Response standardised with tetramethylpyrazine**: every ppm is a TMP-equivalent, no compound-specific factors [!] |
| Identification | prep-GC fractions; IR, GC-MS (OV-101), 400 MHz NMR |
| Sensory | three laboratory panelists, reaction mixtures diluted in 0.5 % NaCl to approximately equal intensity |

and PG non-ideality into a_w; treat the a_w axis as an ordinal label of the solvent mix.

## 4. DERIVED QUANTITIES [Z]

- **MFT molar yield, order of magnitude only:** 7–16 ppm TMP-equivalent at a_w 0.72 in ~97 g of
  mixture ≈ 0.7–1.6 mg ≈ 6–14 µmol if MFT's FID response equalled TMP's per mass, against 9.19 mmol
  thiamine: **~0.6–1.5 × 10⁻³ mol MFT per mol thiamine (0.06–0.15 %)**; 2.6 ppm in all-water ≈
  2.4 × 10⁻⁴ mol/mol. FID response per mass of a C5 thiol vs a C8 pyrazine is unknown (plausibly
  lower for the thiol, which would raise these), and steam-distillation recovery is not corrected,
  so this is ±3× at best. It is **not** a thiamine-only number (cystine and ascorbate supply sulfur
  and a carbohydrate).
- **No rate constant** can be formed: one time (30 min), no precursor depletion measured.

## 5. WHAT THE MODEL CAN TAKE FROM IT

**Context.** The sulfur lane has **no water-activity or solvent term**; thiamine feeds HMP → MFT
(`r_thi_hmp`), MeSH (`r_thi_mesh`) and H2S (B50 share, zero below pH 6.25); cysteine/cystine feed
H2S; norfuraneol + H2S → MFT exists as a furanic edge.

**Verdict: (c) qualitative/ordinal; a semi-quantitative a_w shape for MFT at one temperature.**
- **G5 (thiamine degradation with H2S at ≥ 2 temperatures): NO.** One temperature; H2S inferred, not
  measured; thiamine loss not measured.
- **G1 (thiol loss kinetics): NO.**
- **What it can anchor, if an a_w dependence is ever added to the sulfur lane:** a shape check at
  135 °C, 30 min, pH 5.5 (start): MFT ≲ 3 ppm-eq at a_w ≤ 0.39, 5–10 at 0.49, 7–16 at 0.72, 2.6 in
  all-water; the switch near a_w 0.4; norfuraneol and 2-methylthiophene the reverse. The a_w axis
  is method-biased (§3.2 flag) and PG is a reactant (dioxolanes), so this is a solvent-composition
  series as much as an a_w series. **Not a fit target** (TMP-equivalent FID, unassigned peaks at a_w
  0.72, one replicate).
- **Contradictions with the model:** none testable. One mechanistic note: norfuraneol accumulating
  at low a_w while MFT is low is consistent with the norfuraneol + H2S → MFT step being
  water/H2S-limited at low a_w, which a model without an a_w term cannot express.

**Not usable:** the a_w values as physical a_w; any ppm as an absolute concentration; the sensory
table.

**Caveats.** Mixed precursors (thiamine, cystine, ascorbate, MSG), so MFT cannot be attributed to
thiamine; pH 5.5 at the start only; air in a sealed cylinder; volatiles from 25 g aliquots by
vacuum steam distillation (H2S and MeSH lost); peak areas via TMP standard; curve assignment in
Fig. 6 ambiguous.

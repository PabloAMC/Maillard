### *"Volatile Compounds Formed from Thermal Interaction of 2,4-Decadienal with Cysteine and Glutathione"*

## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

The lipid–thiol companion to `zhang1988_extraction.md`, same vessel, temperature and time, now with
200 mg 2,4-decadienal (≈ 13 mM, 0.27 mol per mol thiol [Z]). **Glutathione becomes as productive as
cysteine: total volatiles 2627.3 (Cys) vs 2511.0 (GSH) mg/mol (Table III) [M]**, against 3840 vs 816
alone (1988). GSH + decadienal is dominated by **2-pentylpyridine (1219 mg/mol, ~half the total)**,
3,5-dimethyl-1,2,4-trithiolanes (368.5) and alkylthiophenes (2-hexylthiophene 87.8, methylpentyl-
thiophene 46.5, 3-(1-hexanoyl)thiophene 60.8); Cys + decadienal by thiadiazine (828.5),
2-pentylpyridine (501.5), hexanal (278.6), thialdine (284.2) and 2-butyl-2-octenal (253.6). The
authors' reading [Q]: decadienal forms a Schiff base directly with GSH's amino group (less free
decadienal → less hexanal: 45.4 vs 278.6), cyclising to 2-pentylpyridine; GSH releases H2S "rapidly"
and NH3 "too slowly", so N-containing S-compounds are ~2 % of GSH volatiles vs 45 % for Cys.
**No MFT or FFT** (no sugar).

### 3.3 Derived and consistency [Z]

- Table I column sums: Cys 2935.7, GSH 2617.7 mg/mol; **without the duplicated butylbenzene line:
  2878.5 and 2561.1** — which are the totals Zhang & Ho 1991b Table II quotes for "CD" (2879.6) and
  "GD" (2561.1). **Table III's totals (2627.3 / 2511.0) match neither** [!]; the difference is
  unexplained. Use Table I.
- **Thiophenes** (lipid × sulfur products): Cys 219.0, GSH 339.6 mg/mol (1991b Table II); in moles
  ≈ 1.4 vs 2.2 mmol/mol.
- **Trithiolanes:** Cys 141.0 mg/mol = 0.93 mmol/mol; GSH 368.5 = 2.42 mmol/mol; vs alone (1988):
  Cys 1.22, GSH 3.39 mmol/mol — decadienal *lowers* trithiolanes from both.
- **2-Pentylpyridine from GSH: 1219 mg/mol = 8.2 mmol/mol GSH = 3.0 % of the decadienal charged**
  (1.31 mmol decadienal per 4.88 mmol GSH).

## 4. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

**Model context.** The model routes GSH only to H2S (`r_gsh_h2s`); the lipid lane carries
2,4-decadienal/hexanal. Nothing here was run against the model.

1. **No kinetics** (one temperature, one time, no reactant decay, no H2S).
2. **Direction test (usable): a reactive aldehyde equalises GSH and Cys as volatile sources.** GSH +
   decadienal ≈ Cys + decadienal in total volatiles, and GSH gives *more* thiophenes and
   trithiolanes. Consistent with `wang2020_extraction.md` (GSH and Cys-Gly form aldehyde adducts
   and thiazolidines at 140 °C) — GSH's thiol and amine are directly available to carbonyls without
   prior degradation. A model in which GSH reacts only after releasing H2S cannot reproduce this.
3. **Lipid × sulfur product ratio (semi-quantitative):** GSH + decadienal → 2-pentylpyridine
   (~3 % of decadienal) via a Schiff base with the peptide N-terminus; hexanal 6× lower than with Cys.
   Usable only as a direction for a decadienal + amine branch.
4. **Not usable:** absolute yields (IS/FID, response factors 1, SDE, single run); the Table III totals.

**Caveats.** Decadienal at 13 mM is far above solubility (two-phase); 180 °C; start-pH only; air
headspace presumed; duplicated row; internal totals disagree; no replicates.

## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

Three pH-series systems were heated in sealed, N2-flushed ampoules at **130 °C for 1 h**: (i) washed
beef fibre alone in 0.2 M pyrophosphate (Table 3), (ii) an aqueous cysteine/thiamine/ribose solution
(**41 / 15 / 30 mM** [Z]) in 0.2 M pyrophosphate (Table 4), (iii) the fibre with the same precursor
**amount** in 3.5× more buffer (Table 5); pH **4.0, 5.0, 5.5, 6.5** in the tables. Then a water-activity
series: 4 g fibre + 400 µL precursor solution (pH 5.8 buffer) + 400 / 1200 / 3000 µL buffer gives
**a_w 0.60 / 0.80 / 0.90** (Table 6). Volatiles: dynamic headspace (Tenax, 5 min at 30 °C after
dilution to 20 mL) and GC–MS in SIR mode, **reported as peak areas**. In the aqueous system almost every
sulfur volatile **rises with pH** (MFT 37 → 193, 2-methyl-3-thiophenethiol 5 → 2293, bis(2-methyl-3-furyl)
disulfide 5.5 → 177 × 10³ area units, pH 4 → 6.5). In the fibre systems MFT and its disulfide **peak at
pH 5.5 and collapse at 6.5**. **On a_w, MFT rises 77-fold from 0.60 to 0.90 (0.50 → 3.50 → 38.5) and
bis(2-methyl-3-furyl) disulfide 4.3-fold (8.9 → 9.1 → 38.2)**, while 2-methylthiophene and
2-methyl-4,5-dihydrothiophene peak at a_w 0.80. The a_w series is confounded with reactant dilution,
buffer load, fibre mass per ampoule and an unmeasured pH, and is single-temperature, single-time,
uncalibrated. **It can test the sign of an a_w term on MFT; it cannot size one.**

### 2.3 The a_w series (p. 70) — how a_w was set

An aqueous solution of cysteine **5.0**, thiamine **5.0** and ribose **4.5 mg/mL** in 0.2 M
pyrophosphate **pH 5.8**. Printed recipe [M]:

| Meat fiber (g) | Buffer (µL) (0.2 M, pH 5.8) | Reaction mixture (µL) (in buffer) | Final a_w |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 400 | 400 | 0.60 |
| 4 | 1200 | 400 | 0.80 |
| 4 | 3000 | 400 | 0.90 |

- "The amount of reaction mixture was kept constant to get a uniform amount of reaction mixture in each
  sample." Fibre + liquids were ground together under **20–30 mL liquid nitrogen** to a fine frozen
  powder; **0.5 g** of that powder was poured (frozen) into a 10 mL ampoule, N2-flushed 2 min,
  flame-sealed. n = 4, 12 ampoules.
- **a_w was set by hydration level of the protein fibre (water added as buffer), not by salts or
  humectants**, and "measured directly" with the Aqualab meter. The paper does not say when (before or
  after heating) or at what temperature; the "Final a_w" values are round numbers, so they are most
  likely target values confirmed at room temperature before heating [!].
- Heating: all pH and a_w ampoules **130 °C for 1 h in an autoclave** (Harvard-LTE Bench top-50);
  come-up time not stated; cooled at room temperature (16 ± 4 °C), stored at "20 °C" until analysis
  (delay not stated).

density 1; one ampoule = 0.5 g of the frozen powder):

| a_w | batch mass (g) | water, % wet basis | fibre per ampoule (g) | water per ampoule (g) | Cys / THI / ribose per ampoule (µmol) | Cys / THI / ribose in the water phase (mM) | pyrophosphate per batch (mmol) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.60 | 4.8 | 21.6 | 0.417 | 0.108 | 1.72 / 0.62 / 1.25 | **15.9 / 5.7 / 11.6** | 0.16 |

### 4.2 Direction of each confound for the MFT rise (bimolecular mass-action reasoning)

| confound (0.60 → 0.90) | size | effect on MFT area at 0.90 relative to 0.60 |
|---|---|---|
| Precursor amount per ampoule | −35 % | **lowers** it (×0.65) |
| Precursor concentration in the water phase | −72 % (3.5× dilution) | **lowers** it for any step second-order in precursors (per-ampoule yield ∝ c²V ∝ 1/V: ×~0.44); a first-order step is unaffected |

| Pyrophosphate per g protein (pH) | ×4.3 | **unknown sign**: fibre pH is 6.99, buffer 5.8; the low-a_w samples carry 0.16 mmol pyrophosphate on 4 g protein, so their reaction pH is probably nearer the fibre's than 5.8; at high a_w it is nearer 5.8. | Thiamine:cysteine | constant | — |

unmeasured pH drift is the only one that could account for a large part of it.**

## 5. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

**The model's state.** `src/kinetic_core/engine.py`, `axis_refusal()`: a comparison whose arms differ in
water activity is **refused** unless a resolved lane is in `AW_TERM_LANES = {TRUNK, ACRYLAMIDE}`. The
trunk's term (B12, `trunk_conditions.AW_MULTIPLIER_TABLE`, Pereyra Gonzales 2010) scales only
`k_schiff`; the acrylamide lane's (B14) holds inside a_w 0.88–0.99. The sulfur lane (MFT, FFT, MFTD,
H2S, thiamine) carries **no a_w term**, so "does a drier pot make more or less MFT?" is unanswerable.

### 5.1 Could Modi 2008 support (parameterise) an a_w term on the sulfur lane? **No.**

1. **Uncalibrated peak areas.** No internal standard, no response factors, headspace after a 20 mL
   dilution. Only within-compound ratios across a_w exist; nothing converts to µg/L or mol yield.
2. **One temperature (130 °C), one time (1 h), three a_w points, one nominal pH.** No time course, so a
   multiplier on a rate constant cannot be separated from a change in yield/branching or in the MFT
   sinks (oxidation to MFTD, protein binding, both water-dependent).
3. **a_w is confounded with water-phase concentration (3.5×), dose per ampoule (−35 %), fibre per
   ampoule (−35 %) and pyrophosphate load (×4.3) with an unmeasured reaction pH** (§4.2). A model that
   carries concentration explicitly could in principle remove the first two, but not the pH.
4. **The a_w was set by hydrating a 93 %-protein powder and measured (probably) at room temperature**;
   the a_w at 130 °C in a sealed ampoule is not the same number.
5. **The matrix is not the model's pot**: washed beef myofibrillar protein, which the pH series shows
   inverts the aqueous pH trend of MFT. The model's matrix layer (`matrix_sites.py`: thiol binding,
   protein disulfides) would have to be engaged for any comparison, adding its own uncertainty.

### 5.2 Could it TEST an a_w term? **Yes, as a directional hold-out, for MFT and MFTD only.**

- **Claim it supports:** in a cysteine + thiamine + ribose + protein-fibre pot at 130 °C, 1 h,
  **MFT increases with a_w across 0.60 → 0.80 → 0.90** (Duncan letters a, b, c: all three pairs
  significant), by **~10–120×** depending on how the dose is normalised; **MFTD is flat 0.60 → 0.80
  and rises ~4× to 0.90**. Sign: positive, monotone, and too large to be the dilution artefact (which
  runs the other way).
- **What a future sulfur-lane a_w term would have to reproduce:** at minimum the sign and the
  monotonicity for MFT over 0.6–0.9. If a candidate term (e.g. borrowing the trunk's Pereyra Gonzales
  multiplier, which is **3.7× higher at a_w 0.69 than at 0.98** for the amine–sugar condensation) made
  MFT *fall* with a_w across 0.6–0.9, this paper is evidence against it. That is a real tension: the
  trunk term accelerates Maillard condensation at lower a_w, whereas Modi's MFT, which needs H2S from
  cysteine/thiamine hydrolysis, falls at lower a_w. **A single global a_w multiplier copied from the
  trunk would likely predict the wrong sign for MFT.** The paper's own explanation (more H2S release at
  higher a_w, after Hartman 1984b) points at the hydrolytic H2S-release steps (`k_cys_h2s`, thiamine
  degradation) as the place a water term would act.
- **Non-monotone thiophenes** (2-methylthiophene ×37 to a_w 0.80, then ×0.34 to 0.90; the
  dihydrothiophene likewise) are the classic Maillard a_w maximum (Eichner & Karel 1972; Hartman 1984b:
  max near a_w 0.72). The model does not carry these compounds; they are context, not tests.
- **Proper use:** a directional claim in the panel's directional-score format (arm A a_w 0.60, arm B
  a_w 0.90, same declared composition per gram of water phase, expected MFT_B/MFT_A > 1), evaluable
  only **after** a sulfur-lane a_w term exists, and **marked as confounded by an unmeasured pH**. It
  must never be used to fit the term it tests. Under the observable-first rule it cannot by itself
  promote any prior.

### 5.3 The pH series

- **Aqueous Table 4 is a usable shape test for the sulfur lane's pH trajectory with thiamine present:**
  every sulfur volatile rises with pH 4 → 6.5 (MFT ×5.2, MFTD ×32, 2-methyl-3-thiophenethiol ×459).
  This is the **opposite sign** to Meynier & Mottram 1995 (cysteine + ribose without thiamine, 140 °C:
  MFT collapses > 150× from pH 4.5 to 6.5; `meynier1995_extraction.md`). The obvious reconciliation is
  that thiamine's H2S and its MFT precursor (`r_thi_hmp`; `arnold1971_extraction.md`: thiamine
  degradation and H2S both switch on above pH 6) take over at higher pH. **That makes Table 4 a sharp
  test of whether the lane's thiamine route carries the right pH dependence**: a cysteine-only MFT route
  that falls with pH plus a thiamine route that rises must net to a ×5 rise between pH 4 and 6.5 at
  41/15/30 mM, 130 °C, 1 h. Ratios only; the 5.0 column may be 4.5 (§2.2).
- Tables 3 and 5 (protein fibre) give an MFT maximum at pH 5.5 and a collapse at 6.5 — not testable
  without the matrix layer, and the fibre-alone control (Table 3) makes **more MFTD (198–906 × 10³)
  than the fibre-with-precursors pot (6–27 × 10³)** [!], which is chemically implausible (adding
  cysteine/thiamine/ribose reduces MFTD 30-fold?) and suggests a table or unit error; do not use Table 3
  or the MFTD row of Table 5 quantitatively.

### 5.4 Not usable

Cross-compound comparisons (different SIR ions, no response factors); any absolute level; Table 6
versus Tables 3–5 (different dose, matrix fraction, buffer); the odour descriptors; Fig. 1.

## 6. FLAGS [!]

1. **No text layer**; all numbers from 300-dpi page images.
2. **pH 4.5 (Methods) vs 5.0 (all tables)** for the second pH level.
3. **"cystine 5.0 mg/mL"** in the aqueous recipe; everything else says cysteine. Probably a typo.
4. **Fibre stored at "20 °C"** — probably −20 °C.
5. **Table 3 (fibre alone) MFTD 198–906 vs Table 5 (fibre + precursors) 6–27**: implausible ordering.
   Also Table 3's 2-methylthiophene pH 4.0 (79.60ᵃ) and 5.0 (72.40ᵇ) are given different letters at a
   difference ~1 SD; the letters are not trustworthy as significance.
6. **Table 6 MTF at a_w 0.90: 0.70 ± 1.00ᵇ** — SD larger than the mean, yet "significantly different".
7. **MFT at a_w 0.80: 3.50 ± 0.00** — zero SD with n = 4.
8. **No post-heat pH** for any sample, although pyrophosphate was chosen to hold it; the a_w samples
   were never pH-checked and the fibre (pH 6.99) outweighs the buffer at low a_w (§4.2).
9. **a_w measurement timing/temperature not stated**; the values are round targets.
10. **Headspace after dilution to 20 mL with buffer** means the fibre-containing samples are measured
    as slurries; any comparison across different fibre loads includes a release term.
11. Text says peak areas of "all compounds" rise with pH in both systems; Table 5's MFT and MFTD do not.
12. The "five-fold" pH result in Table 4 contradicts Meynier 1995 in sign; I attribute it to thiamine,
    but the paper does not test that (no cysteine-only or thiamine-only arm).

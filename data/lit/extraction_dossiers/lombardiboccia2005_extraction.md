## 1. What the excerpt states

"Thiamine concentration varied greatly among the species studied, ranging from 0.01 mg/100 g
determined in beef (thick flank) to 0.9 mg/100 g determined in pork meat." And, for beef:
"significant differences (P<0.05) in thiamine content among cuts were evidenced (from 0.01 to
0.08 mg/100 g)."

A second review (Lee, Lee & Jo 2025, Food Sci Anim Resour 45(1) 303-327,
DOI 10.5851/kosfa.2024.e133) gives beef thiamine as 0.08-0.11 mg/100 g citing Ramalingam 2019.

## 2. What the repo takes, as SECONDARY values

Beef thiamine 0.01 to 0.11 mg/100 g across the two sources. In mM in tissue water at 75 %
moisture (mg/100 g × 10 ÷ 300.8 g/mol ÷ 0.75): **0.00044 to 0.0049 mM**. The stub range swept on
2026-09-13 was 0.001 to 0.01 mM; the measured span sits about twofold lower.

Nothing here for thiamine in cultured cells. DMEM carries about 4 mg/L thiamine hydrochloride
(≈ 12 µM); what a washed, differentiated construct retains is unmeasured in the public record as
of this read.

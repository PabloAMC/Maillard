## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

This is an industrial optimisation study, not a GSH chemistry study. The substrate is a commercial
**GSH-enriched yeast extract ("YE-GSH-10%") mixed 1 : 5 with water**, plus added ribose and cysteine;
GSH is never quantified before or after heating, H2S is not measured, and **the heating time and
vessel are not stated anywhere** [!]. The RSM "optimum" for beef-like sensory score is **159.996 °C,
7.060 % ribose, 2 % cysteine, initial pH 5.159** (predicted score 8.673; validation run 9) [M]. **MFT
was quantified in runs 9, 13, 16, 17, 18, 19, 21 at 1.31–4.81 µg/mL; FFT in runs 9, 13, 19 at
3.61–4.04 µg/mL** [M]. Most MFT/FFT appears at **100 °C** (runs 9, 13, 18, 19) and 130 °C (17, 21);
at 160 °C only run 16 (10 % ribose, 2 % Cys, pH 4.8) shows MFT (1.31) and none shows FFT, while the MFT
disulfide 3,3'-dithiobis(2-methylfuran) appears at 160 °C in run 15 (2.77 µg/mL) [M]. **Run 13
(100 °C, 10 % ribose, no added Cys, pH 6.5) gives MFT 2.28 and FFT 3.88 µg/mL from the GSH-YE alone**
— the only run in which GSH(-YE) is the sole added thiol source and MFT/FFT are both seen. Free Cys is
reported consumed at 160 °C (runs 5, 6, 7, 15, 16 per text) but Table 5 contradicts this for runs 15–16
[!].

## 4. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

**Model context.** GSH → H2S only (`r_gsh_h2s`); pentose + Cys → MFT/FFT in the sulfur lane.
Nothing here was run against the model.

1. **No kinetics** — not even a heating time. No GSH, H2S or residual-ribose numbers in the text
   (Fig. 7 ribose not digitised). Cannot anchor any constant.
2. **Matrix, not model system.** GSH-YE carries free Cys, IMP/AMP/GMP (measured, Fig. 5),
   thiamine and peptides; MFT/FFT cannot be attributed to GSH. Run 13 (YE + 10 % ribose, no added
   Cys, 100 °C, pH 6.5) is the closest to a "GSH-YE + pentose" pot: MFT/FFT ≈ 4–6 × 10⁻⁴ mol/mol GSH
   [Z] — **10–20× higher MFT than `zhou2023c`'s GSH + Ala-xylose ARP (2.8 × 10⁻⁵ at 120 °C)**, as
   expected if the YE's free Cys and ribose-rich nucleotides contribute. Not a GSH number.
3. **Usable direction (weak): MFT/FFT do not survive 160 °C.** Free MFT and FFT are found mainly at
   100–130 °C; at 160 °C FFT is absent everywhere and MFT appears once (run 16), while MFT's disulfide
   and S-heterocycles (trithiolane, trithiane, tetrathiocane, thiazoles) appear. Consistent with
   net formation/loss in closed systems (cf. `zhou2023c`: MFT falls 7× between 90 and 120 min at
   120 °C). A model predicting rising MFT with temperature at fixed (unstated) time would conflict.
4. **Not usable:** Table 5 (units and contradictions), Table 4 (illegible), the RSM optimum (sensory
   score, not chemistry), the "external standard" concentrations as absolute (HS-SPME of a complex
   matrix calibrated in an unstated medium), any Cys-vs-GSH comparison (never isolated).

**Caveats, collected.** Accepted manuscript, not the version of record; heating time, vessel and
headspace absent; basis of % additions absent; temperature range text (100–130 °C) contradicts the
design (100–160 °C); several run/temperature mismatches in the text; implausible amino-acid units and a
~300× methylpyrazine outlier; supplier co-authorship.

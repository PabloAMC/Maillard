**Answers.**

1. **For F15: the 1-AMINO-1,4-dideoxy compounds are elimination products of the 2,3-enolisation branch, not
   reduction products** (confidence ~0.85, on structure: same oxidation level as the 1-deoxyosone). They are distinct
   from Nedvidek 1992's 1,4-dideoxyosones (2-METHYL-3-alkyl quinoxalines, CH3 at C-1, assigned to Strecker-coupled
   reduction). The paper gives no redox data.
2. **For the hub split:** a third exit from the 2,3-enediol (C-4 elimination with the amine retained) exists and is
   favoured by a 4-O-glycosyl leaving group (maltose, by extension lactose-type ARPs) [P]; for the monosaccharide
   (glucose) ARP it is at most minor and not definitely identified [Q]. The engine's hexose 2,3-branch (amine released,
   1-DG) is left as is for free glucose.
3. **Nothing quantitative**: no yields, no rates, one pH, a 50 % dioxane solvent.

## 2. Conditions

- **Detection of 8c** [M]: ARP 1b (1-deoxy-1-propylamino-D-fructose oxalate, 311 mg, 1 mmol) + K2HPO4 200 mg
  (~1.15 mmol [Z]) in water 7 ml (pH adjusted to 5 with 2 M NaOH) + 1,4-dioxane 7 ml; boiled under reflux 3 h;
  concentrated, methanol-extracted, acetylated; GC (dimethylsilicone, 100-200 °C) and GC-MS (M⁺ 209 for 8d). The
  maltose ARP 1d (474 mg, 1 mmol) "in a similar manner": 8c "in comparable amounts".
- **Trap** [M]: as above + o-phenylenediamine 216 mg (2 mmol), reflux 8 h; 7b detected as 7d (GC T 35.76, M⁺ 401) from 1d;
  from 1b "considerably smaller peak", identity "not definite".
- **Standards** [M]: 7b / 7d synthesised from the maltose-OPD quinoxaline 9 via the benzylidene (10a, 50 %), the
  aldehyde (10b, 41 %), reductive amination with propylamine (7d, 25 %); 8c from 1b in 6 M HCl (ref 8); 8d by
  acetylation (86 %).
- Phosphate ~0.08 M in the 14 ml (pH 5: mostly H2PO4⁻) [Z]; O2 not controlled (open reflux); water activity: 50 %
  dioxane.

## 6. Disagreements found

1. **Atlas F15 vs this paper** (sec. 5): the 1-amino-1,4-dideoxy-2,3-hexodiulose is classed under redox in the atlas;
   here it is an elimination product. Cause: name similarity ("1,4-dideoxy") between the amine-retaining
   elimination product and the reduced 1,4-dideoxyosone (Nedvidek's quinoxalines 9 / 15).
2. **[!] The cited pH 7 result** reads as piperidino ARP → 3-deoxyosone and propylamino ARP → 1-deoxyosone
   ("respectively"). That pairing is not shown here; use the primary papers (Beck, Ledl & Severin 1988, 1989; not on
   disk), not this sentence.
3. `docs/history/mechanism_reviews_2026-10-31/thiols.md` lists this paper as "an amine-bound 1,4-dideoxy route
   (hexose)". Correct on structure; but the hexose (glucose ARP) evidence is the weak half (identity "not definite");
   the definite trap is from the maltose ARP.

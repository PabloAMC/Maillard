## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

At 185 °C / 20 min in sealed tubes, **without water or with 5:1 water:solids**, the cysteine/ribose headspace
is dominated by 3,5-dimethyl-1,2,4-trithiolane, 3-methyl-1,2,4-trithiane, 3,6-dimethyl-1,2,4,5-tetrathiane
and thieno[2,3-b]thiophene. **MFT is "tr" (< 1 % of the internal-standard area, i.e. < ~0.65 ng) in every
system where it is seen; FFT is "+" (1–10 %) dry and "tr" wet.** "In the previously reported aqueous
systems … the major volatile components were thiols such as 2-methyl-3-furanthiol, 2-furanmethanethiol …
In the corresponding dry or low-moisture systems these compounds were not detected or were found at only
trace levels." Water favours thiophenes, thienothiophenes, trithiolanes and the MFT disulfides; its absence
favours 1-(2-furanylmethyl)-1H-pyrrole and bis-furans. Cysteine alone is stable dry but degrades to
thiazoles and polysulfur heterocycles with a little water. Lecithin adds 2-alkanones, 1-octen-3-ol and three
methylthio-furans/thiophenes but few lipid–Maillard products.

## 4. DERIVED [Z]

- **MFT upper bound, dry or 5:1, 185 °C/20 min:** "tr" < 0.65 ng TIC-equivalent per tube → < 5.7 pmol per
  82.5 µmol Cys → **< 7 × 10⁻⁸ mol/mol cysteine**. Hofmann 1998 aqueous 145 °C/20 min, pH 5: 19.8 µg / 3.3
  mmol Cys = 5.2 × 10⁻⁵ mol/mol. Ratio > ~700×, **but** headspace-trap recovery of MFT (60 °C, 1 h sweep of a
  24 mL dilution) is unknown and TIC response factors differ, so treat as "≥ 2 orders of magnitude lower"
  at low confidence (~60 %), not as a number.
- **FFT dry C+R "+":** 0.65–6.5 ng → 5.7–57 pmol → 0.7–7 × 10⁻⁷ mol/mol Cys; FFT ≥ MFT dry, MFT ≈ FFT wet.
- **bis(MFT) disulfide "++" in wet C+R** (6.5–32.5 ng) ≫ MFT "tr": most of the MFT signal sits in the disulfide
  — either formed in the tube or during the 1-h 60 °C sweep (cf. `hofmann1995_extraction.md` on workup
  disulfides).

## 5. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT ANCHOR IN THE MODEL

- **Cannot:** any MFT/FFT level. The ordinal codes are TIC-area bins without response factors, recovery or
  replicates, and the tube contents are re-dissolved and swept at 60 °C before analysis.
- **Can (directional HOLD-OUT shape, low moisture):** at 185 °C, (i) furanthiols collapse to trace when bulk
  water is removed, (ii) cysteine self-degradation products (trithiolanes, tetrathianes, thienothiophenes)
  dominate, (iii) the dry arm favours furfuryl-pyrroles. This contradicts the Hofmann 180 °C/6 min silica arm
  (`hofmann1998_reconciliation.md`: ribose FFT ×8 *up* dry) in direction for FFT — but the systems differ
  (silica support, 6 vs 20 min, 1:3 vs 1.24:1 Cys:R, SIDA vs headspace trap). **Two dry arms, opposite FFT
  signs: the sulfur lane cannot be said to have a validated low-moisture behaviour.** The core has no a_w
  term (`src/kinetic_core/__init__.py`), so this is a scope boundary, not a fit target.
- **Water as two points only** (0 and 5:1 water:solids); no a_w measured; no multi-temperature series.
- **Cysteine-alone + water** (column 5): thermal Cys degradation to trithiolanes/thiazoles without sugar at
  185 °C — qualitative support for a sugar-independent Cys → acetaldehyde + H2S + mercaptoacetaldehyde
  route; no rate.
- **Lecithin:** methylthio-furan/thiophenes only with PC (methanethiol from choline + H2S proposed in the
  companion `mottram1995c`); qualitative.

## 1. Methods

Identical to the US member (Examples 1-14, pp. 18-44): heme protein (LegH unless stated) at 1 % in pH 6
phosphate buffer with precursors at 10-20 mM sugar, 10 mM cysteine, 1 mM thiamine, heated to 150 °C for
3 minutes, headspace GC-MS; Magic Mix (Table 13) with LegH; sensory on replica burgers cooked 5 minutes
at 150 °C. See `us9943096_extraction.md` section 1 for the full conditions.

What changed in the EP is the labelling. Examples whose reaction mixtures contain no thiamine are now
**"Reference Example"**: Reference Example 4 (glucose versus ribose, p. 20), Reference Examples 6, 7 and 8
(IMP/GMP, lactic acid, lysine, p. 23) and Reference Example 9 (four heme proteins, p. 24). Examples 1, 2,
3, 5 and 10-14 stay "Example"; each uses a precursor set that includes thiamine (Examples 1-2: 1 mM;
Example 3: Table 7, mixed rows with and without thiamine; Example 5: 1 mM; Example 10: 0.1 mM; Examples
11-13: Magic Mix, 0.5 mM; Example 14: 1 mM).

## 2. Findings that matter

### The maintained claims (p. 44-45)

Nine claims. Claim 1, verbatim:

> 1. A method of making a meat substitute, the meat substitute comprising:
>
> a) an isolated heme-containing protein; and
> b) at least the flavor precursor molecules
>
> i) cysteine, glucose and thiamine, or
> ii) cysteine, ribose and thiamine;
>
> wherein the meat substitute is further **characterized in that** a taste and smell of meat is given to
> the meat substitute during the cooking process; and
> wherein the method comprises a step of combining the isolated heme-containing protein and the flavor
> precursor molecules, wherein the flavor precursor molecules are added in purified form or are derived
> from ingredients in the uncooked meat substitute that are enriched with one or more of the particular
> flavor precursor molecules.

Dependent claims: 2, heme protein from plant, mammal, yeast or filamentous fungus, bacterium or archaeon;
3, a leghemoglobin, optionally from soybean, alfalfa, lupin, fava bean, pea, cow pea or lupine; 4,
recombinant, chemically synthesised or translated in vitro; 5, at least 80 % identity to SEQ ID NOs 1-26;
6, purified; 7, further a food-grade oil, seasoning, flavouring, protein, protein concentrate,
emulsifier, gelling agent or fibre; 8, free of animal products; 9, the taste and smell of meat is that of
beef. The description is amended to match ([0009]-[0010], p. 6: "The present invention is defined by the
scope of the appended claims"; [0033], pp. 10-11, closes "In the method of the present invention, at least the
flavor precursor molecules (i) cysteine, glucose and thiamine, or (ii) cysteine, ribose and thiamine are
used").

Compared with US 9,943,096 claim 1 (heme protein + any of ten sugars + any of cysteine, cystine,
thiamine, methionine), the European claim requires cysteine **and** thiamine **and** glucose or ribose
together, and is a method of making a meat substitute rather than a flavour additive.

### Example 5 (pp. 22-23, [0090]-[0092]): the thiamine omission

Conditions: pH 6, LegH 1 %, cysteine 10 mM, thiamine 1 mM, glucose or ribose 20 mM, with or without
glutamic acid 10 mM; 150 °C for 3 minutes; headspace GC-MS and a trained panel. **No table.** The
result, verbatim from [0091]: "GCMS showed 4-methyl-5-thiazoleethanol, 3,3'-dithiobis[2-methyl]-furan,
and 4-methylthiazole compounds were created by a mixture of LegH with thiamine, a sugar (either glucose
or ribose), and cysteine. The same flavor reaction mixtures without thiamine did not generate these
compounds; additionally these compounds were not generated when heme-proteins were not present in the
flavor reaction mixtures."

So the compounds reported only with thiamine are three: **4-methyl-5-thiazoleethanol,
3,3'-dithiobis[2-methyl]-furan (bis(2-methyl-3-furyl) disulfide), 4-methylthiazole.** The opening
sentence [0090] names a different list ("5-Thiazoleethanol, 4-methyl-furan, 3,3'-dithiobis[2-methyl-
thiazole, and 4-methylthiazole"), which reads as a garbled version of the same three; the internal
inconsistency is printed in both the EP and the US. 2-Methyl-3-furanthiol itself is not named as
thiamine-dependent, and no peak areas, replicate count or no-thiamine compound list are printed. Panel
([0092]): thiamine samples "more complex in taste and more beefy, meaty, and savory"; no scale, no scores.

## 3. What it means for the model and for formulation

**(a) The thiamine row** (`results/validation/cultivated_tissue_invariance_prereg.md` sections 10-11:
thiamine comparable to cysteine as an MFT source at 100 °C, negligible at 140 °C). The EP adds no data
the US lacks; it adds a legal fact. After an opposition the proprietor chose to rest the European claim
on thiamine being present together with cysteine, and demoted every thiamine-free example to reference
status. That is consistent with the applicant believing thiamine contributes to the meaty result in a
heme system, and with Example 5 being its main evidence; it is not evidence about magnitude or
temperature. Example 5 is at 150 °C only, with thiamine at 1 mM (about 250 to 2,300 times the beef
tissue-water range the box uses), and reports the MFT disulfide and two thiazoles, not MFT. It says
nothing on the 100 °C versus 140 °C contrast, and leaves the section 10 probability (about 0.3) where it
is.

**(b) Cysteine and ribose.** Neither the EP's claims nor its examples rank cysteine against thiamine as
an MFT source; cysteine is in every arm. Ribose is claimed as an alternative to glucose, not as better.
The ribose-over-glucose evidence is Reference Example 4 (Table 8: 15 extra peaks with ribose, none of
them MFT or FFT), now formally outside the invention because it lacks thiamine.

**(c) Heme.** The engine has no heme or iron catalysis (verified by grep on `src/kinetic_core/*.py`: the
19 case-insensitive hits for "heme|hemin|leghemoglobin|iron" are all inside the word "Scheme"; whole-word
searches for heme, hemin, leghemoglobin, iron, haem, myoglobin, ferrous and ferric return nothing; the
sulfur lane gates thiol dimerisation on an explicit oxidant pool and records that it cannot predict
dimerisation in a metal-containing matrix it is not told about). The EP claims heme as essential, but
its heme-omission evidence is the same as the US member's: one sensory table (Table 5), the precursor-only
column of Table 18, and unquantified "not generated" sentences, with no equal-protein heme-free control.
For the engine, a heme-containing plant-based matrix is an unmodelled regime, chiefly because iron
porphyrins would be expected to accelerate MFT oxidation to its disulfide, the very compound Example 5
reports.

**Formulation.** The European claim is a recipe in itself: heme protein plus cysteine plus thiamine plus
glucose or ribose, combined in the uncooked product and developed on cooking. For anyone formulating a
plant-based product in Europe this combination is the protected space (subject to the claim's other
terms, its term and territory; not legal advice). Cysteine + ribose or glucose + heme without thiamine,
or Maillard reaction products made outside the product and added (as in US 2025/0221436, dossier
`us20250221436a1_extraction.md`), fall outside the literal wording of claim 1 as maintained; whether they
work as well is not shown here.

## What it does not give

- No data beyond the US member; no new table, no quantification, no replicate spread.
- No MFT measured as thiamine-dependent; Example 5 has no table and no no-thiamine compound list.
- No temperature other than 150 °C in any model reaction, so nothing on the thiamine row's temperature
  contrast.
- No opponent names, grounds or reasoning of the Opposition Division (not printed in the B2).
- No heme-free equal-protein control.

## Where it comes from

Two refusals this week pointed at the same place from different sides. The HMF sink check found the
five hydroxymethylfurfural rows under-predicted 2–12× with the sink already inert, so the gap is in
the SUPPLY of deoxyosone. B32's premise check found that Hofmann 2000b's air/argon order cannot be
produced by any constant on the shared Amadori node, and that the sugar pot's only way to differ from
the fed-Amadori pot is a route that does not pass through the Amadori compound. The trunk has three
such routes — `k_glc_tdg`, `k_fru_odg`, `k_fru_int`, the amine-free entries to the deoxyosones — and
all three are Kocadagli & Gokmen 2016's constants from a **freeze-dried glucose melt at 160–200 °C,
with no pH term**, being asked about aqueous pots at pH 4–8 and 121–180 °C.

One decade moves four of the five inside the 3× band and the fifth to 3.00; two decades overshoot
everything, because the routes then drain the sugar. Across the whole panel at ×10, of 31 re-run
absolute rows seven move by more than 0.05 dex: the five above plus **both DMHF rows** (52.8× → 16.1×
and 270× → 82×), against one row that worsens badly — furfural in `resconi_2023_pbma_beef_identity`,
93× → 1217×, a commercial product scored at a proxy operating point. Within-3× over those rows 4 → 7.

**Hofmann's order does not flip on this lever** except at ×10 000, where the sugar pot's aldehyde has
collapsed to a hundredth — so the direct entries are the right place for the HMF deficit and the wrong
place for B29's finding, which stays open.

## The prediction, declared before any source is read

An aqueous measurement of glucose → 3-deoxyglucosone (or fructose → 1-deoxyglucosone) at 100–140 °C,
pH 4–7, will land the amine-free entries **between 5× and 20× above** the melt values now shipped.
Probability **55 %**. If it does, the wave that installs it is predicted to bring at least four of the
five HMF rows inside 3× and halve the two DMHF misses, and to worsen the proxy furfural row, which
should then be re-examined rather than protected.

**What blocks the wave is a dossier, not code.** Sugar dehydration in water is among the best-measured
reactions in this field and none of it is on disk. The reading is the wave; the number goes in only
with its paper. This document is the registered forecast that reading will be scored against.

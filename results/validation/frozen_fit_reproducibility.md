# Frozen-fit reproducibility

_Generated by `scripts/generators/build_frozen_fit_reproducibility.py`. Does today's engine, on today's data, still reproduce each frozen fit that a shipped constant rests on, and is the frozen point still near-optimal? One objective evaluation per fit plus one Jacobian and one Gauss-Newton step; no refits. Changes no tier, register or constant._

## Method

For every fit that froze a shipped coordinate (the S1 record's identification.fit; B41 for the fed 3-deoxy triangle the record names B39; B21 for its two analogue-tier rows), the fit's own residual function imported from its frozen wave generator, at its frozen point: one objective evaluation (S = r . r, rows then and now), the forward-difference Jacobian (one evaluation per free coordinate, absolute per-kind steps: log10 k 0.05, Ea 2 kJ/mol, pH slope 0.05, pKa 0.1, acid yield 0.01, logit 0.05), and one bounded Gauss-Newton step evaluated (backtracked at most twice). No optimiser runs.

**Classes.** REPRODUCES: |S_today - S_recorded| <= tol x max(1, S_recorded) (the record's stated tolerance, else 1 %) and the same row count. DRIFTED-BENIGN: not reproduced, but the evaluated one-step decrease is below chi2_p(0.95) x s2 (p = the fit's free coordinates) AND every shipped coordinate's quadratic marginal Delta_j = d_S' C_SS^-1 d_S <= chi2_|S|(0.95) x s2 (d the Gauss-Newton step, C = (J'J)^+), AND the quadratic model those tests use holds at the evaluated step (trust ratio = evaluated / predicted decrease >= 0.25). DRIFTED-MATERIAL: any of the three fails (a failed trust ratio means near-optimality cannot be shown, which S1 treats as no support). s2 = max(1, chi2_red at the freeze), never today's chi2_red (a drift must not widen its own tolerance).

**Limits.** Local: the one-step decrease is a LOWER bound on S_frozen - S_min (a material verdict on it is certain), while a benign verdict rests on the quadratic model at the frozen point, which a strongly curved objective can understate; the S1 profile run's refits (PR #106) are the global check where they exist. Rows then come from each report's own row table; B1 keeps none (per-response summaries only) and B60 keeps residuals without targets. Identification bookkeeping, never credit; changes no tier, register or constant.

# B65 premortem: cysteine as a thiol redox partner

*Independent review, 2026-11-25, of `kinetic_core_b65_cysteine_redox_prereg.md` as it stood (uncommitted; it is dated
2026-11-26). Read-only: no model run. Every number below is from the files cited, or is labelled as my own arithmetic or
my own outside knowledge.*

**Premise.** Two weeks on, B65 has failed. Its verdict was foregone, or it shipped an exchange step into the engine for a
reason that is not in-pot chemistry, or the 20,000-draw sampling returned "no B feasible" for reasons unrelated to Q-B.
The likeliest causes follow, most probable first. P is my probability that the cause occurs and decides or corrupts the
verdict.

## 2. The three changes that matter most

1. **Make Q-A a fair contest (causes 1, 4, 8, 12).** Give A0 `k_cys_ox` on its declared B11 band, add the
   measurement-model rival M (vial-stage oxidation and exchange), and add Zhou 2023's dimer shares and Zhang Fig. 2's
   dimer-share direction as signatures A1 can fail. Ship only the exchange (K band), never an oxidant supply. As written,
   the 0.55 "A1 feasible, A0 infeasible" outcome is close to guaranteed by the inert k_cys_ox and by a read-out that is
   not SIDA.
2. **Run the plug-in screen before freezing (causes 2, 3, 9, 10).** The bounds in cause 2 already settle B1, B1T, B2 and
   B3 on S3 and S4. Fold them in: add the missing rivals (cysteine-slowed MFT loss, abundance cross-coupling, reductone
   buffer), put the sugar route on a band as the S1/S3 null, demote the spent Grosch signatures to diagnostics, and
   re-state the outcome probabilities.
3. **Get the cysteine and cystine accounting right (causes 5, 6).** Use a solubility-limited dissolved cystine, the
   engine's exact cystine convention, and the Cys–pentose adduct equilibrium in the module so that c is free thiol. Add
   an engine transfer check on the feasible draws before any decision-rule branch fires.

## 3. What the prereg gets right and must keep

It is engine-free and frozen before any run. It treats rivals as non-exclusive and makes no choice on score. It
attempts negative controls and a power check. It names Grosch as spent. It routes a "needs an unmeasured term" outcome
to a declared band plus a decisive measurement instead of a shipped point value. It keeps the partner's development data
out of the design and out of the repository. Keep all of these through the amendments; the fixes above change the
rivals and the signatures, not that discipline.

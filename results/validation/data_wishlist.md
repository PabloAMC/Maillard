**Where the records disagree about the decisive design or its evidence:**

- `thiol_supply_structure`, the scope of the decisive design: The plan's W6 line summarises it as one cysteine + xylose time course (free cysteine, xylose, TTCA and pH, cooled, at two or three pH values); the case's sec. 4.4, which the plan says names the design, has three arms, five held pH values in Arm 1 and in-situ pH where an electrode allows. This record follows the case.
- `pentose_split_vs_removal`, how the pH is set, and whether cysteine is crossed with pH: Protocol E1 holds pH 5.5 / 6.5 / 7.5 by pH-stat (in situ), crosses phosphate 0 / 0.2 M and cysteine 0 / 5 / 25 mM, adds a 120 C corner and samples 9 times to 240 min; the brief's B1 sets the pH as a 0.2 M phosphate label with the cooled pH read in every vial, samples 4 times, and traps in situ with cysteine at pH 6.5 only (B3). For the cysteine (sulfur-lane) pots the split-vs-removal question across pH needs E1's crossing; the brief does not carry it.
- `norfuraneol_decay_ph_law`, whether the earlier fed-NF arm would decide E16: The protocol draft's fed arm B (norfuraneol 1.75 mM, 0.2 M phosphate pH 6 and 7, 120 C, 0 / 20 / 60 min, air headspace) is one temperature with O2 uncontrolled, so by the prereg's blockers 1 and 2 it would not decide E16; the brief leaves norfuraneol decay out. No record's design varies the norfuraneol concentration, which blocker 4 (self-reaction at 20 mM) would need.
- `glucose_mft_carbon_origin`, the positional evidence on disk: Tressl 1994 (C-1 lost, no equilibration, for the furfuryl skeleton) and Tressl & Rewicki 1999 Fig. 14 (C-1 / C-6 equilibrated, 50 % labelled) disagree within one laboratory. The plan's M4 row and the case on main say no positional glucose label exists on disk; the refreshed case counts Tressl 1994 as one, but not on MFT. Neither changes the decisive design (Arm 1 is Tressl's design with MFT and norfuraneol measured).
- `thiol_absolute_second_lab`, how many new laboratories lift the refusal: H0's prereg triggers H0' on the first batch that keys >= 2 NEW laboratories with ABS-class rows; the plan's W1.2 row, the engine's refusal text and the wishlist's section 6 say 'a second independent laboratory'. The pre-registered condition is the prereg's; read literally, the one-laboratory wording would trigger H0' a laboratory early.
- `methylglyoxal_self_loss`, whether a second laboratory is part of the decisive design: CF-122's decider (written before Kanzler 2021 was read) asks for three temperatures but no second laboratory; the M4 sweep sought one (Pfeifer 2013, which proved the same lineage), and Kanzler 2021 shows a 0.5 dex within-laboratory spread in the MGO-alone k2.

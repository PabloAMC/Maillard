## 7. Lanes whose rows cannot support a trend statement

This is a gap in the benchmark PORTFOLIO, not in the model, and no amount of modelling closes it. A rank correlation across two levels of a condition is a two-group comparison, not a trend; a per-lane summary built from one pot is a statement about that pot. Derived from `lane_offset_diagnostic.json`.

| lane | scored rows | distinct pots | conditions with < 3 levels | what this blocks |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| acrylamide | 11 | 7 | temp_C | a trend statement against temp_C |
| lipid | 8 | 3 | temp_C, water_activity | a trend statement against temp_C, water_activity |
| trunk | 6 | 1 | ph, temp_C, time_min, water_activity | every trend statement about this lane: its rows are ONE pot, so any per-lane summary is a statement about that pot |

**The measurement, in each case:** rows for the acrylamide lane that vary ONE condition at a time over at least three levels, holding the others fixed, for the lane concerned. One pot per level, the other conditions held.
